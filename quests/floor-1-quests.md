# Floor 1 Quests

Ready-to-run quests for Floor 1 (see `floor-1-neighbourhoods`), built with the framework in `quest-builder`: at least one for each neighbourhood, a System Quest, a penalty quest, and a four-link questline. Gold follows the *Rewards* table (20 gp per star on Floors 1–2, times the length). Every quest is floor-bound unless it says otherwise.

Each quest has a bold metadata line: **stars · length · giver · where**.

---

## Neighbourhood Quests

### Quest: Hay There
**★ · Quick · Old Farmer Gustavsen (a crawler, 70, in overalls) · The Llama Pens**

> *"New quest! A pensioner would like his reading glasses back. A llama is wearing them."*

- **Objective.** Get the reading glasses off the Bad Llama in the stables (encounter 2) without killing it. Grapple, distract with hay (DC 12 Animal Handling), or steal (DC 14 Sleight of Hand).
- **Reward.** 20 gp, and Gustavsen tells the party where the water barrels are (a hint for the Big Barn).
- **If it fails.** Gustavsen can't read the Quest Board. He wanders into the Big Barn alone and is not seen again.
- **Twist.** None. Some quests are just about glasses.

### Quest: Party Pooper
**★★ · Short · Pippa, age 7 (a crawler kid, with a paper crown) · The Birthday Party Wing**

> *"New quest! It is Pippa's birthday. It was supposed to be a good one. Please find her cake. Reward: 80 gold, and a slice."*

- **Objective.** Find Pippa's birthday cake in the Birthday Party Wing and bring it to her in the safe room, intact. It is in the ball pit, guarded by 1d4 + 1 animatronics.
- **Deadline.** 8 floor turns (the candles burn down).
- **Reward.** 80 gp, a Bronze Fan box, and a slice of cake (heals 1d4).
- **If it fails.** Pippa cries on camera. The Pet Shop owner (her aunt) closes the shop for the rest of the floor.
- **Twist.** The cake is a mimic. It doesn't want to be eaten. It is willing to negotiate.

### Quest: Kill the Vibe
**★★ · Short · DJ Spore-Lord's ex-hype man, a myconid named Wub · The Mushroom Rave**

> *"New quest! Wub wants his job back. Wub would like you to ruin the set. Wub is very passionate about this."*

- **Objective.** Sabotage the Main Stage sound system before the boss fight: cut its power in the generator room, or swap the records (DC 13 Sleight of Hand).
- **Reward.** 80 gp. In the boss fight, two of the four speaker stacks are already dead (the bass drop deals two dice less).
- **If it fails.** Wub tells DJ Spore-Lord the party was asking around. The boss fight starts with every spore-dancer on the floor already in the room.
- **Twist.** Wub is worse than the Spore-Lord. If he gets the stage, the Rave spreads into the next neighbourhood (Wub's Remix: two extra rave encounters on the Maintenance Tunnels table).

### Quest: Ticket #4471
**★★ · Short · A Sweeper Bot with a fault light (it beeps politely) · The Maintenance Tunnels**

> *"New quest! FAULT TICKET #4471. Unit SB-12 requests assistance with: UNIT SB-12. Please help. Beep."*

- **Objective.** Escort SB-12 to the Recycling Center's service hatch and keep it in one piece. Three Sweeper Bot patrols try to "recycle" it on the way.
- **Reward.** 80 gp in scrap credit (Parts Dealer), and SB-12 opens the service hatch: the party can enter the boss room from behind, with surprise.
- **If it fails.** SB-12 is recycled. Its parts go into the Mega-Sweeper 9000: the boss gains 20 hit points.
- **Twist.** SB-12 is the Mega-Sweeper's missing control chip, and it knows it.

### Quest: Kaboomba's Fireworks
**★★★ · Floor · Grizzle, a goblin bombardier who wants out · The Gobtown Warrens**

> *"New quest! A goblin would like to defect. He says the Grand Finale is going to be very grand. He says nobody should be in it."*

- **Objective.** Get Grizzle out of the Warrens alive (to a safe room). On the way, steal the spare master fuse from the fireworks factory.
- **Reward.** 180 gp, a Silver Demolitionist box, and Grizzle as a shopkeeper: the Bomb Shop sells at −25% for the rest of the floor. Without the spare fuse, the Grand Finale goes off at the end of round 7, not round 5.
- **If it fails.** Chief Kaboomba sets off the Grand Finale early. The Warrens' guarded stairwell is buried (the party has to find another way down).
- **Complication.** Another crawler party has a bounty on Grizzle.

### Quest: Lost Property
**★★ · Short · The Tour Guide's assistant (a lost-looking skeleton with a clipboard) · Catacomb Tours™**

> *"New quest! A tourist has been left behind on the 3 o'clock tour. The tourist is a skeleton. They all are. This one is lost."*

- **Objective.** Find tourist #31 (a skeleton in a bucket hat) in the catacombs and return it to the gift shop before the next tour.
- **Deadline.** 6 floor turns.
- **Reward.** 80 gp and a Catacomb Tours™ VIP Pass (the Tour Guide skips its first action in the boss fight: "Oh! A VIP!").
- **If it fails.** The tourist joins the Tour Guide's honor guard. The boss fight gets 2 extra skeletons.

### Quest: Everything Must Go
**★★★ · Floor · The Hoarder's sister, Marge (a crawler, very tired) · The Hoarder's Garage Sale**

> *"New quest! Marge would like her brother to get help. Marge would like you to be that help. Please bring a large bag."*

- **Objective.** Get into the Clearance Warehouse and convince the Hoarder to let go of one thing (a Charisma challenge, or a fight to 0 hit points that doesn't kill him), or carry out his mother's urn.
- **Reward.** 180 gp, a Silver Adventurer box, and Marge gives the party anything from the Garage Sale worth up to 100 gp.
- **If it fails.** The Hoarder's pile grows: the Warehouse's difficult terrain spreads into the corridors around it, and Marge leaves the floor alone.
- **Twist.** Marge wants the urn because there's a Gold box hidden inside it.

### Quest: On the List
**★★★★ · Floor · The Bouncer (a troll, bored) · Club Stairwell**

> *"New quest! You are not on the list. You could be on the list. The list is very exclusive."*

- **Objective.** Get on the list for Club Stairwell: bring the Bouncer three Boss Trophies from three different neighbourhood bosses. A VIP wristband from the Mushroom Rave's dance-off counts as one.
- **Reward.** 240 gp and a Gold Boss box. The Bouncer lets the party through the club to the stairwell without a fight, or fights at half hit points ("you were on the list, it's only polite").
- **If it fails.** No penalty. It just means the party has to get past the Bouncer the usual way.

## System Quests

### Quest: Rat Race
**★ · Short · The System · Anywhere on the floor**

> *"New quest! Kill 20 rats. Why? The System would like to see you kill 20 rats."*

- **Objective.** Kill 20 rats (giant rats, rat swarms count as 5). The quest log counts them.
- **Reward.** 40 gp and a Bronze Adventurer box, per crawler. The System always pays.
- **If it fails.** Nothing. The rats are pleased.

### Quest: Dare: Mop Boy
**★★★ · Quick · The System (a dare) · The Maintenance Tunnels**

> *"DARE! Kill the Mega-Sweeper 9000 without using a weapon. Penalty for failure: you will be called 'Mop Boy' in every System message until the end of the floor."*

- **Objective.** Defeat the Mega-Sweeper 9000 with unarmed strikes, spells, explosives, environment or a mop. No weapons.
- **Reward.** 60 gp and a Silver Pugilist box.
- **Penalty** (shown up front). The title *Mop Boy* in every System message until the end of the floor, and −1 Audience.

## Questline: The Clown Who Wasn't There

A four-link questline across Floor 1 that sets up a recurring villain. **Uncle Bobo**, a clown animatronic from the Birthday Party Wing, has gained something like a mind and wants out of his neighbourhood. He is charming. He is lying.

**Chain reward.** A Gold Mystery box, the achievement *Send in the Clowns* (Major), and the title *Clown Hunter* on Inspect.

### Quest: Send in the Clowns (1 of 4)
**★ · Quick · Uncle Bobo · The Birthday Party Wing**

> *"New quest! Uncle Bobo has lost his nose. Please help Uncle Bobo find his nose. Uncle Bobo will be ever so grateful."*

- **Objective.** Find Uncle Bobo's red nose in the Party Wing's prize room (a claw machine: DC 12 Dexterity, 1 gp a go).
- **Reward.** 20 gp. Uncle Bobo gives the party a balloon animal (it explodes as an *Alchemist's Fire*, once).
- **On success.** Link 2 appears: Bobo asks for help getting out.
- **If it fails.** Bobo finds it himself. Link 2 appears anyway (fail forward), but Bobo already has one ally: a goblin minion with a squeaky horn.

### Quest: Big Shoes (2 of 4)
**★★ · Short · Uncle Bobo · The Birthday Party Wing → The Maintenance Tunnels**

> *"Questline updated! Uncle Bobo would like to see the world. Or at least the next room. Please escort him."*

- **Objective.** Escort Uncle Bobo through the Maintenance Tunnels. The Sweeper Bots try to "return him to his neighbourhood" (2 fights).
- **Reward.** 80 gp and a Bronze Chatter box.
- **On success.** Bobo is free. Link 3 appears a few floor turns later, as a System message.
- **If it fails.** The bots take Bobo back. The questline ends (broken chain): on Floor 2, an animatronic clown boss is waiting, and it remembers the party.

### Quest: Something Funny (3 of 4)
**★★★ · Short · The System · Wherever Bobo went**

> *"Questline updated! The Clown Who Wasn't There (3 of 4). Several crawlers are missing. They were last seen laughing. The System finds this concerning."*

- **Objective.** Follow Bobo's trail (confetti, honks, a missing crawler's shoe) to his hideout in a disused storeroom, and free three captured crawlers. Bobo has rebuilt himself with Sweeper parts (CR 3; use the Chuckles the Cheese Mouse stat block, with Sweeper Bot armor: AC 16).
- **Reward.** 120 gp and a Silver Armory box. The rescued crawlers each owe the party a favor.
- **On success or failure.** Bobo escapes either way, through a vent toward Club Stairwell. Link 4 appears.
- **If it fails.** The captured crawlers are "made funny": they turn up as enemies (use the Bandit stat block, with clown makeup) in Club Stairwell.

### Quest: The Last Laugh (4 of 4)
**★★★★ · Floor · The System · Club Stairwell**

> *"Questline updated! Uncle Bobo has booked the main stage at Club Stairwell. He would love to see you there. He has saved you seats. The seats are bolted down."*

- **Objective.** Stop Uncle Bobo before he takes the stairs. He is in the club with the Bouncer, who thinks Bobo is on the list.
- **Reward.** 240 gp, a Gold Boss box, and the chain reward.
- **If it fails.** Uncle Bobo takes the stairs to Floor 2. He is a neighbourhood boss there, with a party of his own.
- **Twist.** If the party tells the Bouncer Bobo isn't on the list (DC 15 Persuasion, or proof from link 3), the Bouncer helps fight him.
