# Divine Quests

> *"Your god has a little favor to ask. It is not little. It is not a favor."*

This chapter is for the GM. It lists divine quests for each of the eight major gods, ready to hand to a worshipper (Gods module only; see `gods`). The player rules, the Favor rewards and the smite table are in `quests`. When and how to give a divine quest is in `quest-builder`.

Every quest here is a template: change the target, the place or the deadline to fit the floor. Each god has one quest for each star rating, and one **Atonement quest** (★★) for a worshipper with 2 or more Lapses.

## Rewards and Smites

The same for every god (from `quests`):

| Stars | Favor if done | Divine box | Smite if failed | Lasting effect | Also on failure |
|-------|---------------|------------|-----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| ★ | +2 | — | 2d10 | 1 hour | −2 Favor |
| ★★ | +3 | Usual | 4d10 | Until next long rest | −3 Favor, +1 Lapse |
| ★★★ | +5, −1 Lapse | Big deed | 8d10 | Until the end of the floor | −5 Favor, +1 Lapse |
| ★★★★ | +8, −1 Lapse | Exceptional deed | 12d10 | Until the end of the floor | −8 Favor, +1 Lapse |
| ★★★★★ | +12, all Lapses gone | Legendary | 16d10 | Until the end of the next floor | −12 Favor, +2 Lapses |

The smite is the god's own **Apostate's Smite** (damage type and lasting effect from `pantheon`). An **Atonement quest** removes 1 Lapse on success, on top of its reward.

### Mother Moldra, the Rot Queen
**Rival: Embrix · Smite: Composted (necrotic)**

> *"Mother is hungry. Mother would like you to bring her something. Mother would like it slightly off."*

| Stars | Quest | Deadline |
|-------|-------|----------|
| ★ | **Leftovers.** Bring a whole monster corpse of CR 1 or higher to a shrine, unburnt. | Before your next long rest |
| ★★ | **Spore Season.** Plant a mushroom on the corpse of every creature you kill for a whole day (24 floor turns). | 24 floor turns |
| ★★★ | **Compost Heap.** Turn a room full of the dead (a battlefield, a crypt, a boss room) into a garden: plant it, leave it, defend it for 4 floor turns. | Before the stairs |
| ★★★★ | **Snuff the Torch.** Find a shrine of Embrix on this floor and smother its eternal flame with soil and rot. Its worshippers will come. | Before the stairs |
| ★★★★★ | **The Big Rot.** Kill this floor's boss, and leave the whole boss room untouched to rot for 8 floor turns, guarding it from every crawler who wants the loot. | Before the floor collapses |
| ★★ Atonement | **Eat Your Greens.** Eat a meal made only of monster parts, raw, at a shrine, in front of at least one other crawler. Constitution save DC 15, or Poisoned for 8 hours. | Before the stairs |

## Divine Questlines

A god with a favorite worshipper may give a **divine questline**: three to five divine quests strung together across floors. Use the questline rules in `quest-builder`. Each link is a divine quest (Favor reward and smite as usual). The chain reward is a **Divine box** one rarity higher than the finale's, and a title on Inspect.

| God | Divine questline | Links |
|-----|------------------|-------|
| **Moldra** | *The Long Rot.* Seed a spore that must be carried down four floors and planted in the floor boss's room on the last. | ★★ carry it · ★★ keep it fed (rot, daily) · ★★★ defend it from Embrix's faithful · ★★★★ plant it in a boss room |
| **Embrix** | *The Torch Run.* Carry Embrix's own flame from his temple to the bottom of the next three stairwells, and never let it go out. | ★★ · ★★★ · ★★★ · ★★★★ (light the floor boss's room) |
| **Saint Payola** | *The Franchise.* Open a Saint Payola franchise in a safe room on three separate floors. | ★★ find a site · ★★★ buy out the owner · ★★★ break the competition · ★★★★ the grand opening |
| **Thrumm** | *The Unforgiven.* A creature that killed one of Thrumm's Chosen fled downstairs. Follow it. | ★★ find its trail · ★★★ kill its minions · ★★★★ corner it · ★★★★★ kill it in a storm |
| **Nyx-Ra** | *The Unquiet Grave.* Something keeps coming back from the dead, floor after floor. Find out what, and why. | ★★ · ★★★ · ★★★★ (destroy its phylactery, or whatever it really is) |
| **Brass Warden** | *The Oath-Breaker.* A crawler broke an oath to the Warden and ran. Bring them to justice alive. | ★★ the writ · ★★★ the chase · ★★★★ the arrest |
| **Wobblegob** | *The Long Con.* Set up one joke over three floors. The punchline lands on the third floor's boss. | ★★ · ★★ · ★★★★★ |
| **Hearthmother Tuul** | *Home Cooking.* Gather a recipe ingredient on each of four floors for Tuul's great feast. | ★★ · ★★ · ★★★ · ★★★★ (cook it for a whole stairwell of crawlers) |
