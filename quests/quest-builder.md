# Quest Builder

> *"The System has generated 14,000 quests for this floor. Most of them are about rats. Please enjoy the rats."*

This chapter is for the GM. It is a framework for building quests quickly, at the table or between sessions: who asks, what they want, how hard it is, what it pays, and what happens if the crawlers fail. The player rules are in `quests`. Divine quests for each god are in `divine-quests`, and ready-to-run quests for Floor 1 are in `floor-1-quests`.

---

## Building a Quest in Eight Steps

1. **Giver.** Who wants it done? Pick from the floor, or roll on *Quest Givers*.
2. **Want.** What do they want? One clear objective. Roll on *Objectives* if you are stuck.
3. **Why.** One sentence on why it matters to the giver. This is where the *Fallout* comes from.
4. **Difficulty.** Rate it ★ to ★★★★★ against the party (see *Setting the Stars*).
5. **Length and deadline.** Quick, Short, Floor or Long, and when it fails (see *Deadlines*).
6. **Reward.** Gold, box, item or favor (see *Rewards*).
7. **Fallout.** What happens in the world if it fails (see *Failure and Fallout*). Decide now; you can keep it secret.
8. **Twist** (optional). One thing the crawlers don't know. Roll on *Twists*, or don't.

Then write it on a **Quest Card** (template at the end) and read the System notification out loud.

## Objectives

| d20 | Objective | Example |
|-----|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Kill** a specific creature | "The rat that bit my brother. You'll know it: it's wearing his hat." |
| 2 | **Clear** a place | Empty the feed room of rats so the shop can restock |
| 3 | **Fetch** a thing | A part, a key, a recipe, a pet's favorite toy |
| 4 | **Collect** a number of things | 10 Poor Llama Skins, 5 Mushroom Caps |
| 5 | **Deliver** a thing | A package, a letter, a live grenade, across the floor |
| 6 | **Escort** someone | Get them to a safe room or a stairwell, alive |
| 7 | **Rescue** someone | From a cage, a trap, a boss's room, a bad decision |
| 8 | **Protect** a place or person | Hold the door for 3 floor turns |
| 9 | **Find** something or someone | Where is the missing crawler? Where did the shopkeeper go? |
| 10 | **Investigate** a mystery | Why are the Sweeper Bots building something? |
| 11 | **Steal** something | From a boss, a shop, another crawler |
| 12 | **Sabotage** something | Break the ritual, jam the machine, ruin the party |
| 13 | **Negotiate** | Talk two factions into a truce, or into a war |
| 14 | **Craft** something | Build the giver an item (see `crafting`) |
| 15 | **Tame** a creature | Or at least get it to stop biting |
| 16 | **Perform** on camera | Win a dance-off, give a speech, make the boss laugh |
| 17 | **Survive** something | A trial room, a stampede, a night in the catacombs |
| 18 | **Kill a boss a certain way** | Without weapons, with fire, before it finishes its speech |
| 19 | **Choose a side** | Help one giver against another |
| 20 | **Something stupid** | The System wants to see you do it. That's the whole quest. |

## Complications

Add one when a quest feels too straight. Roll or pick.

| d12 | Complication |
|-----|--------------|
| 1 | Another party is after the same thing |
| 2 | The target moves every few floor turns |
| 3 | The thing to fetch is alive, and doesn't want to go |
| 4 | It has to be done quietly, or the boss wakes up |
| 5 | The deadline is much shorter than it looks |
| 6 | The place is inside another neighbourhood's territory |
| 7 | The escort is useless, loud, or secretly a monster |
| 8 | A trap, a puzzle or a locked door stands in the way |
| 9 | The giver can't pay until something else is done (a hidden second link) |
| 10 | A local faction will be furious whichever way it goes |
| 11 | A System dare is added halfway through (with a penalty, shown up front) |
| 12 | The floor is collapsing around it |

## Twists

The truth the crawlers find out halfway. Use sparingly: one quest in three.

| d12 | Twist |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | The giver is the villain |
| 2 | The "monster" is the victim |
| 3 | The reward doesn't exist (the giver is broke) |
| 4 | The reward is much better than promised |
| 5 | The thing is cursed, or explosive, or both |
| 6 | The missing person doesn't want to be found |
| 7 | Two quests in the party's log are the same job from opposite sides |
| 8 | It's a test by a god (and finishing it counts as that god's Calling) |
| 9 | The target is another crawler |
| 10 | Finishing it starts a questline |
| 11 | The System set the whole thing up for ratings |
| 12 | It was a quest from a boss who is already dead, and something else is answering for it |

## Setting the Stars

Rate a quest by the hardest thing in it and how much of it there is. Use the 2024 encounter difficulties (Low, Moderate, High) for the party's level.

| Stars | Hardest fight or danger | How much of it |
|-------|-------------------------|----------------|
| ★ | Low, or none | One scene |
| ★★ | Moderate | One or two fights, or one hard problem |
| ★★★ | High, or a mini-boss | Several fights, or a fight plus a puzzle or chase |
| ★★★★ | A neighbourhood boss, or High fights back to back | A whole neighbourhood, or a deadly place |
| ★★★★★ | A floor boss, a god, or worse | Whatever it takes |

- **Rate it once.** The stars are fixed when the quest is given, even if the party levels up. A ★★★★ quest the party comes back to at higher level is still paid as ★★★★. They earned the shortcut.
- **The System can be wrong.** Once in a while, the stars lie (a Twist). Pay it at the stars it turned out to be.

## Deadlines

Measure deadlines in floor turns (1 floor turn = 1 hour; see `safe-rooms-and-timer`).

| Length | Good deadline |
|--------|---------------|
| Quick | None, or 1–3 floor turns ("before the oven timer goes off") |
| Short | 6–12 floor turns, or "before your next long rest" |
| Floor | Before the stairs, or by a set floor turn ("by turn 30") |
| Long | By the end of a named floor |

- **Floor-bound** is the default: a quest from someone on this floor fails when the party takes the stairs. Say so if a quest is *not* floor-bound.
- **No deadline** is fine for small quests. The fallout of "never got round to it" is part of the floor.
- **Show the clock.** Write the deadline on the GM Floor Tracker next to the floor timer.

## Rewards

### Gold

Base gold depends on the floor. Multiply by the stars and by the length.

| Floor | Base gold (per star) |
|-------|----------------------|
| 1–2 | 20 gp |
| 3–5 | 60 gp |
| 6–9 | 200 gp |
| 10–14 | 600 gp |
| 15–18 | 2,000 gp |

| Length | Multiplier |
|--------|------------|
| Quick | ×1 |
| Short | ×2 |
| Floor | ×3 |
| Long | ×5 |

**Example.** A ★★ Short quest on Floor 1 pays 20 × 2 × 2 = **80 gp**. A ★★★★ Floor quest on Floor 7 pays 200 × 4 × 3 = **2,400 gp**.

Gold is per crawler for System Quests and Quest Board jobs. A local pays the amount once, for the whole party, unless they are rich.

### Boxes

| Stars | Box rarity (see *Rarity by Floor* in `loot-boxes`) |
|-------|------------------------------------------------------|
| ★ | None, or the usual box for a System Quest |
| ★★ | The usual box |
| ★★★ | The big-deed box |
| ★★★★ | The exceptional-deed box |
| ★★★★★ | Legendary |

Pick the **box type** for the job, as for achievements: escorting someone is an Armory box, a stealth job is a Shadow box, a talking job is a Chatter box, a boss quest is a Boss box. A quest from a god or a shrine pays a Divine box.

### Other Rewards

Swap some gold for something more interesting. These are worth about as much as the gold they replace:

| Reward | Worth about |
|--------|-------------|
| A shop discount (−25% at one shop, for the floor) | ×1 gold |
| A free room: a private safe room, or a guarded place to long rest | ×1 gold |
| A recipe (see `crafting-recipes`) | ×1 gold |
| An ally: an NPC who fights with the party for one fight | ×2 gold |
| A key, a shortcut, or the location of a stairwell | ×2 gold |
| A pet egg (see `pets`) | ×2 gold |
| An item of the giver's (a magic item of a rarity one below the box) | ×3 gold |

**Big rewards.** A ★★★★ quest can give one Crawler Skill level in a skill the crawler used to finish it. A ★★★★★ quest can give a Stat Point or a Crawler Skill level, and a **title** that shows on Inspect. These come from the System only.

**Levels.** With milestone leveling (see `leveling`), a ★★★★ or ★★★★★ quest is a good beat for a level. With the optional XP rule, give the XP of an encounter of the quest's hardest difficulty.

**No Audience.** Never give Audience points for finishing a quest. Give them for how the crawlers finish it, as normal (see `achievements`).

## Failure and Fallout

Most quests have no direct penalty. Instead, every quest you write gets one line: **If it fails**. That is the **fallout**: what the world does when the job isn't done. Decide it when you write the quest.

### Fallout Scale

Bigger quests have bigger fallout.

| Stars | Fallout reaches | Examples |
|-------|-----------------|----------|
| ★ | The giver | They're sad. They stop talking to you. Their prices go up a little. |
| ★★ | The room or the shop | The shop closes, the room is overrun, an NPC is hurt. |
| ★★★ | The neighbourhood | Monsters spread, a faction turns hostile, a boss gets stronger. |
| ★★★★ | The floor | A safe room falls, a stairwell is lost, the floor boss gets a new trick. |
| ★★★★★ | The crawl | Something follows the party down, the System changes a rule, a god takes notice. |

### Fallout Table

When you're stuck, roll a d12 and fit it to the quest's scale.

| d12 | Fallout |
|-----|---------|
| 1 | The giver dies, or disappears |
| 2 | The giver's shop or service closes for the rest of the floor |
| 3 | Prices go up by 50% at a shop |
| 4 | The monsters spread: the next neighbourhood rolls on this one's encounter table too |
| 5 | The boss gets stronger: +1 to its attack rolls and save DCs, or a new minion wave |
| 6 | A faction becomes hostile on sight |
| 7 | Another crawler finishes the job, and gets the reward (and a grudge) |
| 8 | The thing the party didn't stop happens: a ritual, a breakout, a flood |
| 9 | A door locks, a shortcut collapses, or a safe room is lost |
| 10 | The System announces it to the floor: *"Crawler X failed a very simple task."* (no Audience change, just shame) |
| 11 | The giver comes back later as an enemy |
| 12 | Something good happens instead: failing was secretly right |

### Running Fallout

- **Show it.** Fallout should be visible. Let the crawlers walk past the closed shop, hear the screaming, read the System message.
- **Don't double-punish.** No hit point damage or lost levels from ordinary fallout. The penalty is the world getting harder.
- **Partial success.** If the crawlers did half the job, give half the reward and half the fallout.
- **Fail forward.** A failed link in a questline can lead to the next link, just a worse one (see *Building a Questline*).

### Penalty Quests

Sometimes a quest should bite: System dares, boss contracts, crawler deals the System enforces. Give it a **penalty** and show the penalty when the quest is offered. A penalty is never secret. Good penalties:

| Stars | Penalty |
|-------|---------|
| ★–★★ | An embarrassing title in System messages for the floor; −1 Audience; lose the deposit you paid |
| ★★★ | A curse until the next long rest (disadvantage on one kind of check); lose an item you staked |
| ★★★★ | 1 Exhaustion level; a debuff until the end of the floor; the boss gets your item |
| ★★★★★ | A permanent mark on Inspect; a debuff for the next floor; the System takes a Stat Point back |

## Building a Questline

A questline is three to six quests told as one story. Use it for the stories you want to last: a recurring villain, a lost friend, a neighbourhood war, a god's long game.

### Shape

| Link | Job | Stars | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|-------|
| 1. **Hook** | Small and personal | ★–★★ | Introduce the giver and the problem. Easy to say yes to. |
| 2. **Lead** | Follow it up | ★★ | The problem is bigger than it looked. |
| 3. **Turn** | Something goes wrong | ★★–★★★ | A Twist, a betrayal, a failed link that fails forward. |
| 4. **Push** | The big push | ★★★ | Usually across a neighbourhood, or down to the next floor. |
| 5. **Finale** | The showdown | ★★★★–★★★★★ | A boss, a choice, a sacrifice. |

Short questlines drop the Lead and the Push. Long ones repeat the Push.

### Linking

- **Each link is a full quest.** It has its own giver, objective, reward and fallout. Write a Quest Card for each.
- **Reveal one link at a time.** The next link appears when the last one ends. Players see the chain grow; they don't see how long it is.
- **Branch on failure.** For each link, decide what happens if it fails:
  - **Broken chain:** the questline ends here, and the finale's fallout happens (maybe much later).
  - **Fail forward:** the next link still appears, but it's a star harder, or the giver is now hurt, captured or angry.
- **Cross floors.** Links can continue on later floors. A giver on a collapsed floor can still message the party (System Mail, a ghost, a talking item), or a new giver picks up the thread.

### Questline Reward

On top of each link's reward, finishing the whole chain pays:

- a box **one rarity higher** than the finale's box (Legendary at most, or Celestial once in a campaign, Floor 9 or later, for a chain the table will remember),
- an **achievement** (Notable for three links, Major for four or five, Epic for six or a chain across three floors),
- and often a **title** or a lasting **favor** (a friend in every safe room on the floor, a shop discount for the rest of the crawl).

## Divine Quests (GM)

Divine quests are quests from a crawler's god (Gods module only; see `gods`). The player rules, rewards and smite table are in `quests`. Each god's divine quests are in `divine-quests`.

### When a God Gives One

At most **one divine quest per crawler per floor**, and only one open at a time. Good moments:

| Trigger | What the god wants |
|---------|--------------------|
| **Rank up** (Devotee, Zealot, Chosen) | A test of the new rank: a quest of 1 + the rank's number of stars (Devotee ★★, Zealot ★★★, Chosen ★★★★) |
| **Lapses at 2 or more** | A chance to atone: a ★★ quest. Finish it to remove 1 Lapse on top of the reward. |
| **The rival is busy** | Stop the rival's worshippers, ruin its shrine, steal its thunder |
| **A Calling moment** | The crawler did something the god loves. More, please. |
| **The god is bored** | Ratings are down. Do something big on camera. |

### Writing One

1. **Fit the god.** A divine quest is the god's Great Deed turned up a notch, its Calling with a target, or its rival's Taboo turned into a job. Read the god's entry in `pantheon`.
2. **Rate it** like any quest. Higher-rank worshippers get harder quests.
3. **Set the reward and smite** from the tables in `quests`. Don't change them: the player should know exactly what is at stake.
4. **Announce it** with the world freezing and the god's own voice.

### Running the Smite

When a divine quest fails, the god smites the crawler with its Apostate's Smite, at the damage for the quest's stars. Make it a scene:

- **The god arrives.** In a voice, a vision, a light from above, or (for ★★★★★) in person, briefly.
- **The smite lands at the worst moment** the GM can find in the next hour. Usually immediately.
- **The lasting effect** is the god's smite effect from `pantheon`, for the time in the smite table.
- **No smite** if the god was slain, or if the crawler finished the quest late but before the god noticed (GM's call, once).

### Optional Rule: Rank Trials

For a more demanding pantheon, a crawler can't rise to **Zealot** or **Chosen** until they finish a divine quest set for that rank. Favor still builds past the threshold, but the rank (and its Boon) waits for the trial.

## The System's Voice

Read quests out as System notifications. Short, snarky, specific.

- *"New quest! Somebody's grandmother is stuck in a vending machine. Please do not ask how. Reward: 60 gold and a hug."*
- *"Quest complete! The goblin child has her grenade back. She has already thrown it. Reward delivered."*
- *"Quest failed. The shopkeeper waited for you. She is no longer waiting. She is no longer a shopkeeper."*
- *"Questline updated: The Missing Clown (2 of ?). The clown is not missing. The clown is hiding. From you."*
- *"DIVINE QUEST FAILED. Mother Moldra is not angry. She is disappointed. Then she is angry."*
