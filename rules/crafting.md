# Crafting

> *"The System encourages crafting. Nothing says 'entertainment' like a crawler building a bomb out of a toaster and a goblin's spleen."*

Crafting in the World Dungeon builds on the normal 5e rules for tools and crafting. You need the right **tool**, enough **gold**, the right **materials** from the dungeon, and **time**, counted in floor turns. Simple things can be made anywhere. Anything powerful needs a **workbench** in a safe room.

---

## What You Need

| Need | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Tool proficiency** | The tool listed for the item (see *Tools* below). |
| **Gold** | The gold cost, spent on parts, fuel and System licensing fees. |
| **Materials** | Monster Parts, Scrap, Essence and Trophy Parts from bodies, boxes and shops (see *Materials*). |
| **Time** | Floor turns, or a short rest for field crafting. |
| **A place** | **Field** items can be made anywhere during a short rest. **Workbench** items need a safe room. |
| **Requirements** | Some items need a Skill Level in a Crawler Skill, a character level, or a **recipe**. |

### Field Crafting

During a short rest, anywhere, you can craft **one batch** of a Field item: up to your proficiency bonus (PB) of that item, paying the gold and materials for each. The short rest still costs its normal 1 floor turn, and outside a safe room the GM still makes an Ambush Check.

### Workbench Crafting

Every safe room has a **workbench** with every common tool on it. You can use it for free.

- Spend the listed floor turns at the workbench. You do not need to spend them all at once, or in the same safe room. Record your progress on your crafting sheet.
- Time at the workbench counts as time in a safe room (it adds to your safe room run).
- **Crafting and resting.** During a long rest, you can spend **2** of its 8 floor turns crafting and still get the long rest.
- **Helpers.** Up to two other crawlers proficient with the same tool can help. Divide the floor turns by the number of crafters, rounded up. Helpers' time counts as theirs too.

## Materials

Materials come from **enemy loot**, loot boxes, harvesting (Forager) and safe room shops. Each has a **tier**, matching the tier of the creature it came from.

| Material | From | Used for |
|----------|------|----------|
| **Monster Part** | Beasts, monstrosities, aberrations, oozes, plants | Potions, food, leather, glue, poisons |
| **Scrap** | Constructs, broken gear, junk piles | Guns, explosives, gadgets, armor |
| **Essence** | Undead, fiends, elementals, fey, aberrations | Magic items, enchantments, special rounds |
| **Trophy Part** | Fine pelts, horns and skulls from strong creatures | Very rare items, themed magic items |
| **Boss Trophy** | Neighborhood and floor bosses | Legendary items |

**Writing materials.** Recipes list materials as *type (tier) × amount*: **Scrap (T2) ×3** means three tier-2 Scrap.

**Flavors.** Essence and Trophy Parts often have a **flavor**: Essence (fire), Trophy Part (dragon). Write the flavor down when you loot it. A recipe that names a flavor needs that flavor. One with no flavor takes any.

**Breaking down.** You can break one material into **three** of the same type one tier lower (one Scrap (T2) becomes three Scrap (T1)). This takes no time. You cannot combine lower tiers into higher ones.

## Tools

| Tool | Crafts | Crawler Skills that help |
|------|--------|--------------------------|
| **Alchemist's supplies** | Explosives, acids, mana potions | Demolitionist (proficiency at Level 1), Alchemy |
| **Herbalism kit** | Healing potions, antitoxin, poisons | Alchemy, Forager |
| **Tinker's tools** | Firearms, rounds, gadgets, wondrous items | Tinkerer (proficiency at Level 1), Gunslinger |
| **Smith's tools** | Metal weapons, armor, shields | Weapon Specialist, Tinkerer |
| **Leatherworker's tools** | Leather armor, holsters, bandoliers, pet barding | Pet Trainer, Tinkerer |
| **Woodcarver's tools** | Bows, arrows, staffs | Dead Eye, Pitcher's Arm |
| **Calligrapher's supplies** | Spell scrolls, copied tomes | Tome Scholar |
| **Cook's utensils** | Food, treats, shakes | Cooking |

A crawler with the right Crawler Skill can use that skill's key ability for any crafting check.

## Requirements

### Explosives and Firearms

Explosives need the **Demolitionist** Crawler Skill. Firearms and gun gear need the **Tinkerer** Crawler Skill. The Skill Level needed depends on rarity:

| Rarity | Skill Level needed |
|--------|--------------------|
| Common | Proficiency with the tool only |
| Uncommon | 3 |
| Rare | 7 |
| Very rare | 11 |
| Legendary | 15 |

### Magic Items

Standard 5e magic items, and magic versions of setting items, follow the **magic item crafting** rules below. You need:

1. **A recipe** for the item. Common items do not need one. Recipes come from loot, quests, and safe room shops. A recipe is used up when you finish the item.
2. **A character level** high enough for the rarity.
3. **The base item** (a longsword for a flame tongue, a revolver for a +1 revolver).
4. The gold, materials and floor turns for its rarity, plus any themed materials the recipe lists.

| Rarity | Character level | Gold | Materials | Floor turns |
|--------|-----------------|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Common** | 1 | 50 gp | Essence (T1) ×1 | 4 |
| **Uncommon** | 3 | 200 gp | Essence (T1) ×2, Monster Part or Scrap (T1) ×2 | 8 |
| **Rare** | 6 | 2,000 gp | Essence (T2) ×3, Monster Part or Scrap (T2) ×3 | 24 |
| **Very rare** | 11 | 20,000 gp | Essence (T3) ×4, Trophy Part (T3) ×2 | 48 |
| **Legendary** | 17 | 100,000 gp | Essence (T4) ×5, Boss Trophy ×1 | 96 |

**Consumables** (potions, scrolls, ammunition, and one-use items) cost **half** the gold and floor turns.

### Things You Cannot Craft

The System keeps some things for itself: **Stat Potions, Skill Books, Training Manuals, Manuals of Limits, Safe Room Keys, Pocket Expanders, Celestial relics, troll prizes**, and anything that is an event (Sponsor's Attention, Celebrity Status).

## Crawler Skill Bonuses

| Crawler Skill | Crafting bonus |
|---------------|----------------|
| **Tinkerer 1** | Repair a broken item, or build a simple trap or tool, during a short rest. |
| **Tinkerer 5** | Break a common magic item down into Essence (T1) ×2. |
| **Tinkerer 10** | Uncommon items take half the gold and floor turns. |
| **Tinkerer 15** | Rare items take half the gold and floor turns too. |
| **Demolitionist 5** | Bomb Maker: field-craft Pipe Bombs with no gold or materials, up to PB per long rest. |
| **Alchemy 1–15** | Brewing features as listed in the Crawler Skill. Potions take half the floor turns. |
| **Cooking** | Food and treats are always Field items for you. |
| **Bomb Surgeon 10** | Frankenbomb: combine two explosives into one during a short rest. |

These bonuses do not stack to less than 1 floor turn, or less than half the gold.

## Crafting Checks (optional)

By default, crafting always works if you meet the requirements. For more risk, the GM can call for a tool check when the item is finished.

| Rarity | DC |
|--------|----|
| Common | 10 |
| Uncommon | 13 |
| Rare | 16 |
| Very rare | 19 |
| Legendary | 22 |

On a failure, the floor turns are spent but you can try again for half the turns. On a failure by 5 or more, the materials are also lost. An explosive that fails its check is a dud and never goes off.

---

## Selling What You Make

Safe room shops buy crafted items at **half their value**, like any other item (see `safe-room-shops`). Crafting to sell is rarely worth the time. Crafting what you need, when the shop does not sell it, usually is.
