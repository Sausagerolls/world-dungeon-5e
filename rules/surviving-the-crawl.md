# The Show Must Go On

> *"Smile, Crawler. Billions of viewers across the galaxy are watching you right now. Try to die somewhere with good lighting."*

The World Dungeon is a game show, and you are the contestant. This chapter is the player's view of how the show works: the **audience**, **achievements**, and the **loot boxes** they pay out. The GM has the full rules and tables in the *Loot and Achievements* handbook.

---

## The Audience

Every crawler has an **Audience Meter** from 0 to 10. It tracks how entertaining you have been lately. Mark it on page 1 of your Crawler Character Sheet.

### Gaining Audience Points

The GM awards points when you do something worth watching.

| You… | Points |
|------|--------|
| Do something entertaining: a good joke, a cool kill, a daring move | +1 |
| Do something jaw-dropping: a wild stunt, a perfect plan, a huge explosion | +2 |
| Make the clip everyone will share for years | +3 |

### Losing Audience Points

The audience gets bored, and it hates a coward.

| You… | Points |
|------|--------|
| Play a whole session where nothing interesting happens | −1 |
| Hide in a safe room for 24 floor turns or more in a row | −1 |
| Take the stairs down early (see `safe-rooms-and-timer`) | −1 to −3 |

### The Payoff

When your meter reaches **10**, the fans send you a **Fan Box** and the meter resets to 0 (extra points carry over). Fan Boxes can hold anything: a stat potion, a magic weapon covered in fan art, or a single damp sock. Fans are generous. They are not always helpful.

> **Playing to the crowd.** You do not have to be a hero to be popular. Villains, clowns and lovable idiots all get ratings. The only thing the audience will not forgive is being boring.

## Achievements

The System watches everything you do. When you do something notable, brave, stupid or embarrassing, it gives you an **achievement**.

- **You find out when it happens.** A System message pops up with the achievement's name and a short, usually rude, comment.
- **You don't get a list.** Achievements are hidden. Part of the game is discovering them, often by accident.
- **Each one counts once.** Some come in a series (kill 1, 25, 100 things with your fists), so you can earn the next step.
- **Everyone who helped gets it.** In a group effort, each crawler who took part earns the achievement.
- **Be first.** The first crawler in the whole dungeon to earn an achievement gets a better reward, and the System tells everyone.

Most achievements come with a **loot box**. Some also give a Stat Point, Audience points, a title, or even a Crawler Skill.

Some achievements are *not* compliments. Blowing up your own party, running from three fights in a row, or fighting naked will all get noticed.

## Loot Boxes

Loot boxes are how the System pays you. You earn them from achievements, boss kills, a full Audience Meter, and some of your worse decisions.

### What a Box Is

Every box has a **type** and a **rarity**.

- The **type** says what kind of loot is inside: an *Adventurer Box* holds potions and gear, a *Demolitionist Box* holds explosives, a *Pet Box* holds things for your pet, and so on. There are 19 types.
- The **rarity** says how good it is:

| Rarity | What to expect |
|--------|----------------|
| **Bronze** | Useful basics. Most early boxes are Bronze. |
| **Silver** | Good gear, and some minor magic. |
| **Gold** | Real magic items. A great day. |
| **Legendary** | Items that change how you play. Rare. |
| **Celestial** | Almost never seen. Game-breaking power. |

### Using Boxes

1. **It goes on your list.** A box goes to the box list on your Interface. It does not take an inventory slot. Mark it on the Loot Box Tracker page of your character sheet with a **/**.
2. **Open it in a safe room.** You can only open boxes in a safe room. Opening one takes an action, and the System plays a fanfare. Cross the mark into an **X**.
3. **Keep or trade the loot.** The box is bound to you and cannot be traded, stolen or given away. What comes out of it can.
4. **Don't die with boxes.** Unopened boxes are lost when you die. Do not hoard them for too long.

### Boxes You Might Not Want

- **Spicy Box.** You did something saucy on camera. The audience enjoyed it.
- **Murder Box.** You killed a non-combatant NPC or another crawler. The System gives this box automatically, and it also gives you the **Player Killer** mark: a red skull next to your name that other crawlers can see. Some shops and NPCs will refuse to deal with you. The mark fades after two floors of good behavior.

## Patches

The System is an AI, and it fixes its own game while you play it. When crawlers find an exploit, a bug or a badly tuned fight, it freezes the world for a **hotfix** or posts **Patch Notes** at the stairs. It never undoes what already happened, never punishes the crawler who found the problem, and pays a **Bug Bounty** for it. See `patches`.

## Loot on Bodies

You can also search the things you kill. Searching one body takes an action, or 1 minute for everything after a fight. Bodies give coins, gear, monster parts, scrap and the occasional surprise. Monster parts, scrap and essence sell in shops, and Crawler Skills like Tinkerer, Alchemy and Cooking use them as materials.
