# Prestige Levels

> *"Twenty levels in one class? The System is impressed. Would you like to keep going? Of course you would."*

No class goes above 20th level, but you do not have to multiclass to keep growing. Once a class reaches 20th level, every further level you take in it is a **prestige level**. Prestige levels reward sticking with one class: big signature features you can't get any other way.

**Multiclassing is still an option.** At every level, you choose: a prestige level in a class at 20th level, or a level in another class.

---

## Prestige Rules

### What a Prestige Level Gives

| Prestige level | Benefit |
|----------------|---------|
| **Every prestige level** | The class's hit die + Con modifier, and the class's **prestige scaling** (below). |
| **1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th** | The class's next **prestige feature** (I, II, III, IV, V). |
| **2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th, 10th** | An **Epic Boon** feat (if your edition has them), or **2 Stat Points**. |
| **11th and up** | Every level: an Epic Boon or 2 Stat Points, plus scaling. |

- **Counting.** Your 21st level in a class is its 1st prestige level, your 22nd its 2nd, and so on. Write it on the Levels page as, for example, *Fighter P3*.
- **More than one.** You can have prestige levels in several classes, if you get each one to 20th level.
- **Spellcasting.** Prestige levels still count as class levels for caster level, so they keep adding class mana (+6 per caster level above 20; see `mana`). Warlocks gain +7 Pact Mana per prestige level.
- **Proficiency bonus** keeps following character level (see `leveling`).
- **Overlaps.** If your class or subclass already gives you what a prestige feature does, take an Epic Boon or 2 Stat Points instead of that feature.

### Prestige or Multiclass?

| Path | You get |
|------|---------|
| **Prestige** | Huge, unique features, Epic Boons, and scaling in your best class. Depth. |
| **Multiclass** | Many low-level features from new classes, new proficiencies, new spell lists. Breadth. |

Both are good. Prestige is better for crawlers who love what their class does and want more of it.

## Class Prestige Features

"DC" means **8 + your proficiency bonus + the class's key ability modifier**.

### Barbarian
**Prestige · Hit die d12**

*Scaling:* Rage damage **+1 per 2 prestige levels**. One extra Rage per long rest every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Endless Rage.** Your Rage only ends early if you choose. When you start raging, gain temporary hit points equal to your Barbarian level.
- **II — Titan's Grip.** You can wield a two-handed weapon in one hand. Your heavy weapons' damage dice increase by one size.
- **III — Unstoppable.** While raging, you cannot be charmed, frightened or stunned.
- **IV — Earthshaker.** Once per turn, when you hit while raging, creatures of your choice within 10 feet of the target must succeed on a Strength save or fall prone.
- **V — Avatar of Wrath.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute: you become Huge, your reach increases by 5 feet, you have resistance to all damage, and your weapon hits deal +2d12 damage.

### Bard
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* Your Bardic Inspiration die gains +1 to its result per 2 prestige levels.

- **I — Encore.** When a creature succeeds on a roll using your Bardic Inspiration, you regain that use.
- **II — Standing Ovation.** When you roll initiative, every ally within 30 feet gains one of your Bardic Inspiration dice for free. Once per session, gain +2 Audience points.
- **III — Showstopper.** Once per short rest, cast a bard spell of 5th level or lower without spending mana.
- **IV — Magnum Opus.** Learn two spells from any class's spell list, of any level you can cast. They count as bard spells.
- **V — Final Act.** Once per long rest, as an action: every ally within 60 feet regains all its hit points, ends all conditions on itself, and has advantage on all rolls until the end of its next turn.

### Cleric
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* One extra use of Channel Divinity per long rest every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Chosen.** Once per long rest, your Divine Intervention automatically works.
- **II — Radiant Soul.** Add your Wisdom modifier to the damage and healing rolls of your cleric spells.
- **III — Sanctified Ground.** Once per long rest, as an action, bless a 30-foot radius for 1 minute. Allies there regain 2 × PB hit points at the start of each of their turns; undead and fiends there have disadvantage on attacks.
- **IV — Miracle Worker.** Once per long rest, cast a cleric spell of 7th level or lower without spending mana.
- **V — Hand of God.** Once per long rest, when an ally within 60 feet would die, it instead regains all its hit points.

### Druid
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* One extra use of Wild Shape per long rest every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Nature's Arsenal.** While in Wild Shape, your natural attacks count as magical, use your Wisdom modifier for attack rolls if it is higher, and deal extra damage equal to your Wisdom modifier.
- **II — Living Fortress.** When you Wild Shape, gain temporary hit points equal to 3 × your Druid level.
- **III — Many Shapes.** You can Wild Shape into monstrosities and plants, not only beasts, using your normal Wild Shape CR limit. An owlbear is now on the menu.
- **IV — Verdant Rebirth.** Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you instead return to half your hit points in a burst of flowers.
- **V — Avatar of the Wild.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute: Wild Shape into any beast, monstrosity or dragon of CR 10 or lower (GM approves the stat block). You keep your spellcasting.

### Fighter
**Prestige · Hit die d10**

*Scaling:* One extra Action Surge per short rest every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Weapon Master.** Your weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20 (or one lower than your current range, if it is already wider).
- **II — Relentless.** When you roll initiative with no Action Surge left, you regain one use.
- **III — Extra Extra Attack.** You make one more attack whenever you take the Attack action.
- **IV — Indomitable Will.** You gain two more uses of Indomitable. When you succeed on a save with it, you have advantage on your next attack roll.
- **V — Living Legend.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute: once per turn, you can turn a missed attack into a hit.

### Monk
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* Your Martial Arts damage gains +1 per 2 prestige levels. You gain 2 extra Focus (ki) points per prestige level.

- **I — Endless Flow.** At the start of each of your turns, if you have no Focus points, regain 1.
- **II — Hundred Fists.** Flurry of Blows makes four unarmed strikes instead of two.
- **III — Perfect Body.** You are immune to disease, poison and exhaustion, and you no longer need food or water.
- **IV — Pressure Points.** Once per turn, you can use Stunning Strike without spending Focus.
- **V — Ascended Master.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute: your speed doubles, your unarmed strikes deal +2d10 force damage, and you do not provoke opportunity attacks.

### Paladin
**Prestige · Hit die d10**

*Scaling:* Your auras' range increases by 10 feet every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Blessed Smite.** Your Divine Smite deals +1d8 radiant damage.
- **II — Bulwark Aura.** At dawn, choose a damage type. Allies in your aura have resistance to it.
- **III — Holy Champion.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute: you sprout wings (fly 60 feet), your weapon hits deal +2d8 radiant damage, and allies in your aura regain 10 hit points at the start of their turns.
- **IV — Oathkeeper.** When an ally in your aura takes damage, you can use your reaction to take it instead, with resistance.
- **V — Final Judgment.** Once per long rest, when you hit with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a Constitution save or drop to 0 hit points. On a success, it takes 10d10 radiant damage. Gods are immune.

### Ranger
**Prestige · Hit die d10**

*Scaling:* Your Hunter's Mark (or favored-enemy) damage gains +1d4 every 4 prestige levels.

- **I — Apex Hunter.** Hunter's Mark costs no mana and does not need concentration.
- **II — Pack Tactics.** Your pet or companion makes two attacks when commanded to attack.
- **III — Ghost of the Wilds.** While you are lightly or heavily obscured and have not attacked this turn, you are invisible.
- **IV — Volley.** When you take the Attack action with a ranged weapon, you can instead make one attack against every creature of your choice within a 10-foot radius you can see.
- **V — Death Sentence.** Once per long rest, mark a creature you can see. For 24 hours, all your hits against it are critical hits.

### Rogue
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* Sneak Attack **+1d6 per 2 prestige levels**.

- **I — Twice Sneaky.** You can deal Sneak Attack damage twice per round: once on your turn, and once on another creature's turn.
- **II — Untouchable.** You can use Uncanny Dodge twice per round (the second costs no reaction).
- **III — Master Thief.** Cunning Action also lets you pick a lock, disarm a trap, make a Sleight of Hand check, or use an object.
- **IV — Shadowstep.** As a bonus action, teleport up to 60 feet from one area of dim light or darkness to another.
- **V — Stroke of Fate.** Once per short rest, turn a missed attack into a hit, or a failed ability check into a 20.

### Sorcerer
**Prestige · Hit die d6**

*Scaling:* +2 sorcery points per prestige level.

- **I — Overchannel.** Once per long rest, when you cast a sorcerer spell that deals damage, it deals maximum damage.
- **II — Metamagic Master.** Learn two more Metamagic options. You can apply two Metamagic options to one spell.
- **III — Wellspring.** When you roll initiative with fewer than half your sorcery points, regain 4 sorcery points.
- **IV — Living Spell.** Once per long rest, cast a sorcerer spell of 8th level or lower without spending mana.
- **V — Unbound.** Once per long rest, for 1 minute, your spells cost no mana. The once-per-long-rest limit on 6th–9th level spells still applies.

### Warlock
**Prestige · Hit die d8**

*Scaling:* +7 Pact Mana per prestige level (see `mana`).

- **I — Patron's Favor.** Gain one more Mystic Arcanum, of any level from 6th to 9th that you do not already have.
- **II — Invocation Master.** Learn two more Eldritch Invocations.
- **III — Pact Surge.** Your pact level becomes 6th. Spells cast with Pact Mana are cast at 6th level and cost 9 Pact Mana.
- **IV — Patron's Avatar.** Once per long rest, summon an avatar of your patron for 1 minute. It uses a stat block of CR up to half your Warlock level, chosen with the GM, and obeys you.
- **V — Dark Bargain.** Once per long rest, ask your patron for anything (as the *wish* spell). Your patron will name a price.

### Wizard
**Prestige · Hit die d6**

*Scaling:* Learn 1 extra wizard spell as a tome spell each prestige level. Arcane Recovery restores 1 more spell level every 2 prestige levels.

- **I — Spell Mastery+.** Choose a 3rd-level wizard spell in your tome. You can cast it at its lowest level without spending mana.
- **II — Arcane Library.** When you read a tome holding a wizard spell, the spell gains 1 extra rank.
- **III — Archmage.** Once per short rest, cast a wizard spell of 5th level or lower without spending mana.
- **IV — Twin Thought.** Once per long rest, cast two spells with one action. Pay mana for both, and only one can need concentration.
- **V — Rewrite Reality.** Once per long rest, cast *wish* without spending mana and without its stress.

## Designer Notes

- **Why prestige?** Without it, everyone past level 20 would multiclass into a pile of low-level features. Prestige makes "stay the course" a real choice.
- **Power trip.** These features are meant to be very strong. Scale encounters to match.
- **Edition.** Feature names follow the Player's Handbook. If your edition calls something different (for example, *ki* or *Focus*), use your edition's version.
