# Safe Room Shops

> *"Welcome to the shop! Prices are final. Haggling is permitted. Stabbing the shopkeeper is not."*

Most safe rooms have at least one **shop**, run by a System shopkeeper: an alien, a construct, or something that only looks like a person. Shops sell gear, weapons, crafting materials and recipes, and they buy almost anything you drag in.

---

## Gold Only

The World Dungeon deals only in **gold pieces (gp)**. There is no silver, copper or platinum.

- **Your purse.** Gold goes into a coin purse on your Interface. It has no weight and uses no inventory slot. If you die, it spills out with your inventory.
- **Small prices.** Anything the 5e price list gives in sp or cp costs **1 gp**, or comes in a bundle worth 1 gp (10 torches, 5 days of rations, 50 feet of rope).
- **Old money.** Earth cash, cards and other coins are junk. Shops will not take them as money.

## Shop Rules

- **Shopping takes time.** A shopping trip (buying, selling and opening boxes) costs 1 floor turn.
- **No theft, no violence.** Shops are part of the safe room. Stealing or attacking counts as violence: you are thrown out, and the shop will not serve you again on this floor.
- **Restock.** Shops restock at the start of every dungeon day (every 24 floor turns).
- **Player Killers.** Shops refuse crawlers with the Player Killer mark, unless it is hidden. Black Market traders (Haggler 15) do not care.
- **Shopkeepers talk.** Shopkeepers know rumors about the floor. A good Charisma check, or a big purchase, can get you one.

## Shop Types

Small safe rooms have one shop. Big ones, and hub safe rooms, can have three or four. The GM picks them, or rolls a d8.

| d8 | Shop | Sells |
|----|------|-------|
| 1 | **General Store** | Adventuring gear, food, tools, healing potions, mana potions, survival kits |
| 2 | **Armory** | Weapons, armor, shields, whetstones, weapon oils |
| 3 | **Gun Shop** | Firearms, rounds, special rounds, gun gear, Kevlar vests |
| 4 | **Bomb Shop** | Explosives, explosive gear, alchemist's supplies |
| 5 | **Apothecary** | Potions, antitoxin, poisons, herbalism kits, Monster Parts |
| 6 | **Arcane Shop** | Spell tomes, spell scrolls, wands, mana items, Essence |
| 7 | **Parts Dealer** | Crafting materials (Scrap, Monster Parts, Essence), recipes, tool kits |
| 8 | **Pet Shop** | Pet treats, pet food, barding, collars, pet carriers |

## What Is in Stock

The deeper the floor, the better the stock. "Limited" means the shop has only that many items of that rarity each day; the GM picks or rolls them.

| Floors | Always in stock | Limited |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **1–2** | Mundane gear, common items | — |
| **3–4** | Mundane, common | Uncommon: 1d4 items |
| **5–8** | Mundane, common, uncommon | Rare: 1d2 items |
| **9–12** | Up to rare | Very rare: 1 item |
| **13–17** | Up to rare | Very rare: 1d3 items |

Legendary items are never for sale, except on the Black Market.

### Today's Special

Each shop has one **special item** each day. Roll on the loot box table that matches the shop (Gun Shop: Firearms Box; Pet Shop: Pet Box; and so on), using the column for the floor:

| Floors | Column |
|--------|--------|
| 1–4 | Bronze |
| 5–9 | Silver |
| 10–14 | Gold |
| 15–17 | Legendary (the GM can reroll anything too powerful) |

## Prices

### Buying

| What | Price |
|------|-------|
| Mundane weapons, armor, gear and tools | The 5e price list (Player's Handbook) |
| Explosives and firearms | The **Value** in the Explosives and Firearms handbooks |
| Magic items: common / uncommon / rare / very rare | 100 / 400 / 4,000 / 40,000 gp |
| Consumable magic items (potions, scrolls, one-use items) | Half the magic item price |

### Crafting Materials

Shops sell materials at twice what they pay for them. Each shop has **1d4 flavors** of Essence and Monster Part in stock each day (fire, cold, venom, and so on).

| Material | Shop buys (T1 / T2 / T3 / T4) | Shop sells (T1 / T2 / T3 / T4) |
|----------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Monster Part | 5 / 25 / 100 / 500 gp | 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 gp |
| Scrap | 5 / 25 / 100 / 500 gp | 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 gp |
| Essence | 10 / 50 / 200 / 1,000 gp | 20 / 100 / 400 / 2,000 gp |
| Trophy Part | Three times a Monster Part | Not for sale |
| Boss Trophy | 100 gp × the boss's CR | Not for sale |

Shops only sell materials up to the tier of the floor: T1 on Floors 1–4, T2 on Floors 5–9, T3 on Floors 10–14, T4 from Floor 15.

### Recipes

Recipes for magic items are sold mostly by Arcane Shops and Parts Dealers.

| Recipe rarity | Price | First available |
|---------------|-------|-----------------|
| Common | Not needed | — |
| Uncommon | 100 gp | Floor 3 |
| Rare | 1,000 gp | Floor 5 |
| Very rare | 10,000 gp | Floor 9 |
| Legendary | Never sold | Loot and quests only |

## Selling

- Shops buy gear and magic items at **half their value**.
- Materials sell at the "Shop buys" price above. Junk sells at its listed price.
- Unidentified magic items sell for the common price (50 gp) until identified.
- Shops will not buy loot boxes, which are bound to you anyway.

## Services

| Service | Cost | Time |
|---------|------|------|
| Repair a broken item | 10% of its value | 1 floor turn |
| Identify a magic item (properties, curses, charges) | 25 gp | None |
| Fit an attachment to a firearm | 10 gp | None |
| Weapon or armor training (Armory or Gun Shop) | 250 gp | 8 floor turns |
| Workbench use | Free | — |

## Discounts

| Source | Discount |
|--------|----------|
| Haggler Crawler Skill | 2% per Skill Level |
| Celebrity Status | 10% |
| Fake VIP Pass | 20% for one day |

Discounts add together, up to **50%** off.
