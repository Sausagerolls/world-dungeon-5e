# Achievements and the Audience

> *"New achievement! Punching Up. You killed something that should have killed you. The audience is thrilled. Your mother would be horrified."*

The System watches everything a crawler does and rewards what it finds notable, brave, stupid or entertaining. These rewards are **achievements**. Most achievements come with a **loot box**.

---

## How Achievements Work

1. **The GM decides.** When a crawler does something that deserves recognition, the GM announces an achievement. Use the examples below, or invent one on the spot.
2. **The System announces it.** Read it out as a System message, with a name and a short, snarky line. The notification arrives between turns.
3. **The GM picks a box.** Choose the **box type** that fits the deed, and the **rarity** from the achievement's tier (below).
4. **The crawler claims it.** The box goes to the crawler's box list. It can be opened in a safe room (see `loot-boxes`).

### Achievement Tiers

| Tier | Box rarity | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Minor** | The floor's usual box | First kill with a new weapon type |
| **Notable** | The floor's "big deed" box | Killing five mobs with one explosion |
| **Major** | The floor's "exceptional deed" box | Killing a creature far above your level |
| **Epic** | Legendary | Killing a floor boss alone |
| **Mythic** | Celestial | Something the whole table will talk about for years |

"Usual", "big deed" and "exceptional deed" refer to the **Rarity by Floor** table in `loot-boxes`. A Minor achievement gives a Bronze box on Floor 1, and a Gold box on Floor 15.

### Achievement Rules

- **Once each.** A crawler earns each achievement once. **Series** achievements (marked I, II, III) can be earned again at higher counts.
- **Everyone who helped.** In a group effort, each crawler who took part earns the achievement and their own box.
- **Global firsts.** The first crawler in the whole dungeon to earn an achievement gets a box one rarity higher. The System announces it to everyone.
- **Extras.** An achievement can also give a Stat Point, Audience points, a title, or a System grant of a Crawler Skill. Use these sparingly.
- **Hidden list.** Crawlers do not see the list of achievements. Finding them is part of the fun.

## The Audience

Billions of viewers watch the crawl. Each crawler has an **Audience Meter** from 0 to 10.

| Moment | Audience points |
|--------|-----------------|
| Entertaining (a good joke, a cool kill, a daring move) | +1 |
| Jaw-dropping (a wild stunt, a perfect plan, a huge explosion) | +2 |
| Legendary (the clip everyone will share) | +3 |
| A whole session with nothing interesting | −1 |
| Hiding in safe rooms for 24 floor turns or more in a row | −1 |
| Taking the stairs down early (see `safe-rooms-and-timer`) | −1 to −3 |

**Filling the meter.** When the meter reaches 10, the crawler gets a **Fan Box** and the meter resets to 0. Extra points carry over.

| Floor | Fan Box rarity |
|-------|----------------|
| 1–4 | Bronze |
| 5–9 | Silver |
| 10–14 | Gold |
| 15–18 | Legendary |

When the Fan Box arrives, roll a d20. On a 20, it is one rarity higher. The fans are generous, and also strange: Fan Boxes can hold **troll prizes**.

*(Viewers, followers and sponsors will get full rules in a forthcoming `viewers-and-sponsors` file.)*

## Spicy and Murder Boxes

**Spicy Box.** When crawlers do something saucy on camera (flirting mid-battle, a romantic moment in a safe room, something the System has to blur), the GM can award a Spicy Box. Keep it to the table's comfort level. "Fade to black" is always fine.

**Murder Box.** This is automatic. The System awards it every time a crawler kills a non-combatant NPC or another crawler, and gives the **Player Killer** mark (see `loot-boxes`).

| Victim | Murder Box rarity |
|--------|-------------------|
| Non-combatant NPC | Bronze |
| Crawler of your level or lower | Silver |
| Crawler of a higher level | Gold |
