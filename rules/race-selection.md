# Floor 3 Race Selection

> *"Tired of being a squishy, unremarkable human? So is everyone watching. Please select a new race from the menu. Tails are extra. Just kidding. Tails are free."*

On Floor 3 the **Race and Class selection** menu (see `class-selection`) asks for a race as well as a class. Each crawler chooses both before they leave the first safe room on the floor. All sales are final.

## Before Floor 3: No Race

Until Floor 3 you have **no race**. You are what you were before the collapse, normally a human, and you have only the "ordinary person" traits from character creation (one extra skill, and one extra language or tool; see `crawler`).

- **Being human is not the Human race.** A crawler who was human before the collapse does not have the Human species traits (Resourceful, Skillful, Versatile). They gain them only by choosing **Human** on Floor 3, like any other race.
- You use the plain defaults until then: **Medium** size, **30 feet** of speed, no darkvision, no resistances.

## Choosing a Race

**You can pick any species from the 2024 Player's Handbook:** Aasimar, Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc or Tiefling. Your GM can add species from other books.

- **Hidden options.** Some extra races appear on the menu only for crawlers with specific achievements. The GM decides which, and when. A crawler who has spent two floors hiding in vents might find something small and sneaky on their list.
- **The System's pitch.** As with classes, the System "recommends" a race. Ignore it if you like.

| **Aasimar** | "A little bit of heaven. Sponsors love a halo." |
| **Dragonborn** | "Breathe fire. Literally. The audience has been asking." |
| **Dwarf** | "Short, sturdy and poison-resistant. Ideal for a dungeon, which is where you are." |

## Gaining Your Race

1. **Gain every trait.** You gain all of the race's traits. A trait that improves at a character level (a Tiefling's legacy spells at 3rd and 5th level, an Aasimar's Celestial Revelation at 3rd, a Dragonborn's Draconic Flight at 5th, a Goliath's Large Form at 5th) uses your **character level**. You are usually 7th–9th level by Floor 3, so you gain most of them at once.
2. **Make the race's choices.** Choose everything the race asks for:
   - **Size**, if the race lets you choose (Small or Medium).
   - **Lineage or ancestry:** an Elven lineage, a Gnomish lineage, a Dragonborn's draconic ancestry, a Goliath's giant ancestry, a Tiefling's fiendish legacy.
   - **Skills:** Human *Skillful* gives one skill of your choice. Other races name their own list.
   - **Feats:** Human *Versatile* gives an Origin feat of your choice. Make that feat's own choices too.
   - **Spellcasting ability** for the race's spells: Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma, where the race lets you choose.
   - **Resistances** where the race offers a choice.
3. **Spells go in your tome.** Spells a race teaches are tome spells (see `tome-magic`). You learn them straight away, ignoring the tome spell level limit and the Intelligence requirement. A race spell you can cast "once without a spell slot" can be cast once per long rest without spending mana.
4. **Replace the defaults.** The race's size, speed, darkvision and other senses replace the ordinary-person defaults.
5. **Ability scores.** 2024 species do not raise ability scores, and crawlers already have their Stat Points, so nothing changes. If your table uses older races that do give ability bonuses, they apply in full, with no cap.
6. **Keep the old you.** You keep the ordinary-person skill and language or tool. If the race gives a proficiency you already have, choose another skill of the same kind instead.

The Crawler Sheet asks for all of these choices when you make your selection.

## Changing Race

All sales are final. A crawler changes race only through a rare loot box reward, a celestial relic, or something worse. The GM decides.

## GM Notes

- Run race and class selection as one scene. Read the race pitches as the System's sales patter.
- Players who were human before the collapse often forget that Human is a choice with its own traits. Remind them: the System does not count "being from Earth" as a race.
- Characters arrive at Floor 3 already 7th–9th level, so a Tiefling or Aasimar gains several level-based traits at once. Give the table time to read them.
