# Pets

> *"Pets are the most popular contestants on the show. Statistically, viewers would rather watch your dog than you. Please do not take this personally."*

A pet is a creature that fights beside you, follows your commands and grows stronger as you do. The audience adores them. Any crawler can have one; the **Pet Trainer** Crawler Skill makes them much better.

---

## Getting a Pet

| Way | How |
|-----|-----|
| **Pet Egg** | Found in Pet Boxes and some enemy loot. It hatches after your next long rest. Roll on the egg's hatch table (below). |
| **Taming** | Calm a hostile creature and win it over (see *Taming*). |
| **From Earth** | If your starting pocket item was an animal (a cat, a dog, a hamster), it is your pet from 1st level. The System may have plans for it. |
| **Rewards** | Some achievements, quests and sponsors give a pet. |

### How Many Pets

- You can have **one active pet**: the one that follows you and fights.
- Extra pets must be stored in a **Pet Carrier** (one pet per carrier), or left in a safe room. A pet left in a safe room waits there safely, but it is lost if the floor collapses before you come back for it.

---

## Pet Rules

### In Combat

- **Turn.** Your pet acts on your initiative, right after you.
- **Commands.** On your turn, you can use a **bonus action** to command your pet. It takes one action from its stat block (Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, or a special action) and moves as you like. Without a command, it takes the **Dodge** action and stays near you.
- **If you go down.** While you are incapacitated, your pet acts on its own. It protects you, attacking whatever threatens you.
- **Pet Trainer 5** lets you command an attack as part of your Attack action. A **Leash of Command** lets you command it with no action at all.

### Statistics

Your pet uses its creature's stat block, with these changes:

| Stat | Rule |
|------|------|
| **Hit points** | The higher of its stat block hit points, or **4 × your character level**. Add Pet Trainer and Tough upgrade bonuses on top. |
| **Bond bonus** | With the Pet Trainer Crawler Skill, your pet adds your **proficiency bonus** to its AC, attack rolls, damage rolls and saving throws. |
| **Understanding** | It understands simple commands in your language, even if it cannot speak. |
| **Hit Dice** | It has the Hit Dice from its stat block and can spend them on a short rest like you. |

### Resting, Healing and Safe Rooms

- Your pet rests when you do and gains the same benefits.
- **Heal Self** does not work on pets (it is *self*). Pet Treats, Premium Pet Food, healing potions and healing spells all do.
- Pets are allowed in safe rooms. Like everyone else, they cannot fight there.
- Living pets cannot go in your Crawler Inventory, except in a Pet Carrier.

### Dropping to 0 Hit Points

- At 0 hit points, your pet falls unconscious and makes **death saving throws**, like a crawler.
- Any healing, or a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check, stabilizes it.
- If it dies, a **Pet Revival Stone** (within 10 days) or *revivify* / *raise dead* can bring it back.

### The Audience Loves Pets

When your pet does something heroic, cute or ridiculous on camera, the GM can award your Audience Meter **+1**. Achievements like *Who's a Good Boy?*, *Beastmaster* and *Evolution* are all about pets.

## Pet Size Limits (CR)

The strongest creature you can keep as a pet depends on the Pet Trainer Crawler Skill.

| Pet Trainer | Maximum pet CR (taming) |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| None | 1/4 |
| Skill Level 1 | 1/4 (and it gains the bond bonus) |
| Skill Level 5 | 1 |
| Skill Level 10 | 3 |
| Skill Level 15 | 5 |

**Eggs ignore this limit.** A hatchling from a Pet Egg is always yours, whatever its CR.

## Pets Grow With You

Pets do not gain levels. Instead, they grow as **you** level:

- Their hit points rise with your level (4 × your level, at least).
- At character levels **4, 8, 12, 16 and 20**, and every 4 levels after that, your pet gains one **upgrade** (below). Write it on your Pet page.
- **Evolutions** are rarer and much bigger (see *Evolutions*).

### Upgrades

Choose one each time. You can take an upgrade more than once unless it says otherwise.

| Upgrade | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Tough** | +10 hit point maximum. |
| **Sharp** | +1 to the pet's attack and damage rolls (up to +3). |
| **Armored** | +1 AC (up to +3). |
| **Quick** | +10 feet to all its speeds. |
| **Keen** | Darkvision 60 feet (or +60 feet), and advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that use smell or hearing. Once only. |
| **Growth** | The pet grows one size larger (up to Large). Its damage dice increase by one step (d4 to d6, d6 to d8, and so on). A Large pet can carry a Medium or smaller rider. Once only. |
| **Trick** | The pet learns one trick (below). |

### Tricks

A **trick** is something you have trained your pet to do on top of fighting: fetch things, keep watch, sniff out traps, play dead. Tricks are the pet's skills outside its stat block.

**Learning a trick.** A pet learns a trick when you choose the **Trick** upgrade (above), at character level 4, 8, 12 and so on. Pick any trick from the table. A pet can know each trick only once, and keeps its tricks for good, through evolutions too. Write them on your Pet page.

**Using a trick.** The **Use** column says how each trick works:

| Use | What it means |
|-----|---------------|
| **Command** | Use your **bonus action** to command the pet, as for any other command. Doing the trick is the pet's action for that turn, and it can move as part of it. With a *Leash of Command*, commanding takes no action. |
| **Reaction** | The pet uses its reaction when the trigger happens. You don't need to command it, but it must not be incapacitated. It can do this once per round. |
| **Always on** | Works on its own whenever the pet is with you (within 30 feet), awake, and not incapacitated or in a Pet Carrier. |
| **Limited** | Works on its own, but only as often as the trick says. |

| Trick | Use | Effect |
|-------|-----|--------|
| **Fetch** | Command | The pet moves up to its speed, picks up one unattended object within 30 feet that it can carry, and brings it back to you. It can fetch an item from a creature only if the creature is unconscious or dead. |
| **Guard** | Reaction | When a creature within 5 feet of the pet makes an attack roll against you, the pet gives that attack disadvantage. You must also be within 5 feet of the pet. |
| **Sniff Out** | Always on | You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Intelligence (Investigation) checks to find traps, secret doors and hidden creatures, and on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track. The pet can follow a scent up to a day old. |
| **Pack Mule** | Always on | The pet carries up to **10 inventory slots** of items for you, on top of your own. Taking an item from it, or giving it one, is your free object interaction while you are within 5 feet. Everything it carries spills out where it falls if it drops to 0 hit points. |
| **Alarm** | Always on | While you rest, the pet keeps watch and wakes you when danger comes. Your party can't be surprised during a short or long rest, and Ambush Checks made while you rest get −2. |
| **Play Dead** | Command | The pet drops and lies still. Until it moves, acts, or takes damage, enemies ignore it and won't attack it. A creature that looks closely sees through it with a Wisdom (Insight) check (DC 8 + your PB + the pet's Charisma modifier). |
| **Grab** | Always on | When the pet hits a creature no more than one size larger than itself with a melee attack, it can also grapple it (escape DC 8 + its Strength modifier + your PB). It can grapple one creature at a time. |
| **Shiny!** | Limited | After each fight, if the party loots the bodies, the pet finds coins the crawlers missed: **1d6 gp × the floor number**. |
| **Cute** | Limited | Once per session, when the pet does something adorable on camera, the audience melts: **+1 Audience point**. You describe it; the GM decides if it lands. |

**Teaching tricks in play.** The GM can also award a trick for training it on screen: practising Fetch in every safe room for a whole floor, say. A trick learned this way doesn't use up an upgrade.

## Evolutions

An evolution is a major change: wings, a breath weapon, the ability to talk. Pets evolve from:

- **Pet Trainer 15** (one evolution).
- A **Pet Evolution Crystal** (one each).
- The **Crown of the Beast King** relic, special achievements, and sponsors.

Roll a d12 or choose with the GM. A pet can have each evolution only once.

| d12 | Evolution | Effect |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| 1 | **Wings** | It gains a flying speed equal to its walking speed. |
| 2 | **Breath Weapon** | Action (recharge 5–6): 15-foot cone, Dexterity save (DC 8 + your PB + its Con modifier), damage **1d6 per your PB** of one type (chosen at evolution), half on a success. |
| 3 | **Armored Hide** | +2 AC, and resistance to one damage type of your choice. |
| 4 | **Titan** | It grows one size larger (past Large). +2 Strength and Constitution. Its hit points become 6 × your level, at least. |
| 5 | **Venom** | Its attacks deal +1d8 poison damage. A creature hit must succeed on a Constitution save (same DC as Breath Weapon) or be poisoned until the end of its next turn. |
| 6 | **Mind Link** | It can speak to you telepathically up to 1 mile away. Its Intelligence becomes at least 8. |
| 7 | **Elemental Aura** | Enemies that start their turn within 5 feet of it take **1d6 fire, cold or lightning** damage (chosen at evolution). |
| 8 | **Regeneration** | It regains 5 hit points at the start of its turn while it has at least 1 hit point. |
| 9 | **Blink** | Once per turn, it can teleport up to 30 feet as part of its movement. |
| 10 | **Talking** | It can speak, read and write. It has opinions. Once per session, its commentary earns +1 Audience point. |
| 11 | **Pack Leader** | As an action, it summons 1d4 smaller copies of itself (CR 1/4 versions) that fight for 1 minute. Once per long rest. |
| 12 | **Apex** | +2 to all its ability scores, and its attacks score a critical hit on a 19 or 20. |

## Taming

You can try to tame a hostile creature with an Intelligence of 3 or lower, of a CR you are allowed to keep.

1. The creature must be **calm or cornered**: below half its hit points, restrained, charmed, or fed something it likes (Pet Treats count).
2. As an action, make a **Wisdom (Animal Handling)** check. DC = **10 + 2 × the creature's CR** (minimum 10). Pet Treats or Premium Pet Food give advantage.
3. **Success:** it becomes your pet. It keeps its current hit points.
4. **Failure:** it attacks or flees. You cannot try to tame that creature again for 24 floor turns.

Taming a creature earns the *Beastmaster* achievement the first time.
