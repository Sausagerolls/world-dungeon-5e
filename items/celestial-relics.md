# Celestial Relics

> *"Attention, Crawler. You have received a box of Celestial rarity. This has happened four times in the history of the show. Two of those crawlers are now gods. The other two are craters."*

Celestial relics are the most powerful items in the World Dungeon. They are **meant to break the game**. Each one is unique: only one of each exists in a campaign.

## Rules

- A **Celestial-rarity box** holds the box type's **signature relic**, plus two rolls on that box's Legendary column, plus 10,000 gp.
- If that signature relic is already claimed in this campaign, roll on the **General Relics** table instead.
- Relics require attunement, but do not count against your attunement limit.
- Relics cannot be destroyed by normal means, and cannot be stored in another crawler's inventory against their will.
- **GM warning:** give out at most one or two in a whole campaign. A relic changes what the party can do. Plan the floors after it with that in mind.

## Signature Relics

### The Everpack
**Celestial relic · Adventurer Box**

Your Crawler Inventory has unlimited slots, and you can store or retrieve any number of items each turn with no action. Once per long rest, reach in and pull out any common or uncommon potion, or any mundane item worth 100 gp or less.

### The Big One
**Celestial relic · Demolitionist Box**

A bomb the size of a suitcase. One use. Set it, and choose a timer from 1 minute to 1 hour. When it goes off, everything in a **500-foot radius** takes **40d6 fire damage** (Dexterity save DC 25, half on a success), and all structures there are destroyed. Boss arenas and safe rooms survive. Nothing else does.

### The Godslayer
**Celestial relic · Firearms Box**

A long rifle with a single golden round, which reloads itself at the start of each floor. Range 1,000/4,000. On a hit, the target takes damage equal to its **hit point maximum**. A creature with legendary resistance can use one to take 20d10 radiant damage instead. A god takes 20d10 radiant damage and remembers your face.

### Edge of Ending
**Celestial relic · Swordsman Box**

A +4 sword of any kind. When you hit, the target's hit point maximum drops by the damage dealt, until it finishes a long rest. On a critical hit against a creature with a head, it loses the head. A boss can make a Constitution save (DC 25) to take 20d8 slashing damage instead.

### Bow of the Hunter Moon
**Celestial relic · Archer Box**

A +4 longbow. Once per turn, one of your attacks with it hits automatically. It can target any creature you have Inspected on the same floor, at any range, around corners.

### Fists of the Breaker
**Celestial relic · Pugilist Box**

Your unarmed strikes deal **4d12 force damage**, ignore resistance and immunity, and score a critical hit on 18–20. You can punch through any nonmagical wall, door or floor as part of an attack.

### Aegis of the Last Floor
**Celestial relic · Armory Box**

Armor that looks like whatever you were wearing when you fell into the dungeon. Your AC is 22, and you add your Dex modifier. You are immune to critical hits, and you have resistance to all damage.

### Tongue of the Archon
**Celestial relic · Chatter Box**

Once per long rest, speak one sentence to a creature that can understand you. It must obey for 24 hours, unless the order is to kill itself. A boss can make a Wisdom save (DC 25). Gods are immune, but find it charming.

### Cloak of Nowhere
**Celestial relic · Shadow Box**

You can become invisible, or visible, at will. While invisible, truesight cannot see you, the System cannot track you, and Inspect shows nothing.

### The Unwritten Grimoire
**Celestial relic · Tome Box**

You can learn any spell from any list as a tome spell, of any level, without a tome and with no spell level limit. Once per long rest, you can cast each tome spell you know once without spending mana.

### Manual of Mastery
**Celestial relic · Skill Box**

Choose a Crawler Skill. It becomes Skill Level 20, with the Limit Breaker feat, at once. Any Crawler Skill you gain from now on starts at Skill Level 5.

### Crown of the Beast King
**Celestial relic · Pet Box**

Your pet evolves into a legendary creature (GM's choice, CR up to half your character level). If it dies, it returns to life at the next dawn. It can talk, and it has opinions about the crown.

### Spark of Divinity
**Celestial relic · Divine Box**

You become a demigod. Two ability scores increase by 4. You are immune to one damage type of your choice. Once per long rest, you can cast any cleric spell, of any level, without a slot or mana. Gods now treat you as a rival.

### The Golden Ticket
**Celestial relic · Fan Box**

Name any item in this book that is not a Celestial relic. You get it. Or, give the ticket back to the audience, and every crawler on the floor gets a Gold Fan Box.

### The Kiss of Ruin
**Celestial relic · Spicy Box**

A tube of lipstick that never runs out. Once per long rest, blow a kiss at a creature within 60 feet. It is charmed by you for 24 hours, with no save. Bosses can make a Wisdom save (DC 25).

### The Reaper's Ledger
**Celestial relic · Murder Box**

Once per floor, write the name of a creature you have Inspected. At the next midnight, it dies. A boss takes 20d10 necrotic damage instead. A god is not amused. Owning the ledger gives you a permanent Player Killer mark that no mask can hide.

### Boss Core
**Celestial relic · Boss Box**

You gain **3 legendary actions** per round (attack, move up to your speed, or cast a cantrip, each costing one) and **3 legendary resistances** per long rest. Inspect now lists you as a boss.

### Sigil of Two Paths
**Celestial relic · Class Box**

Choose a second class. You gain its features as if you had levels in it equal to half your character level (rounded down), on top of your normal class levels. This continues as you gain levels.

## General Relics

Roll when a box's signature relic is already claimed.

| d10 | Relic | Effect |
|-----|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Bottled Wish** | Three uses. Each casts *wish* with no stress or risk. |
| 2 | **Phoenix Heart** | When you die, you return to life at the next dawn with full hit points. Three uses. |
| 3 | **Floor Skip Token** | One use. Your party skips the rest of the current floor and arrives at the first safe room of the next one. You keep all achievements earned so far. |
| 4 | **Stopwatch of Do-Overs** | Once per floor, rewind the last round of combat. Everyone but you forgets it happened. |
| 5 | **Font of Potential** | Gain 6 Stat Points. |
| 6 | **System Override Key** | One use. Change one rule of the current floor (for example, its timer, its weather, or one boss's immunity). The GM has the final say on wording. |
| 7 | **Doppel Mirror** | One use. Create an exact copy of any item that is not a Celestial relic. |
| 8 | **Crown of Command** | Once per long rest, cast *dominate monster* (DC 25) with no concentration. It lasts 24 hours. |
| 9 | **Sponsor Contract** | A god becomes your sponsor. Once per floor, it sends you a Gold box of your choice. It will also ask for favors. |
| 10 | **Seed of a Floor** | Plant it in a safe room. It grows into a private floor-sized pocket world you can enter at will. It is safe, it is yours, and the System hates it. |
