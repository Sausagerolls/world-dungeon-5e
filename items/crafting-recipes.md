# Crafting Recipes

Every item you can craft, with its cost. See `crafting` for the rules.

**How to read the tables**

- **Gold** is per item. **Materials** are per item. A time of "1 (×2)" means 1 floor turn makes two items for that cost.
- **Time** is in floor turns at a safe room workbench, or **Field** (one batch of up to PB items during a short rest, anywhere).
- **Req** is the Crawler Skill Level or other requirement. Explosives need Demolitionist, firearms need Tinkerer (see `crafting`).
- **T1–T4** are material tiers. A flavor in brackets, like *Essence (fire)*, must match.
