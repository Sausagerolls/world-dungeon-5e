# Firearms

> *"The System notes that the dungeon's residents have never seen a gun. They will learn quickly. So will the crawler who missed."*

Guns came into the World Dungeon with the crawlers, and the System's loot tables were happy to add more. Firearms come mostly from **Firearms Boxes** and safe room shops.

---

## Firearm Rules

### Proficiency

Firearms are **martial ranged weapons**. A character proficient with martial weapons is proficient with firearms. A crawler without that proficiency can gain it from the **Gunslinger** or **Weapon Specialist** Crawler Skills, by training, or by using a firearm in five fights. Without proficiency, you add **half** your proficiency bonus to the attack roll (see *Learning by Doing* in The Crawler handbook).

### Firearm Properties

| Property | Rule |
|----------|------|
| **Ammunition** | Each shot uses one round. Fired rounds cannot be recovered. |
| **Reload X** | The weapon fires X shots before it must be reloaded. Reloading takes an action (or a bonus action with a Speed Loader or Gunslinger). |
| **Burst Fire** | As an action, spray a 10-foot cube within normal range. Each creature in it makes a Dexterity save (DC 8 + PB + Dex modifier) or takes the weapon's normal damage. Uses 10 rounds. |
| **Scatter** | As an action, fire a 15-foot cone instead of making an attack. Each creature in it makes a Dexterity save (DC 8 + PB + Dex modifier) or takes the weapon's damage, half on a success. Uses 1 round. |
| **Loud** | Gunfire can be heard up to 300 feet away. Mobs come to look. |
| **Misfire** | If you roll a 1 on the attack roll, the weapon jams. Clearing it takes an action. |
| **Heavy, Two-Handed, Light** | As the standard 5e weapon properties. |

### Ammunition

Standard rounds are cheap and common. A **box of rounds** holds 20 rounds of one type (pistol, rifle, or shotgun shells). Special rounds replace standard ones and add an effect on a hit.

| Special Round | Rarity | Effect on a hit | Value (each) |
|---------------|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| Rubber | Common | Damage is nonlethal. The target drops to 0 and is unconscious, not dying. | 1 gp |
| Tracer | Common | The next attack roll against the target before the end of your next turn has advantage. | 2 gp |
| Hollow Point | Uncommon | +1d6 damage against a target wearing no armor. | 5 gp |
| Armor-Piercing | Uncommon | +2 to the attack roll against a target wearing armor or with natural armor. | 5 gp |
| Incendiary | Uncommon | +1d6 fire damage. Flammable objects ignite. | 5 gp |
| Silver | Uncommon | Counts as silvered. +1d6 damage against shapechangers. | 10 gp |
| Holy | Rare | +1d8 radiant damage against undead and fiends. | 25 gp |
| Explosive | Rare | +1d10 thunder damage, and creatures within 5 feet of the target take 1d6 fire. | 25 gp |

## Firearm Notes

### Nail Gun
**Firearm · Common · Bronze box**

A builder's tool, not a weapon, which makes it quiet: it does not have the Loud property. It reloads from any box of nails.

### Flare Gun
**Firearm · Common · Bronze box**

On a hit, a flammable target catches fire (1d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns until it uses an action to put it out). You can fire it into the air to light a 40-foot radius with bright light for 1 minute.

### Sawn-Off Shotgun
**Firearm · Uncommon · Silver box**

**Point blank.** When you use scatter, creatures within 5 feet of you take full damage even if they succeed on the save.

### Sniper Rifle
**Firearm · Rare · Gold box**

**Scoped.** If you have not moved this turn, attacking at long range does not give you disadvantage.

### Hand Cannon
**Firearm · Rare · Gold box**

**Knockback.** On a hit, a Large or smaller target is pushed 10 feet away from you.

### Grenade Launcher
**Firearm · Rare · Gold box**

Fires any explosive marked "grenade" (Frag, Concussion, Frost, Flashbang, Smoke Bomb, Sticky Bomb) to a point within range, instead of throwing it. A Sticky Bomb fired this way still needs an attack roll.

### Rocket Launcher
**Firearm · Very rare · Legendary box**

Fires one **rocket** to a point within range. Rockets cost 1,000 gp each. A rocket explodes in a 20-foot radius: Dexterity save DC 16, 8d6 fire damage, half on a success. Double damage to objects and structures.

### Minigun
**Firearm · Very rare · Legendary box**

You need Strength 15 or higher to use it. It has to spin up: you cannot use burst fire with it on the turn you draw it.

## Magic Firearms

### +1, +2 or +3 Firearm
**Firearm · Uncommon / Rare / Very rare**

You have a bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic firearm. The bonus depends on its rarity.

### Ghost Gun
**Firearm · Rare · Gold box**

A service pistol that makes no sound and never needs reloading. Attacks with it ignore half and three-quarters cover.

### Dragonbreath Shotgun
**Firearm · Very rare · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

A +2 pump shotgun. Its scatter is a 30-foot cone of fire: 4d8 fire damage instead of piercing, and flammable objects in the cone ignite.

### Six-Shooter of the Last Stand
**Firearm · Very rare · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

A +2 revolver. While you are below half your hit points, it scores a critical hit on a 19 or 20. Once per long rest, as an action, you can **fan the hammer**: make six attacks with it at disadvantage.

### The Negotiator
**Firearm · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

A +3 hand cannon. On a hit, the target must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 17) or be frightened of you for 1 minute. While you hold it, you have advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. It talks, and it has opinions.

### Plasma Caster
**Firearm · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

An alien rifle. 4d8 radiant damage, range 150/600, two-handed, reload 20 (recharges at dawn). Once per long rest, fire a 100-foot line, 5 feet wide: Dexterity save DC 18, 10d8 radiant damage, half on a success.

### Railgun
**Firearm · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

4d10 lightning damage, range 500/2,000, heavy, two-handed, reload 1. Its shot pierces: it can hit every creature in a 5-foot-wide line up to its normal range. Make one attack roll and compare it to each creature's AC.

## Gun Gear

### Box of Rounds
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

20 standard rounds of one type: pistol, rifle, or shotgun shells.

### Speed Loader
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

You can reload one firearm as a bonus action.

### Holster
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

You can draw a pistol from it as part of the attack. You have +1 to initiative while it holds a pistol.

### Bayonet
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Fits a two-handed firearm. You can make a melee attack with it, as a spear (1d6 piercing).

### Laser Sight
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

Attachment. +1 to attack rolls with the firearm within its normal range.

### Scope
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

Attachment. The firearm gains the Scoped property (see Sniper Rifle).

### Suppressor
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

Attachment for a pistol or rifle. The firearm loses the Loud property.

### Extended Magazine
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

Attachment. Double the firearm's Reload number.

### Kevlar Vest
**Armor · Uncommon · Silver box**

Light armor, AC 13 + Dex modifier. You have resistance to piercing damage from firearms.

### Riot Shield
**Armor · Rare · Gold box**

A shield (+2 AC). When a ranged attack hits you, you can use your reaction to add +3 to your AC against it, which may make it miss.
