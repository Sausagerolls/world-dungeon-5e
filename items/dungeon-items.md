# Dungeon Items

These are the setting items found in loot boxes, other than explosives (`explosives`), firearms (`firearms`) and Celestial relics (`celestial-relics`). They are grouped by the box they usually come from, but many appear in several boxes.

**Reading an entry.** The metadata line gives the item type, its 5e rarity, and the box tier it usually appears in. A **troll prize** is a joke from the audience: it has little or no use.

---

## Universal Items

### Stat Potion
**Potion · Uncommon · Any box**

When you drink it, you gain **1 Stat Point**. If the box says an ability, for example *Stat Potion (Charisma)*, the point must go into that ability.

### Skill Book
**Book · Varies · Any box**

Reading it takes 1 minute, then it crumbles. You learn the named Crawler Skill at Skill Level 1. If you already know it, it gains 1 Skill Level instead (maximum 15).

- **Random:** roll a d6 for the category (1–2 Combat, 3 Survival, 4 Utility, 5 Showmanship, 6 Magic). The GM picks or rolls a Crawler Skill from it.
- **Your choice:** you pick any Crawler Skill.

### Training Manual
**Book · Rare · Gold box**

Choose a Crawler Skill you know. It gains **2 Skill Levels** (maximum 15).

### Manual of Limits
**Book · Legendary · Legendary box**

If you have a Crawler Skill at Skill Level 15, you gain the **Limit Breaker** feat for it. Otherwise, one Crawler Skill you know gains 3 Skill Levels (maximum 15).

## Adventurer Items

### Mana Potion
**Potion · Common · Bronze box**

You regain **2d4 + 2** mana.

### Greater Mana Potion
**Potion · Uncommon · Silver box**

You regain **4d4 + 4** mana.

### Superior Mana Potion
**Potion · Rare · Gold box**

You regain **8d4 + 8** mana.

### Supreme Mana Potion
**Potion · Very rare · Legendary box**

You regain **10d4 + 20** mana.

### Survival Kit
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Rations (5 days), a waterskin, 5 torches, 50 feet of rope, a tinderbox, a bedroll and a mess kit. It fills one inventory slot.

### Pocket Expander
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

A small silver key. Turn it in the air and it vanishes. Your Crawler Inventory permanently gains **5 slots**.

### Grand Pocket Expander
**Wondrous item · Very rare · Legendary box**

As a Pocket Expander, but your inventory gains **20 slots**.

### Stairwell Compass
**Wondrous item · Common · Bronze box**

The needle always points to the nearest stairwell on the current floor.

### Safe Room Key
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box**

One use. As an action, touch a wall to open a door into a private safe room for 8 hours. It holds up to 8 creatures. It counts as a safe room for long rests and opening loot boxes, but has no shop. It does not work in a boss arena, or in the last hour of the floor timer.

## Swordsman Items

### Whetstone of Keenness
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Spend 1 minute sharpening a weapon. Its next 10 hits deal +1d4 damage.

### Weapon Oil
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

As an action, coat a weapon. For 1 minute, it deals +1d4 fire, cold or lightning damage (chosen when the oil is found).

### Duelist's Gloves
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box · Requires attunement**

While you wield a one-handed melee weapon and nothing in your other hand, you gain +1 AC and +2 damage with that weapon.

### Scabbard of Quick Draw
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

While a weapon is in the scabbard, you have +2 to initiative. When you roll initiative, you can draw it for free.

## Archer Items

### Rope Arrow
**Ammunition · Common · Bronze box**

When it hits a surface, it anchors 50 feet of rope between the arrow and a point you choose next to you. The rope holds 500 pounds.

### Glue Arrow
**Ammunition · Common · Bronze box**

On a hit, the target must succeed on a Strength save (DC 13) or its speed becomes 0 until it uses an action to pull free.

### Stormstring Bow
**Weapon · Very rare · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

A +2 longbow. On a hit, it deals +1d8 lightning damage, and lightning arcs to another creature within 10 feet of the target for 1d8 lightning damage. Once per long rest, as an action, fire a storm volley at a point within range: each creature in a 30-foot radius makes a Dexterity save (DC 16), taking 6d8 lightning damage on a failure, half on a success.

## Pugilist Items

### Protein Shake
**Potion · Common · Bronze box**

As a bonus action, drink it. You gain 1d10 temporary hit points and have advantage on Strength checks for 1 hour.

### Brass Knuckles
**Weapon · Common · Bronze box**

Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage, if that is higher than your normal unarmed damage.

### Steel-Toe Boots
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

You can make unarmed strikes with your feet for 1d6 bludgeoning damage, even while your hands are full or you are grappled.

### Hand Wraps
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

Your unarmed strikes count as magical. You have +2 to Strength (Athletics) checks to grapple and shove.

### Iron Knuckles
**Weapon · Uncommon (+1) / Rare (+2) / Very rare (+3)**

Your unarmed strikes deal at least 1d6 damage, and you have a bonus to attack and damage rolls with them equal to the knuckles' bonus.

### Thunder Gloves
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

When you hit with an unarmed strike, it deals +1d6 thunder damage, and a Large or smaller target is pushed 5 feet away.

### Champion's Belt
**Wondrous item · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

Your Strength increases by 2. Your unarmed strikes score a critical hit on a 19 or 20. When you knock out a creature with an unarmed strike, your Audience Meter gains 1 point.

## Chatter Items

### Breath Mints
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

Eat one as a bonus action. You have advantage on your next Charisma check within 10 minutes.

### Perfume of First Impressions
**Potion · Common · Bronze box**

Three uses. For 1 hour, creatures that meet you for the first time start one attitude step friendlier (hostile to unfriendly, unfriendly to indifferent, and so on).

### Megaphone
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Your voice carries 600 feet. While you use it, the range of Trash Talk and Hype Man features is tripled. It is very loud.

### Liar's Tie
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box · Requires attunement**

You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks. Wisdom (Insight) checks against you have disadvantage. Magic that detects lies does not work on you.

### Silver Lozenge
**Consumable · Rare · Gold box**

As an action, put it in your mouth. Within the next minute, you can cast *suggestion* once (save DC 15) without components.

### Fake VIP Pass
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box**

Once per floor, guards, doormen and staff in non-boss areas treat you as a VIP. Very smart creatures can make a Wisdom save (DC 18) to see through it. For that day, safe room shops give you a 20% discount.

### Talk Show Mic
**Wondrous item · Very rare · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

- **Interview.** As an action, make a Charisma (Persuasion) check against a creature's Wisdom save within 30 feet. If you win, it answers three questions truthfully. Once per creature.
- **Hype the crowd.** As a bonus action, allies within 60 feet add 1d4 to their next saving throw. PB times per long rest.

### Crown of the Talk Show Host
**Wondrous item · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

Your Charisma increases by 2. Once per long rest, as an action, every creature of your choice within 60 feet that can hear you makes a Wisdom save (DC 19). On a failure, it sits down to watch you: it is charmed and incapacitated for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and the effect ends if it takes damage.

### Shadow Goggles
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

You have darkvision to 120 feet, and you can see invisible creatures and objects within 30 feet.

## Tome Items

### Mana Crystal
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

Stores up to 10 mana. During a short rest, you can move mana from your pool into it. As a bonus action, you can draw mana from it into your pool.

### Ink of Copying
**Consumable · Rare · Gold box**

One use. Copy a tome spell you know into a new spell tome. You can give or sell the copy, or read it yourself to raise the spell's rank.

## Pet Items

### Pet Treats
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

As a bonus action, feed your pet. It regains 2d4 + 2 hit points.

### Premium Pet Food
**Consumable · Uncommon · Silver box**

As a bonus action, feed your pet. It regains 4d4 + 4 hit points and has advantage on attack rolls for 1 minute.

### Squeaky Toy
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

As an action, squeak it. Each beast within 30 feet, except your pet, makes a Wisdom save (DC 12). On a failure, it spends its next turn moving toward the toy.

### Pet Barding
**Armor · Uncommon (+1) / Rare (+2) / Very rare (+3)**

Armor for your pet. Its AC increases by the barding's bonus. Barding resizes to fit when your pet grows.

### Pet Collar of Returning
**Wondrous item · Common · Bronze box**

Once per long rest, as a bonus action, your pet teleports to a space next to you, if it is within 1 mile.

### Pet Carrier
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

Your willing pet can be stored in your Crawler Inventory. It uses one slot. While stored, it does not age, heal or take damage. Releasing it takes no action.

### Leash of Command
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box**

You can command your pet without using an action. Your pet follows complex commands, not only simple ones.

### Pet Egg
**Wondrous item · Common / Uncommon / Rare / Very rare**

It hatches after your next long rest. Roll on its rarity's hatch table in the *Pets* handbook: CR 0–1/4 (common), 1/2–1 (uncommon), 2–3 (rare), or 4–6 (very rare). The hatchling is your pet, even if its CR is higher than you could normally keep.

### Pet Revival Stone
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

Touch it to the body of your pet that died within the last 10 days. The pet returns to life with 1 hit point.

### Pet Evolution Crystal
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box**

Your pet gains one evolution from the Evolutions table in the *Pets* handbook (roll a d12 or choose with the GM).

## Divine Items

### Blessed Bandage
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

As an action, apply it to a creature. It regains 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier hit points, and one disease on it ends.

### Shrine Candle
**Consumable · Uncommon · Silver box**

Light it at the start of a short rest. Each creature that rests in its light regains an extra 2d8 hit points and one spent Hit Die.

### Prayer Card
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

A card for a random god. Once, add 1d4 to one roll. The god notices you used it.

### Divine Favor Token
**Consumable · Rare · Gold box**

One use. After you or an ally within 30 feet makes a d20 roll, you can make them reroll it. They use the new roll.

### Halo of Light
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

You shed bright light for 20 feet and dim light for 20 feet more (you can turn it off). Undead and fiends in your bright light have disadvantage on attacks against you. Once per long rest, you can cast *daylight* or *cure wounds* (at 3rd level) from it.

### Angel Feather
**Wondrous item · Very rare · Legendary box**

One use. If you die while carrying it, it burns away and you return to life 1 minute later with half your hit points, if your body still exists. Or, touch it to a creature that died within the last day, as *raise dead*.

### Relic Shard
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

A piece of a broken holy object. Sell it for 500 gp, or trade **5 shards** at a shrine for a Divine Favor Token. A crawler who collects **50 shards** can forge them into one Celestial relic (GM's choice).

### Sponsor's Attention
**Event · Very rare · Gold box**

A god (GM's choice) has noticed you. Once, as an action, call out to the god. The GM decides how it helps, at about the power of a 5th-level spell. The god may offer you a sponsorship later.

## Fan Items

### Fan Mail
**Letter · Common · Bronze box**

A letter from a viewer. It holds a real tip: the GM reveals one useful secret about the current floor (a trap, a shortcut, a boss's weakness). One letter in six is only creepy.

### Foam Finger
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Silver box**

It says #1. While you wear it, as a bonus action, point at an ally within 60 feet. It has advantage on its next attack roll. PB times per long rest.

### Fan-Made Weapon
**Weapon · Uncommon (+1) / Rare (+2) / Very rare (+3)**

A magic weapon of a random type, with a bonus to attack and damage rolls. It is covered in fan art and has a terrible name, chosen by the GM.

### Celebrity Status
**Event · Rare · Gold box**

Permanent. Non-hostile NPCs recognize you. You have advantage on Persuasion checks with them, and safe room shops give you 10% off. Enemies with a grudge also recognize you.

### A Single Sock
**Troll prize · Bronze box**

One sock. Slightly damp. It has no use, unless the GM decides it does.

### Participation Trophy
**Troll prize · Silver box**

It weighs 10 pounds and cannot be stored in your inventory. If you throw it away, it returns to you at dawn. You can leave it in a safe room for good.

### Signed Photo of the System
**Troll prize · Bronze box**

"To my favorite crawler!" Every crawler who gets one gets the same photo.

### Rubber Chicken of Summoning
**Troll prize · Silver box**

Squeeze it to summon one ordinary chicken. The chicken hates you.

### Mystery Meat
**Troll prize · Bronze box**

Eat it and roll a d6: 1–2, you are poisoned for 1 hour; 3–5, nothing; 6, you regain 2d4 + 2 hit points.

### Bobblehead of Yourself
**Troll prize · Gold box**

Once per floor, ask it a yes-or-no question. It nods or shakes its head. It is right half the time.

### Golden Toilet
**Troll prize · Legendary box**

A toilet made of solid gold. It weighs 300 pounds and fills 10 inventory slots. A buyer would pay 5,000 gp for it, but most shops refuse to take it. Using it gives your Audience Meter 2 points, once.

## Spicy Items

### Chocolate Box
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

Six chocolates. Eat one as a bonus action to gain 1d4 + 1 temporary hit points, or give one to a creature to gain advantage on your next Charisma check with it.

### Body Glitter
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

For 1 hour, you have advantage on Charisma (Performance) and Charisma (Persuasion) checks against creatures that can see you. You cannot hide. You sparkle.

### Scented Candle
**Consumable · Common · Bronze box**

A short rest in its light takes only 10 minutes.

### Smoldering Gaze Potion
**Potion · Uncommon · Silver box**

For 1 minute, you can use an action to look at a creature within 30 feet. It must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 13) or be charmed by you until the end of your next turn.

### Fuzzy Handcuffs
**Gear · Uncommon / Rare (+1)**

Manacles in pink fur. Escaping them needs a DC 20 check (DC 25 for the +1 version, which also stops teleportation). You can put them on a restrained, incapacitated or willing creature.

### Love Letter Grenade
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

Throw it like any explosive (see `explosives`). It fills a 15-foot radius with pink smoke. Each creature in it makes a Wisdom save (DC 14). On a failure, it is charmed by the nearest other creature until the end of its next turn, and it does not attack anyone.

### Silk Robe of Distraction
**Armor · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

Light armor, AC 12 + Dex modifier. The first time a creature attacks you each turn, it must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 15) or have disadvantage on that attack.

### Heartbreaker
**Weapon · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

A pink +2 rapier or whip (your choice when found). On a hit, the target must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 15) or be charmed by you until the end of its next turn.

### Cupid's Pistol
**Firearm · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

A +3 flintlock pistol that never needs reloading and never misfires. On a hit, the target must succeed on a Wisdom save (DC 18) or be charmed by a creature you choose for 1 hour, or until that creature harms it.

### Novelty Underwear
**Troll prize · Bronze box**

Printed with your own face. Wearing it in public gives your Audience Meter 1 point, once.

## Murder Items

### Bloodstained Knife
**Weapon · Common / Uncommon (+1) / Rare (+2) / Very rare (+3)**

A dagger that never rusts and is never clean. It scores a critical hit on a 19 or 20 against creatures that cannot see you. Magic versions add their bonus to attack and damage rolls.

### Shovel of Quick Burial
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Dig a grave in 1 minute. Bodies buried with it do not show on Inspect, and the System's tracking cannot find them. The System still knows.

### Hitman's Suit
**Armor · Uncommon · Silver box**

A sharp suit. It counts as light armor, AC 12 + Dex modifier. You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks to seem harmless. Blood never stains it.

### Mask of Anonymity
**Wondrous item · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

While you wear it, Inspect shows you as *Unknown Crawler*, and hides your name and your Player Killer mark. Divination magic cannot identify you.

### Bounty Contract
**Document · Uncommon · Silver box**

Names a random hostile NPC or mob leader on this floor. If you kill it before the floor ends, you get a Silver Adventurer Box.

### Reaper's Ledger Page
**Document · Legendary · Legendary box**

One use. Write the name of a creature you have Inspected. At the next midnight (dungeon time), it must succeed on a Constitution save (DC 20) or drop to 0 hit points. A boss takes 10d10 necrotic damage instead.

### Guilty Conscience
**Troll prize · Bronze box**

A voice whispers your victim's name. Until you finish a long rest, you have disadvantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks.

## Boss Items

### Boss Trophy
**Trophy · Varies · Boss box**

A part of the boss: a horn, a crown, a tooth. You can sell it for **100 gp × the boss's CR**, or keep it. A kept trophy counts as rare crafting material for Tinkerer and Alchemy. Showing it off gives your Audience Meter 1 point, once.

### Boss Core Fragment
**Wondrous item · Legendary · Legendary box · Requires attunement**

Once per long rest, when you fail a saving throw, you can succeed instead.
