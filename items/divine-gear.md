# Divine Gear

> *"Holy relics! Blessed weapons! Gear with terms and conditions printed on the inside of the strap!"*

Items made by the gods, for the people who pray to them (or against them). They are part of the optional Gods module (see `gods`).

**Getting divine gear.**

- **Temples** sell their own god's gear, for gold or for Favor (spending Favor lowers your Favor, and can drop your rank).
- **Rank rewards.** When a worshipper first reaches Zealot, their god sends one Uncommon or Rare item of its own gear. At Chosen, one Very Rare item.
- **Loot.** Any box can hold divine gear instead of a magic item of the same rarity, at the GM's choice. Gear taken from an apostate's body is a classic.

| Rarity | Temple price | Or Favor | Rank needed to buy |
|--------|--------------|----------|--------------------|
| Common | 50 gp | 1 | Initiate |
| Uncommon | 300 gp | 3 | Initiate |
| Rare | 1,500 gp | 8 | Devotee |
| Very Rare | 8,000 gp | 15 | Zealot |

**Reading an entry.** The metadata line gives the item type, its rarity, and which god it belongs to. **Faithful only** means the item works only while you worship that god with fewer than 3 Lapses. An apostate's divine gear stops working.

## Gear of Any God

### Holy Symbol
**Wondrous item · Common · Any god**

An amulet, emblem or reliquary with a god's symbol (a mushroom skull, a little torch, a gold coin…). It is a spellcasting focus for spells your god's Boons gave you. Once per floor, while holding it, you can ask your god one yes-or-no question as if you were at a shrine.

### Indulgence Coupon
**Consumable · Uncommon · Any god**

A glossy coupon: *"ONE (1) FORGIVENESS. Not valid with other offers."* Tear it up to remove 1 Lapse at once, whichever god you worship. Every god accepts them. Every god resents them.

### Gauntlet of the Grudging Peace
**Wondrous item (gauntlet) · Rare · Sworn to one god · Requires attunement**

A heavy iron gauntlet engraved with one god's symbol, made for keeping the peace between the faithful and everyone else, whether everyone else likes it or not.

- **Bonus.** While you wear it, you have +1 AC, and your unarmed strikes with this hand deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage and count as magical. They deal an extra 2d6 force damage to apostates of the sworn god.
- **The Peace.** You can't attack a worshipper of the sworn god, or target one with a harmful spell or effect. If you try, the attack misses or the spell fizzles (mana spent), and you take 2d6 psychic damage as the gauntlet squeezes.
- **Curse.** Once attuned, you can't take the gauntlet off. Only a worshipper of the sworn god can remove it, and only if they want to. You don't have to worship the sworn god to wear it, which is the point.

> *"Note: this item will not stop the sworn god's worshippers from punching YOU."*

### Prayer Beads of the Fence-Sitter
**Wondrous item · Rare · Any two gods · Requires attunement**

Two strings of beads knotted together, each with a different god's symbol. While attuned, you can secretly worship two major gods at once (keep two Favor and Lapse tracks). Each god's Rite and Offering must be done separately.

- **Found out.** If either god, one of its minor deities or its Chosen worshipper Inspects you, both gods know. You gain 2 Lapses with each.
- The beads snap if the two gods are rivals.

### Apostate's Salve
**Potion · Very Rare · Any god**

A jar of grey ointment that smells faintly of a dozen temples. Rubbing it on ends one lasting effect of an Apostate's Smite from the rank of Zealot or lower. Against a Chosen's permanent smite, it only suppresses the effect until the end of the floor.

## Mother Moldra

### Sporemother's Veil
**Wondrous item (veil) · Rare · Moldra · Faithful only · Requires attunement**

A veil of fine white mycelium. You are immune to poison damage and the Poisoned condition. As an action, you can breathe spores in a 15-foot cone: each creature there makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or has the Poisoned condition for 1 minute (save at the end of each of its turns). Once per long rest.

### Compost Cleaver
**Weapon (greataxe) · Very Rare · Moldra · Requires attunement**

A rusted cleaver with fungus in the grain. +2 to attack and damage rolls. A hit deals an extra 2d6 necrotic damage. When it kills a creature, a mushroom sprouts from the body: you or an ally can eat it as a bonus action to regain 2d8 hit points (it rots after 1 minute).

## Embrix the Unquenched

### Torch of the Unquenched
**Wondrous item (torch) · Uncommon · Embrix**

A torch that never goes out (not even underwater, which upsets fish). As a weapon it is a club that deals an extra 1d6 fire damage. A worshipper of Embrix holding it casts *Torch* at Rank III or their own rank, whichever is higher.

### Ember Halo
**Wondrous item (circlet) · Very Rare · Embrix · Faithful only · Requires attunement**

A ring of floating embers that hovers over your head. You have resistance to fire damage. As a bonus action, three times per long rest, you can set your weapons alight for 1 minute: your weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 fire damage, and you shed bright light in a 20-foot radius.

---

## Saint Payola

### Tithe Purse
**Wondrous item (purse) · Uncommon · Saint Payola · Faithful only**

A purse with a gold coin stitched on it. When you take the stairs down, it automatically takes 10% of the gold you gained on the floor, and that counts as your Offering. It also holds 500 gp that doesn't take an inventory slot.

### Contract Quill
**Wondrous item · Rare · Saint Payola · Requires attunement**

A gold-nibbed quill that writes in very small print. Once per floor, you can write a contract and have a creature sign it: the creature is bound as if by *Terms of Service* (Rank I), no save if it signed willingly. If you break your side of the deal, you take 5d10 psychic damage.

---

## Thrumm

### Grudge Ledger
**Wondrous item (book) · Uncommon · Thrumm**

A heavy book with a brass lock. Write a creature's name in it to put it on your Grudge List. When a creature named in it comes within 300 feet of you, its name glows and the book hums.

### Stormhorn
**Wondrous item (horn) · Rare · Thrumm · Requires attunement**

A horn carved from a storm beast's antler. As an action, blow it: each creature of your choice within 30 feet makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. *Failure:* 6d8 thunder damage and the Deafened condition for 1 minute. *Success:* half damage only. Once per long rest (twice for a worshipper of Thrumm).

## Nyx-Ra

### Hushcloak
**Wondrous item (cloak) · Rare · Nyx-Ra · Requires attunement**

A black cloak that swallows sound. You make no sound when you move. While you are in dim light or darkness, you can take the Hide action as a bonus action, and a worshipper of Nyx-Ra has advantage on the Stealth check.

### Bone of Remembering
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Nyx-Ra**

A small finger bone tied with black thread. Once per day, touch it to a corpse to cast *Exit Interview* at Rank I without mana. A worshipper of Nyx-Ra casts it at Rank III.

---

## The Brass Warden

### Warden's Key
**Wondrous item (key) · Rare · Brass Warden · Faithful only · Requires attunement**

A brass key as long as your forearm. Once per turn, you can touch a door, gate or lock and lock or unlock it (as *Open Sesame* and *Deadbolt* at Rank I), without mana and silently. A door you lock with it can't be opened by anyone but you for 1 hour, except by a god.

### Bailiff's Shackles
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Brass Warden**

Brass manacles. A creature shackled in them (it must be Grappled, Incapacitated or willing) is Restrained, can't teleport or change shape, and needs a DC 20 Strength or Dexterity check to escape.

## Wobblegob

### Rubber Chicken of Smiting
**Weapon (club) · Rare · Wobblegob · Requires attunement**

A rubber chicken that squeaks. +1 to attack and damage rolls, and it deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage plus 2d8 thunder damage. When you roll a 20 on an attack with it, also roll on Wobblegob's Gag table (see `pantheon`).

> *"Somewhere, a god is laughing. It's this one. It's the chicken."*

### Whoopee Aegis
**Armor (shield) · Rare · Wobblegob · Requires attunement**

A +1 shield with a deflated cushion on the front. When a creature hits you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to make the shield blow a loud raspberry: the attacker has disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn (it is too embarrassed). Three times per long rest.

## Hearthmother Tuul

### Ladle of Plenty
**Wondrous item · Uncommon · Hearthmother Tuul**

A wooden ladle, warm to the touch. Once per day, stir any pot of water with it to make a hot meal for ten. Each creature that eats it regains 1d8 hit points, and it counts as a shared meal for Tuul's Rite.

### Hearthstone Amulet
**Wondrous item (amulet) · Rare · Hearthmother Tuul · Faithful only · Requires attunement**

A smooth warm stone on a cord. Once per floor, as an action, you teleport to the last safe room you rested in on this floor. You can bring one willing creature you are touching.
