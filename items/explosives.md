# Explosives

> *"Reminder: the System is not liable for lost limbs, lost teammates, or lost floors. Please blow up responsibly."*

Explosives are the great equalizer of the World Dungeon. Anyone can throw a grenade. A crawler with the **Demolitionist** and **Bomb Surgeon** Crawler Skills can level a city block. Explosives come mostly from **Demolitionist Boxes**, safe room shops, and the Bomb Maker feature of Demolitionist.

---

## Explosive Rules

### Using an Explosive

- **Throw.** *Attack.* Throwing an explosive replaces one attack of your Attack action, so Extra Attack lets you throw more than one per turn. Choose a point within range: **30/90 feet**. At long range, the explosive lands 1d4 × 5 feet from the point, in a random direction (roll a d8). You do not need proficiency to throw an explosive. Pitcher's Arm doubles the range.
- **Set.** *Action.* Place an explosive within your reach and choose its trigger (below). Setting takes care, so it uses your whole action.
- **Launch.** A grenade launcher or rocket launcher fires an explosive much further. Each shot replaces one attack, like a throw (see `firearms`).
- **Detonation.** Each creature in the area makes the listed saving throw. On a failure it takes the full effect, and on a success it takes half damage and no other effect, unless the entry says otherwise.

### Explosive Properties

- **Save DC.** Each explosive has its own DC. The Demolitionist Crawler Skill increases it.
- **Objects and structures.** Explosives deal **double damage** to objects and structures.
- **Friendly fire.** An explosion hits everything in its area: you, your allies and your pet included.
- **Dud.** Explosives marked *Dud* fail to go off when they detonate if you roll a 1 on a d20. A dud can be recovered and used again.
- **Loud.** Every explosion can be heard up to 300 feet away, or further for big ones. Mobs come to look.

### Triggers

| Trigger | How it works |
|---------|--------------|
| **Impact** | Goes off when it lands. This is the default for thrown explosives. |
| **Timed** | Goes off at the start or end of a turn you choose, up to 1 minute later. Needs a Fuse Kit or the Demolitionist Crawler Skill. |
| **Remote** | Goes off when you use a Detonator as a bonus action, from up to 1 mile away on the same floor. |
| **Proximity** | Goes off when a Small or larger creature moves within 5 feet. A creature notices it with a Wisdom (Perception) check against the listed DC. |

### Safety

- Explosives in your **Crawler Inventory** cannot go off.
- If you are holding or wearing explosives (in your hand, or a Bomb Bandolier) and you fail a save against fire damage with a roll of 1, one of them goes off.
- To disarm a set explosive, take an action and make an Intelligence check with thieves' tools against its save DC. On a failure by 5 or more, it goes off.

## Explosives

### Firecracker Bundle
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 10-foot radius · **Save:** Constitution DC 10
- **Effect:** No damage. On a failed save, a creature is deafened until the end of its next turn, and has disadvantage on its next attack roll.
- Very loud. Every creature within 300 feet hears it. Good for distractions.

### Smoke Bomb
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 20-foot radius · **Save:** none
- **Effect:** The area is heavily obscured for 1 minute, or until a strong wind disperses it.

### Molotov
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 5-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 12 · **Damage:** 2d6 fire
- The ground in the area burns for 1 minute. A creature that enters the area or starts its turn there takes 1d6 fire damage. *Dud.*

### Pipe Bomb
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 10-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 12 · **Damage:** 3d6 piercing
- The classic dungeon-made bomb. Bomb Maker (Demolitionist) produces these. *Dud.*

### Stick of Dynamite
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 5-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 12 · **Damage:** 3d6 bludgeoning
- Lighting it is part of the throw, if you have a flame. It has a built-in timed fuse of up to 1 round. *Dud.*

### Flashbang
**Explosive · Common · Bronze box**

- **Area:** 15-foot radius · **Save:** Constitution DC 13
- **Effect:** No damage. On a failed save, a creature is blinded and deafened until the end of your next turn.

### Frag Grenade
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 20-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 13 · **Damage:** 5d6 piercing
- The standard military grenade. Pull the pin, throw, duck.

### Sticky Bomb
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** target + 10-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 13 (others only) · **Damage:** 4d6 fire
- Make a ranged attack against a creature (you are proficient). On a hit, the bomb sticks to it. It explodes at the start of the target's next turn. The target takes the full damage with no save. Other creatures within 10 feet make the save.
- A creature can pull the bomb off as an action with a DC 13 Strength check.

### Concussion Grenade
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 15-foot radius · **Save:** Constitution DC 13 · **Damage:** 3d6 thunder
- On a failed save, a creature is also pushed 10 feet away from the center and knocked prone.

### Frost Grenade
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 15-foot radius · **Save:** Constitution DC 13 · **Damage:** 3d6 cold
- On a failed save, a creature's speed is halved until the end of its next turn. The area becomes difficult terrain (ice) for 1 minute.

### Acid Bomb
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 10-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 13 · **Damage:** 4d4 acid
- On a failed save, a creature takes another 2d4 acid damage at the end of its next turn. Nonmagical armor it wears takes a permanent −1 penalty to AC until repaired.

### Explosive Arrow
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 10-foot radius around the target · **Save:** Dexterity DC 13 (others only) · **Damage:** 2d6 fire
- Fire it from a bow as normal ammunition. On a hit, the target takes the arrow's damage plus 2d6 fire damage with no save. On a miss, it explodes where it lands.

### Bundle of Dynamite
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 20-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 14 · **Damage:** 6d6 bludgeoning
- Five sticks taped together. Built-in timed fuse of up to 1 round. *Dud.*

### Land Mine
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 10-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 14 · **Damage:** 4d6 piercing
- **Trigger:** Proximity (Perception DC 15 to notice). Arming it takes an action.

### Remote Charge
**Explosive · Uncommon · Silver box**

- **Area:** 15-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 14 · **Damage:** 5d6 fire
- **Trigger:** Remote (needs a Detonator). Deals **four times** damage to objects and structures, instead of double.

### Cluster Bomb
**Explosive · Rare · Gold box**

- **Area:** four 10-foot radii · **Save:** Dexterity DC 15 · **Damage:** 3d6 fire each
- When it lands, it splits into four bomblets. Place each one at a point within 30 feet of where it landed. A creature in more than one area makes a save for each one.

### Thermite Charge
**Explosive · Rare · Gold box**

- **Area:** 5 feet · **Save:** Dexterity DC 15 · **Damage:** 6d6 fire
- **Trigger:** Set only. It burns for 1 minute and melts a hole 5 feet deep per round through stone or metal. A creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of it makes the save.

### Thunderclap Charge
**Explosive · Rare · Gold box**

- **Area:** 30-foot radius · **Save:** Constitution DC 15 · **Damage:** 6d6 thunder
- On a failed save, a creature is knocked prone and deafened for 1 minute. It can be heard for miles.

### Gravity Grenade
**Explosive · Rare · Gold box**

- **Area:** 20-foot radius · **Save:** Strength DC 15 · **Damage:** 4d10 force
- On a failed save, a creature is pulled to the center and restrained until the end of your next turn.

### Bunker Buster
**Explosive · Very rare · Legendary box**

- **Area:** 30-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 16 · **Damage:** 8d6 fire + 8d6 bludgeoning
- **Trigger:** Set or launch only (too heavy to throw). Nonmagical structures in the area are destroyed. Boss arenas and safe rooms are immune.

### Banshee Bomb
**Explosive · Very rare · Legendary box**

- **Area:** 30-foot radius · **Save:** Wisdom DC 17 · **Damage:** 6d10 psychic
- On a failed save, a creature is frightened for 1 minute. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

### Hellfire Barrel
**Explosive · Very rare · Legendary box**

- **Area:** 40-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 17 · **Damage:** 10d6 fire + 5d6 necrotic
- **Trigger:** Set only (100 lb). The area burns for 1 minute. A creature that enters it or starts its turn there takes 3d6 fire damage.

### Mini Sun
**Explosive · Legendary · Legendary box**

- **Area:** 60-foot radius · **Save:** Dexterity DC 19 · **Damage:** 12d6 fire + 12d6 radiant
- On a failed save, a creature is also blinded for 1 minute. The area stays in bright light for 1 hour, and undead there take 2d6 radiant damage at the start of each of their turns.

## Explosive Gear

### Fuse Kit
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

Ten uses. When you set an explosive, you can spend a use to give it a timed trigger.

### Detonator
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

As a bonus action, set off up to 10 remote charges linked to it, from up to 1 mile away on the same floor.

### Blast Goggles
**Gear · Common · Bronze box**

While you wear them, you have advantage on saves against being blinded by explosions and bright light.

### Blast Shield
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

A shield (+2 AC). When you make a Dexterity save against an explosion, you take no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

### Bomb Bandolier
**Gear · Uncommon · Silver box**

Holds up to 8 explosives outside your inventory. You can draw an explosive from it with no action. Once per turn, when you take the Attack action and throw at least one explosive, you can throw one common explosive from the bandolier as a bonus action.

### Demolition Gloves
**Gear · Rare · Gold box · Requires attunement**

The save DC of explosives you throw or set increases by 1. You take no damage from your own explosives.

### Satchel of Endless Pipe Bombs
**Gear · Very rare · Legendary box**

At dawn, the satchel holds 1d4 + 1 Pipe Bombs. Pipe Bombs from the satchel never dud. Bombs left in it at dawn vanish.
