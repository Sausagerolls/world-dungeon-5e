# Cantrips

> *"Free magic! The System is contractually obliged to remind you that 'free' does not mean 'safe'."*

Cantrips cost no mana. Your **character level** sets a cantrip's minimum rank: Rank II at 5th level, Rank III at 11th and Rank IV at 17th. Duplicate tomes and class Rank Ups can push it higher, up to the usual rank gates (see *Spell Ranks* in `tome-magic`). Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Fire Flick
**Cantrip · Evocation · Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Fire Bolt, Produce Flame

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You flick a marble of fire at a creature or object. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 fire damage. A flammable object hit by this spell starts burning if no one is wearing or carrying it.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 1d10 fire damage. |
| II | 2d10 fire damage. |
| III | 3d10 fire damage. You can hold the flame in your palm as a torch between casts (bright light 20 feet). |
| IV | 4d10 fire damage. |
| V | 4d10 fire damage. On a hit, the target also burns for 1d10 fire damage at the start of its next turn. |

> *"Please do not flick fire at the sponsors' banners."*

| III | 3d8 cold damage, −15 feet speed. |
| IV | 4d8 cold damage. |
| V | 4d8 cold damage. A target whose speed drops to 0 this way is frozen to the floor: it is Restrained until the start of your next turn. |

### Torch
**Cantrip · Evocation · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Light, Dancing Lights

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Touch · *Components:* V · *Duration:* 1 hour

You touch an object no larger than 10 feet in any dimension. It sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for a further 20 feet. You can end the light as a bonus action.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One light on an object. |
| II | The light can float instead, following 5 feet from you. |
| III | Up to three lights at once, each on an object or floating within 60 feet of you. You can move them as a bonus action. |
| IV | You can choose the color. Sunlight color counts as sunlight for creatures that hate it. |
| V | As a bonus action, flare one light. Each creature within 10 feet of it makes a Constitution saving throw or is Blinded until the end of its next turn. The light then goes out. |

### Grabby Hand

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 30 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* 1 minute

A floating cartoon glove appears. As a Magic action you can use it to handle an object, open a door or container, or pour something out. It can't attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 10 pounds.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One hand, 30 feet, 10 pounds. |
| II | The hand can carry 25 pounds and move 60 feet. |
| III | You control the hand as a bonus action instead of an action. |
| IV | The hand can make Sleight of Hand checks for you, using your bonus. |
| V | The hand can slap: a creature within 5 feet of it makes a Dexterity saving throw or takes 2d8 bludgeoning damage (once per turn). |

### Party Trick
**Cantrip · Transmutation · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Prestidigitation, Thaumaturgy, Druidcraft

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 30 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Up to 1 hour

A small, harmless bit of magic for the cameras. Choose one: clean or soil a small object; chill, warm or flavor food; make a small mark or symbol appear for an hour; make your voice boom three times louder; make flames flicker or change color; make a harmless sensory effect (a smell, a sound, a puff of wind, a flower blooming); or make the ground tremble harmlessly.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One effect at a time. |
| II | Up to two effects at a time. |
| III | Up to three effects at a time. You have advantage on Performance checks you make while one is active. |
| IV | You can change the weather for 1 minute in a 30-foot radius (a light drizzle, a breeze, a small spotlight). |
| V | Once per long rest, a party trick earns a cheap viewer reaction. Make a Performance check with advantage to impress a crowd or distract a mob. |

### Cardboard Cutout
**Cantrip · Illusion · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Minor Illusion

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 30 feet · *Components:* S · *Duration:* 1 minute

You make a sound or a still image of an object (no larger than a 5-foot cube) in range. A creature that studies it can see through it with an Investigation check against your spell save DC. Physical contact reveals an image as fake.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One sound or one image. |
| II | A sound and an image at once, in the same place. |
| III | The image can be up to a 10-foot cube and can move up to 30 feet each turn as you direct it (no action). |
| IV | Creatures have disadvantage on the check to see through it. |
| V | The illusion lasts 1 hour, and touching it no longer reveals it unless the creature also succeeds on the Investigation check. |

### Weapon Hype

| Rank | Effect |

| III | The extra damage becomes 2d6 force. Damage die at least d10. |
| IV | The extra damage becomes 3d6 force. |
| V | The extra damage becomes 3d6 force. The weapon glows with your sponsor's logo, and its damage die becomes a d12. |
