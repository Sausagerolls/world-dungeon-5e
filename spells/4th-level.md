# 4th-Level Spells

> *"Fourth level. You're officially a threat. The System has updated your file accordingly."*

Every 4th-level spell costs **6 mana** at every rank. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Creature Feature
**4th level · Transmutation · Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Polymorph

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 hour

You turn a creature into a beast. An unwilling target makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it becomes a beast of your choice with a CR no higher than its CR (or level), using the beast's stat block, and gains temporary hit points equal to the beast's hit points. It keeps its personality. It reverts when the temporary hit points run out or the spell ends.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One creature. |
| II | You can choose a beast up to CR 1 higher for a willing target. |
| III | Two creatures. |
| IV | Monstrosities count as beasts for this spell. |
| V | A willing target can switch between its forms as a bonus action for the duration. |

> *"Congratulations! You are now a frog. The audience thanks you for your service."*

### Ban Hammer
**4th level · Abjuration · Cleric, Paladin, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Banishment

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

A giant glowing gavel comes down. A creature makes a Charisma saving throw. On a failure, it is banished to a harmless waiting room and is Incapacitated there until the spell ends. If the spell lasts the full minute and the creature is not from this plane, it doesn't come back.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One creature. |
| II | Two creatures. |
| III | Three creatures. A creature that succeeds still takes 4d8 force damage. |
| IV | Four creatures. |
| V | Five creatures. The target has disadvantage on the save if it attacked you last turn. |

> *"You have been banned. Reason: being a jerk. Appeals: no."*

### Shortcut
**4th level · Conjuration · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Dimension Door

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 500 feet · *Components:* V · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You teleport to a spot within range that you can see, picture or describe ("200 feet straight down"). You can bring one willing creature you touch. If you would arrive in an occupied space, you take 4d6 force damage and fail.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 500 feet, one passenger. |
| II | Two passengers. |
| III | 1,000 feet. |
| IV | Three passengers. |
| V | You can teleport through the floor to a stairwell you have seen on this floor, any distance. |

### Firewall
**4th level · Evocation · Druid, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Wall of Fire

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

You make a wall of fire up to 60 feet long, 20 feet high and 1 foot thick, or a ring 20 feet across. Each creature in its area when it appears makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 5d8 fire damage on a failure or half as much on a success. One side of the wall (your choice) deals 5d8 fire damage to a creature that ends its turn within 10 feet of it or enters the wall.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 5d8 fire damage. |
| II | 6d8 fire damage. |
| III | 7d8 fire damage. You can let creatures you choose pass through unharmed. |
| IV | 8d8 fire damage. |
| V | 9d8 fire damage. 120 feet long. |

| III | 4d10 + 4d6. Creatures that fail fall Prone. |

| V | 6d10 + 4d6. 40-foot radius. |

### Tentacle Party
**4th level · Conjuration · Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Evard's Black Tentacles, Black Tentacles

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 90 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

Rubbery black tentacles fill a 20-foot square. It is Difficult Terrain. A creature that enters it for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there makes a Strength saving throw. On a failure, it takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage and is Restrained until the spell ends. A restrained creature takes the damage at the start of each of its turns, and can use an action to make a Strength or Dexterity check against your spell save DC to escape.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 3d6 bludgeoning. |
| II | 4d6 bludgeoning. |
| III | 5d6 bludgeoning. Restrained creatures can't speak (a tentacle is in the way). |
| IV | 6d6 bludgeoning. |
| V | 7d6 bludgeoning. 30-foot square. |

> *"The less said about this one, the better. The ratings, however, were spectacular."*

### Glitch
**4th level · Enchantment · Bard, Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Confusion

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 90 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

Creatures in a 10-foot-radius sphere start glitching. Each makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it can't take reactions and rolls a d10 at the start of each of its turns: 1, it moves in a random direction; 2–6, it does nothing; 7–8, it attacks a random creature in reach; 9–10, it acts normally. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 10-foot radius. |
| II | 15-foot radius. |
| III | 20-foot radius. You choose which creatures are affected. |
| IV | A glitching creature takes 2d6 psychic damage when it does nothing. |
| V | 30-foot radius. |

| III | 30 damage, after 100. It also shoves: a creature that fails is pushed 10 feet. |
| IV | Vanishes after 120. |
| V | 40 damage, after 160. |

> *"Name's not on the list."*

Everyone, conga! Any number of creatures you choose that can hear you make Wisdom saving throws. On a failure, as a bonus action each turn, you can make an affected creature use its movement to dance in a direction you choose, without safety concerns.

| Rank | Effect |

### Crafting Table
**4th level · Transmutation · Wizard**

*Replaces:* Fabricate

*Casting time:* 10 minutes · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You turn raw materials into finished goods of the same material: a bridge from planks, rope from hemp, armor from scrap. Up to a 10-foot cube (or a 5-foot cube of metal or stone). You can't make magic items, and fine items need the right tool proficiency. Treat it as a Workbench crafting session in the field (see `crafting`).

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 10-foot cube, nonmagical goods. |
| II | Casting time 1 action for objects you could make in one hour. |
| III | Count as having a Workbench for one crafting check. |
| IV | 20-foot cube. |
| V | You can craft a common magic item if you have its recipe and materials. |

### Ask the System
**4th level · Divination · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Divination, Augury, Commune (Rank II), Contact Other Plane (Rank II), Legend Lore (Rank II), Commune with Nature (Rank II)

*Casting time:* 1 action (ritual) · *Range:* Self · *Components:* V, S, M (25 gp of incense, consumed) · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You open a support ticket with the System and ask one question about a goal, event or activity within the next 7 days. The GM answers truthfully, in a short phrase, rhyme or omen. The System is helpful, technically. Each casting after the first before a long rest has a cumulative 25% chance of an unhelpful answer.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One question. |
| II | Three yes-or-no questions instead, or learn the legends of an item, place or person. |
| III | You learn the layout of the surrounding 3 miles (terrain, water, settlements, safe rooms). |
| IV | Five yes-or-no questions. |
| V | Once per floor, the System's AI answers one question about the floor's boss truthfully. It's grumpy about it. |

> *"Your ticket has been received. Estimated response time: whenever. Thank you for crawling."*
