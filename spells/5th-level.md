# 5th-Level Spells

> *"Fifth-level spells. The highest a paladin or ranger will ever go. Don't worry, guys. You've got swords."*

Every 5th-level spell costs **7 mana** at every rank. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Brain Freeze
**5th level · Evocation · Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Cone of Cold

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Self (60-foot cone) · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

A blast of frozen air. Each creature in a 60-foot cone makes a Constitution saving throw, taking 8d8 cold damage on a failure or half as much on a success. A creature killed by it becomes a frozen statue.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 8d8 cold damage. |
| II | 9d8 cold damage. |
| III | 10d8 cold damage. Creatures that fail have their speed halved until the end of their next turn. |
| IV | 11d8 cold damage. |
| V | 12d8 cold damage. Creatures that fail can't take reactions until the end of their next turn. |

> *"Ow, ow, ow, ow. Brain freeze! The bad kind. The fatal kind."*

### Sky Laser
**5th level · Evocation · Cleric**

*Replaces:* Flame Strike

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

An orbital laser burns down in a 10-foot-radius, 40-foot-high cylinder. Each creature in it makes a Dexterity saving throw. It takes 5d6 fire and 5d6 radiant damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 5d6 fire + 5d6 radiant. |
| II | 6d6 + 6d6. |
| III | 7d6 + 7d6. Creatures that fail are Blinded until the end of their next turn. |
| IV | 8d6 + 8d6. |
| V | 9d6 + 9d6. You can move the laser 30 feet as a bonus action on your next turn and hit again for half (no mana). |

### Sprinkler System
**5th level · Abjuration · Bard, Cleric, Druid**

*Replaces:* Mass Cure Wounds

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

Glowing healing water sprays over a 30-foot-radius sphere. Up to six creatures of your choice in it regain 5d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier hit points.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 5d8 + modifier. |
| II | 6d8 + modifier. |
| III | 7d8 + modifier. It also puts out fires and ends the Poisoned condition. |
| IV | 8d8 + modifier. |
| V | 9d8 + modifier. Any number of creatures in the area. |

> *"Please do not drink from the sprinklers. Actually, go ahead."*

### Good As New
**5th level · Abjuration · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger**

*Replaces:* Greater Restoration

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Touch · *Components:* V, S, M (100 gp of diamond dust, consumed) · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You end one of these on a creature: one Exhaustion level, the Charmed or Petrified condition, a curse, a reduction to an ability score, or a reduction to its hit point maximum.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One effect. |
| II | Two effects. |
| III | All of them. Range 30 feet. |
| IV | It also regrows a lost limb over 1 minute. |
| V | No diamond dust needed. |

### Pause Screen
**5th level · Enchantment · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Hold Monster

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 90 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

You press pause on a creature. It makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is Paralyzed until the spell ends. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. Undead are immune.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One creature. |
| II | Two creatures. |
| III | Three creatures. Undead are no longer immune. |
| IV | Four creatures. |
| V | Five creatures. A target repeats the save only every other turn. |

> *"PAUSED. Press start to continue."*

### Puppet Show
**5th level · Enchantment · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Dominate Person

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

A humanoid makes a Wisdom saving throw, with advantage if you're fighting it. On a failure, it is Charmed, and you control it telepathically on the same floor. As a bonus action you can take over its whole turn. Each time it takes damage, it repeats the save.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 1 minute. |
| II | 10 minutes. |
| III | Any creature type except undead. |
| IV | 1 hour. |
| V | 8 hours. |

### Invisible Wall
**5th level · Evocation · Wizard**

*Replaces:* Wall of Force

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

The kind of invisible wall that ends every video game level. It's up to ten 10-foot panels, or a dome or sphere 10 feet across. Nothing can pass through it. It is immune to all damage, and only *disintegrate* or *factory reset* (at Rank V) removes it.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Ten panels. |
| II | Fifteen panels, or a 20-foot dome. |
| III | You can make one panel one-way. |
| IV | Twenty panels. |
| V | It no longer needs concentration. |

> *"You cannot go that way. You've seen the cutscene. You know why."*

### Stonewall
**5th level · Evocation · Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Wall of Stone

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

A wall of stone up to ten 10-foot panels, 6 inches thick, rises from the floor. Each panel has AC 15 and 30 hit points per inch of thickness. If you concentrate for the full duration, it becomes permanent.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Ten panels. |
| II | Fifteen panels. |
| III | You can shape it into stairs, battlements or a bridge, and creatures caught in it are pushed to the side you choose. |
| IV | Twenty panels. |

### Yoink
**5th level · Transmutation · Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Telekinesis

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Telekinesis. As an action on later turns, move a creature (Strength save to resist; a contested check if you prefer) or an object up to 1,000 pounds, 30 feet in any direction. You can hold a creature Restrained in the air. You can also yank an object someone is holding (contested check).

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 1,000 pounds, 30 feet. |
| II | 2,000 pounds. |
| III | You can move it as a bonus action. |
| IV | 4,000 pounds, 60 feet. |
| V | You can slam a creature you are holding into a surface: 6d6 bludgeoning damage. |

> *"Yoink!"*

### Livestream
**5th level · Divination · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Scrying

*Casting time:* 10 minutes · *Range:* Self · *Components:* V, S, M (a focus worth 1,000 gp) · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

You tune into someone's feed. A creature on the same floor makes a Wisdom saving throw, modified by how well you know it (−5 if you know it well, +5 if barely). On a failure, you see and hear it through an invisible sensor next to it.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One creature on the same floor. |
| II | You can also scry a place you have visited. |
| III | Duration 1 hour. |
| IV | You can cast spells of 3rd level or lower through the sensor (only at the target). |
| V | You can scry onto another floor. |

> *"Like and subscribe to this crawler's feed. Ignore that they're being stalked."*

### Terms of Service
**5th level · Enchantment · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Geas, Planar Binding

*Casting time:* 1 minute · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V · *Duration:* 30 days

A creature that can understand you must agree to the terms. It makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is Charmed and must follow a command you give, or do or not do something. Each day it acts against the terms, it takes 5d10 psychic damage (once a day).

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 30 days. |
| II | 60 days. |
| III | 180 days. It also works on a creature you summoned: it serves you for the duration. |
| IV | 1 year. |
| V | Until dispelled. |

> *"By continuing to exist, you agree to these Terms of Service."*

You print a nonliving object of vegetable matter, stone, crystal or metal, up to a 5-foot cube. It lasts 1 day for vegetable matter, 12 hours for stone or crystal, 1 hour for metal. Using it as a crafting material (see `crafting`) makes the crafted item vanish when it does.

| Rank | Effect |

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Self (10-foot emanation) · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 hour

Living creatures other than you can't move into the 10-foot area around you or reach through it. If you move into one, the spell ends.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|

### Tree Hopper

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Self · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

Once on each of your turns, you can step into a living plant (or a big fungus) and come out of another one within 500 feet. It costs 5 feet of movement.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|

| III | Duration 10 minutes. |
| IV | You can make a plant a door: it stays open for a round for anyone to use. |
| V | Duration 1 hour. |

### Portal
**5th level · Conjuration · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Teleportation Circle, Arcane Gate (Rank II)

*Casting time:* 1 minute · *Range:* 10 feet · *Components:* V, S, M (rare inks worth 50 gp, consumed) · *Duration:* 1 round

You draw a glowing portal that links to a place you have been on the same floor. Anyone who steps in arrives there. It stays open until the end of your next turn.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 1 round, a place you've been. |
| II | Casting time 1 action. Instead you can make two linked portals within 500 feet of each other that last for concentration, up to 10 minutes. |
| III | It stays open for 1 minute. |
| IV | You can link it to a stairwell you have found, even across the whole floor. |
| V | It stays open for 10 minutes. |

> *"Portals cannot take you to another floor. Stop asking. The stairs are RIGHT THERE."*
