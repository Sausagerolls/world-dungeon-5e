# 2nd-Level Spells

> *"Look at you, casting second-level spells. Your mother would be so proud, if she were still alive. Too soon?"*

Every 2nd-level spell costs **3 mana** at every rank. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Puddle Jumper
**2nd level · Conjuration · Bard, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Misty Step

*Casting time:* 1 bonus action · *Range:* Self · *Components:* V · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You step into the floor like it's a puddle and pop out of another one. Teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. You arrive slightly damp.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Teleport 30 feet. |
| II | Teleport 60 feet. |
| III | You can bring one willing creature you touch. It arrives next to you. |
| IV | Teleport 90 feet. You can also jump to a space you saw in the last minute, even if you can't see it now. |
| V | Bring up to two creatures. When you arrive, creatures of your choice within 5 feet get splashed: 2d6 cold damage, no save. |

> *"Puddle Jumper! Not available on floors without puddles. All floors have puddles. Don't ask what's in them."*

### Lights Out
**2nd level · Evocation · Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Somebody hit the breaker. Magical darkness fills a 15-foot-radius sphere centered on a point or an object you touch. Darkvision can't see through it, and nonmagical light can't light it.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 15-foot radius. |
| II | You can see through your own darkness. |
| III | 20-foot radius. Creatures you choose can see through it too. |
| IV | Creatures that can't see through it have disadvantage on concentration saves while inside. |
| V | 30-foot radius. Your darkness snuffs out magical light of 3rd level or lower. |

| Rank | Effect |

### Clockwork Triplicate
**2nd level · Illusion · Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Mirror Image, Blur, Mislead (Rank IV)

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* Self · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* 1 minute

Two ticking, clockwork copies of you appear in your space. Each time a creature attacks you, roll a d6 for each copy. If any die rolls a 3 or higher, the attack hits a copy instead and the copy shatters into gears. A copy has AC 10 + your Dexterity modifier. Creatures with Blindsight or Truesight aren't fooled.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Two copies. |
| II | Three copies. |
| III | A copy that shatters sprays gears: the attacker takes 1d6 slashing damage. |
| IV | As a bonus action, you can turn invisible and leave one copy standing in your place until the start of your next turn. The copy can talk and move as you direct it. |
| V | Four copies. Each copy can make a melee attack using your spell attack modifier as part of the spell's casting (1d6 force damage). |

> *"Three of you! The audience asked for many things. This was not one of them."*

### Sticky Situation
**2nd level · Conjuration · Druid, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Web

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 hour

Thick, sticky webbing fills a 20-foot cube. It is Difficult Terrain and Lightly Obscured. A creature that starts its turn there or enters it makes a Dexterity saving throw or is Restrained while it stays in the web. A restrained creature can use an action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC to break free. The web burns away in 1 round if fire touches it, dealing 2d4 fire damage to creatures in it.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 20-foot cube. |
| II | Fire no longer burns it away. |
| III | 30-foot cube. Restrained creatures can't cast spells with somatic components. |
| IV | Restrained creatures take 2d6 acid damage at the start of each of their turns. |
| V | 40-foot cube. |
