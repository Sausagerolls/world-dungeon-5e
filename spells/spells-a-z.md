# Spells A to Z

> *"Every spell the System is legally allowed to sell you, in alphabetical order. The System's lawyers insisted on alphabetical order."*

Every World Dungeon spell, from cantrips to 9th level, in alphabetical order. The tag under each name gives its level, school and the classes that can learn it. To find the spells your class can learn, see `spell-lists`.

- **Mana.** A spell always costs the mana of its own level, at every rank: cantrips free, 1st 2, 2nd 3, 3rd 5, 4th 6, 5th 7, 6th 9, 7th 10, 8th 11, 9th 13. You can cast one spell of each level from 6th to 9th per long rest (see `mana`).
- **Ranks.** Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise (see *Spell Ranks* in `tome-magic`).
- **Cantrips.** Your character level sets a cantrip's minimum rank: Rank II at 5th level, Rank III at 11th and Rank IV at 17th.
