# 3rd-Level Spells

> *"Third-level spells! This is where the fun starts. And by fun, we mean property damage."*

Every 3rd-level spell costs **5 mana** at every rank. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Kaboom
**3rd level · Evocation · Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Fireball

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 150 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

A bead of fire streaks to a point you choose and explodes. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere makes a Dexterity saving throw. It takes 8d6 fire damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. Flammable objects in the area that no one is holding start burning.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 8d6 fire damage. |
| II | 9d6 fire damage. |
| III | 10d6 fire damage. You can make a pocket in the blast: up to three creatures you choose take no damage. |
| IV | 11d6 fire damage. |
| V | 12d6 fire damage. You can delay the blast up to 1 minute. It goes off when you choose (no action) or when a creature touches the bead. |

> *"KABOOM! The single most popular spell among viewers, for nine seasons running."*

### Unsubscribe
**3rd level · Abjuration · Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Counterspell

*Casting time:* 1 reaction, when you see a creature within 60 feet casting a spell · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You hit "unsubscribe" on someone's spell. The caster makes a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the spell fails, and any mana or slot it used is wasted.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Caster saves to keep the spell. |
| II | The caster has disadvantage on the save if the spell is 3rd level or lower. |
| III | If the spell fails, you regain 2 mana. |
| IV | The caster has disadvantage on the save if the spell is 5th level or lower. |
| V | If the spell fails and it targeted only you, you can cast it back at its caster, using the caster's DC. |

> *"You have been unsubscribed. We're sorry to see you go. Not really."*

### Factory Reset
**3rd level · Abjuration · Bard, Cleric, Druid, Paladin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Dispel Magic, Remove Curse

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You turn something off and on again. Choose a creature, object or magical effect. Any spell of 3rd level or lower on it ends. For each spell of 4th level or higher, make an ability check with your spellcasting ability (DC 10 + the spell's level). On a success, it ends. You can also end one curse on a creature you touch, and break a cursed item's hold on its owner.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Ends spells of 3rd level or lower automatically. |
| II | 4th level or lower. |
| III | 5th level or lower. Add your proficiency bonus to the check for higher spells. |
| IV | 6th level or lower. |
| V | 7th level or lower. You can target every creature and object of your choice in a 20-foot radius. |

> *"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"*

### Fast Forward
**3rd level · Transmutation · Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Haste

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 30 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

A willing creature plays at double speed. Its speed doubles, it gains +2 AC, it has advantage on Dexterity saves, and it gets one extra action on each of its turns (Attack with one weapon attack only, Dash, Disengage, Hide or Use an Object). When the spell ends, the target is Incapacitated until the end of its next turn as the lag catches up.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | One creature. |
| II | The lag after the spell ends lasts only until the start of its next turn instead. |
| III | Two creatures. |
| IV | The extra Attack action allows two weapon attacks. |
| V | Three creatures, and no lag when the spell ends. |

### Buffering
**3rd level · Transmutation · Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Slow

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

Up to six creatures in a 40-foot cube start buffering. Each makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, its speed is halved, it takes −2 to AC and Dexterity saves, it can't take reactions, and on its turn it can take either an action or a bonus action, not both. It can't make more than one attack on its turn. If it casts a spell with a casting time of 1 action, roll a d20: on 11 or higher, the spell doesn't take effect until its next turn. It repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Six creatures. |
| II | Seven creatures. |
| III | Eight creatures. Affected creatures repeat the save only every other turn. |
| IV | Nine creatures. |
| V | Ten creatures. Affected creatures can't Dash. |

> *"Your opponent is experiencing technical difficulties. Please stand by."*
