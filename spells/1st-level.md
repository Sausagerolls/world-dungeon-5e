# 1st-Level Spells

> *"Your first real spells. Try not to cast them at your own feet. We say this from experience. Yours."*

Every 1st-level spell costs **2 mana** at every rank. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it unless it says otherwise.

### Magic Missile
**1st level · Evocation · Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Magic Missile

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 120 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

Glowing darts of force streak from your fingers. Each dart hits a creature of your choice that you can see within range, and deals 1d4 + 1 force damage. You can split the darts however you like. They never miss.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | Three darts, 1d4 + 1 force damage each. |
| II | Four darts. |
| III | Five darts. The darts bend around corners and full cover, as long as you know where the target is. |
| IV | Six darts. |
| V | Seven darts, 1d4 + 2 force damage each. |

> *"A classic. A favorite of cats everywhere. We can't imagine why."*

### Mystery Box
**1st level · Evocation · Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Chromatic Orb, Ice Knife

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 90 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Instantaneous

You hurl a gift-wrapped sphere of energy. Make a ranged spell attack. On a hit, the target takes 3d8 damage. Roll a d6 for the type: 1 acid, 2 cold, 3 fire, 4 lightning, 5 poison, 6 thunder. If two or more of the damage dice roll the same number, the box bounces to another creature within 30 feet of the target: make a new attack roll against it. Each creature can be hit only once per casting.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 3d8 damage, random type, bounces on doubles. |
| II | 4d8 damage. |
| III | 5d8 damage. You can reroll the type die once. |
| IV | 6d8 damage. |
| V | 7d8 damage. You choose the type instead of rolling. |

> *"What's in the box? Pain. It's always pain."*

| V | 6d8 thunder damage. You choose which creatures in the cube are affected. |

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | +5 AC until the start of your next turn. |

### Nap Time
**1st level · Enchantment · Bard, Sorcerer, Wizard**

*Replaces:* Sleep

*Casting time:* 1 action · *Range:* 60 feet · *Components:* V, S · *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

A lullaby only monsters can hear. Each creature you choose in a 5-foot-radius sphere makes a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it has the Incapacitated condition until the end of its next turn, then repeats the save. If it fails again, it has the Unconscious condition until the spell ends, it takes damage, or someone uses an action to shake it awake. Creatures that don't sleep, such as elves and undead, are immune.

| Rank | Effect |
|------|--------|
| I | 5-foot radius. |
| II | 10-foot radius. |
| III | 15-foot radius. Sleeping creatures snore loudly and have disadvantage on the first save they make after waking. |
| IV | 20-foot radius. |
| V | Creatures that succeed on the first save still have their speed halved until the end of their next turn. |

### Signature Move

|------|--------|

| II | +2d6 damage. |
| III | +3d6 damage. Saving throws against your move have disadvantage. |
| IV | +4d6 damage. |
| V | +5d6 damage. Choose two moves. |
