> **Inspect:** *"Imani "Knuckles" Okafor. Level 11 Human Crawler. 8,840 followers. Fan favourite."*

#### Traits

- **Iron Fist.** Their unarmed strikes count as magical, and they can make one extra Unarmed Strike as a bonus action.

#### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Imani makes three Unarmed Strike (taped fists) attacks.
- **Unarmed Strike (taped fists).** *Melee Attack Roll:* +10, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 9 (1d6 + 6) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Suplex.** *Strength Saving Throw:* DC 18, one creature it is grappling. *Failure:* 18 (4d8) Bludgeoning damage and the Prone condition. The grapple ends. *Success:* Half damage.

#### Bonus Actions

- **Grab.** Imani grapples one creature within 5 feet (escape DC 18).

#### Crawler

- **Race and class.** Human Crawler (no class yet)
- **Level.** 11 (floor 1)
- **Party.** Solo (for now)
- **Titles on Inspect.** Llama Puncher
- **Crawler Skills.** Iron Fist 2, Wrestler 1, Dirty Fighting 1

#### Fame

- **Followers.** 8,840
- **Audience.** Unknown
- **Player Killer.** Yes: the Player Killer mark shows on Inspect (see `loot-boxes`)

#### Gear

- **Weapons and ranks.** Unarmed Strike (taped fists) (rank I)

- **Unopened boxes.** 1 Bronze Adventurer box
- **When killed.** Their inventory spills out, and the killer gets a Murder Box (see `loot-boxes`).

#### Roleplay

- **Attitude.** Hostile
- **Personality.** A former amateur wrestler who has realised she is good at this, and that the audience loves it. Loud, showy, and always looking for the camera.
- **Wants.** Ratings. She hunts other crawlers because the audience pays more attention to a Player Killer.
- **Will trade.** Nothing. She will offer a "fair fight" instead: one on one, in a room of her choosing.

#### Tactics

Challenges the strongest-looking crawler to a one-on-one fight, then grapples and suplexes them in front of everyone. Fights dirty if she starts losing. Runs if bloodied and alone.

#### Audience

Beating her in her own "fair fight": +2 Audience. Ganging up on her: the audience boos (no Audience).

#### Voice

"Ladies and gentlemen, in the left corner…"
