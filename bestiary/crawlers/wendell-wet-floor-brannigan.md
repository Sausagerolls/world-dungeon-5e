#### Traits

- **Bomb Surgeon.** Wendell has advantage on saving throws against explosives, and takes no damage from them on a successful save.

#### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Wendell makes two Crowbar attacks.
- **Crowbar.** *Melee Attack Roll:* +9, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage.
- **Molotov (3/Day).** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 5-foot-radius Sphere within 90 feet. *Failure:* 7 (2d6) Fire damage. *Success:* Half damage.
- **Pipe Bomb.** *Dexterity Saving Throw:* DC 17, each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere within 60 feet. *Failure:* 10 (3d6) Piercing damage. *Success:* Half damage.

#### Bonus Actions

- **Cook the Fuse.** Wendell plants a Proximity or Remote charge in an empty space within 5 feet. It uses the Pipe Bomb above when it goes off.

#### Roleplay

- **Attitude.** Wary
- **Personality.** A school janitor who has decided the dungeon is the biggest mess he has ever been asked to clean. Polite, tired, and very good with a bucket of pipe bombs.
- **Wants.** To get his two teenage nephews down the stairs alive. He will trade almost anything for a safe route to Club Stairwell.
- **Will trade.** Pipe bombs, mop handles, the location of the Maintenance Tunnels' water barrels.

#### Tactics

Doesn't want a fight. If pushed, he mines the corridor behind him and throws pipe bombs from cover while his nephews run. If one of the nephews is hurt, he stops being polite.

#### Audience

Talking him into joining forces instead of fighting: +1 Audience. Killing him in front of his nephews: −1 Audience and a very bad look.

#### Voice

"Mind the floor. No, really. Mind the floor."
