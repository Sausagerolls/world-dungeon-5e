# Crawler

> *"Welcome, Crawler. Your ordinary life has ended. Your new one will probably be shorter."*

Every survivor who enters the World Dungeon becomes a Crawler. It is not a profession. It is the System's baseline: a heads-up display, an extradimensional inventory, and a body that gets better every time it levels up.

## How the Crawler Works

The Crawler has **two layers**, and you keep both for good. Think of the Crawler as the chassis, and the Floor 3 class as the engine bolted on top.

| Layer | What it gives | When | After Floor 3 |
|-------|---------------|------|---------------|
| **Crawler Core** | Interface, inventory, Stat Points, Skill Picks, Crawler Skills, tome magic | Every level, forever | **Kept** |
| **Survivor Features** | Combat and survival kit learned on the first floors | Levels 1–9 | **Kept**, alongside your class |

You customize your crawler in three ways:

1. **Stat Points** — +1 to an ability score every level.
2. **Skill Picks** — learn or level up a Crawler Skill at every odd level.
3. **GM awards** — Crawler Skills also level up when the GM decides you have earned it.

## Creating a Crawler

**Step 1 — Ability scores.** Crawlers start as ordinary people.
- Set all six ability scores to **10**.
- Distribute **6 points**. No score can go above **14** in this step.
- *Option:* lower one score to 8 to gain 2 more points.
- Then spend your 1st-level Stat Point.

**Step 2 — "Ordinary person" traits.** You have no race until Floor 3. Until then you are what you were before the collapse (normally human). You gain:
- One extra proficiency skill of your choice.
- One extra language, or one tool proficiency, from your old life.

You keep these after Floor 3.

**Step 3 — Background.** Use any 5e background for your life before the dungeon. Ask: *Where were you when the world ended, and what were you wearing?*

**Step 4 — Crawler Skill and first spell.** Spend your 1st-level Skill Pick (see `crawler-skills`). Write **Heal Self** (1st level, Rank I, 1 mana) in your tome spells.

**Step 5 — Starting equipment.** You have only what you had on you:
- The clothes you were wearing (no armor, unless you had a very strange job).
- One **pocket item**: phone, keys, multitool, lighter, a cat. The GM decides if it is useful.
- A System starter pack: 1 day of rations, a waterskin, and a common healing potion (heals 2d4 + 2).

Weapons and armor come from the dungeon.

## Hit Points and Hit Dice

Every level gives you **one Hit Die** and the hit points that go with it. Which die you get depends on what you leveled.

| Level | Hit Die | Hit points gained |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| 1st character level | d8 | 8 + Con modifier |
| Each level before your class (Floors 1–2) | d8 | 1d8 (or 5) + Con modifier |
| Each class level after Floor 3 | That class's die | Roll it, or take the fixed value, + Con modifier |

| Class hit die | Classes | Fixed value |
|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **d6** | Sorcerer, Wizard | 4 |
| **d8** | Bard, Cleric, Druid, Monk, Rogue, Warlock | 5 |
| **d10** | Fighter, Paladin, Ranger | 6 |
| **d12** | Barbarian | 7 |

- **No recalculation on Floor 3.** The levels you lived through as a crawler keep their d8 Hit Dice and hit points, even though class selection gives you those class levels' features. From your next level on, you gain your class's die.
- **A mixed pool.** Your Hit Dice can be a mix of sizes (for example, 9d8 + 3d12). On a short rest, spend whichever dice you like. On a long rest, regain spent Hit Dice as your edition's rules say, choosing which ones.
- **Constitution.** If your Constitution modifier changes, your hit point maximum changes by that amount for every level, as normal.
- **Record it.** Write each level's class, die and hit points on the Level Log page of your character sheet.

## Crawler Core Features

Every crawler has these features for the whole campaign. Class selection does not remove them.

### The Interface
**Core · 1st level**

The System gives you a heads-up display that only you can see. It does **not** include a map: crawlers map the dungeon themselves, on paper.

- **Status bars.** You always know your exact hit points, mana, and active conditions.
- **Inspect.** *Bonus action.* Choose a creature you can see within 60 feet. You learn its name or title, its level (or CR), and one short, often rude, description. The System sometimes lies about bosses.
- **Notifications.** The System tells you about achievements, loot boxes, Crawler Skill level-ups, timer warnings, and announcements. Notifications arrive between turns and cost no actions.

### Crawler Inventory
**Core · 1st level**

You have a personal extradimensional inventory.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Capacity** | 20 + character level slots |
| **One slot holds** | One item, or a stack of identical small items (20 arrows, 10 potions, and so on) |
| **Weight** | Stored items weigh nothing |
| **Store or retrieve** | Free object interaction once per turn. More in the same turn: bonus action each |
| **Cannot store** | Living creatures, items another creature holds or wears, anything larger than a 10-foot cube |
| **Gold** | Kept in a coin purse on your Interface: no weight, no slot. The dungeon only uses gold pieces (gp). |
| **On death** | Your inventory and gold spill out where your body lies |

### Stat Points
**Core · every level**

At each level you gain **1 Stat Point**. Spend it to increase one ability score by 1. Loot boxes and achievements can give more.

- You cannot spend Stat Points during combat.
- Unspent Stat Points are saved for later.
- **There is no cap.** Ability scores can go past 20, and past the normal 5e limit of 30. The modifier keeps following the usual formula: (score − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down. A score of 34 gives +12.
- Race bonuses, class Ability Score Improvements, Stat Potions and every other boost stack freely.

### Skill Picks
**Core · 1st level and every odd level**

You gain a **Skill Pick** at every odd character level (1, 3, 5, 7, and so on). Spend it on one of these:

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Learn** | Gain a new Crawler Skill at Skill Level 1. |
| **Train** | Raise a Crawler Skill you know by 1 Skill Level (maximum 15). |
| **Training Montage** | Gain 2 Stat Points. |
| **Drill** | Gain proficiency with all simple weapons, all martial weapons, all firearms, or one armor category (see *Learning by Doing*). |

### Learning by Doing
**Core · 1st level**

The dungeon does not care what you trained for. You can pick up any weapon, armor or shield and use it.

- **No penalties.** Armor and shields you are not proficient with give **no disadvantage** and do not stop you casting spells. Heavy armor's Strength requirement still applies.
- **Half bonus.** With a weapon you are not proficient with, you add **half your proficiency bonus** (rounded down) to the attack roll, instead of your full bonus.

**Gaining proficiency.** You become proficient with a weapon type (longsword, revolver, frying pan) or an armor category in any of these ways:

| Way | How |
|-----|-----|
| **Use** | Rely on it in real fights. After enough fights (below), you become proficient. The GM decides what counts. Tick the fights on your tracker sheet. |
| **Training** | Pay a safe room trainer (Armory or Gun Shop) 250 gp and spend 8 floor turns. |
| **Skill Pick** | Spend a Skill Pick on **Drill** (above). |
| **Crawler Skills** | Weapon Specialist, Gunslinger, and the Bulwark Inclination all grant proficiencies. |
| **Class** | Your Floor 3 class adds its proficiencies as normal. |

| Learning | Fights needed |
|----------|---------------|
| A simple weapon, or light armor | 3 |
| A martial weapon, a firearm, medium armor, or shields | 5 |
| Heavy armor | 8 |

You must be proficient with light armor before you can learn medium, and with medium before heavy. Proficiencies you learn are yours for good, whatever class you pick.

### Crawler Skills
**Core · 1st level**

Crawler Skills are trained abilities such as *Demolitionist*, *Iron Fist* and *Cooking*. Each one levels from **1 to 15** and gains a new feature at Skill Levels 1, 5, 10 and 15.

Crawler Skills level up in two ways:

- **Skill Picks** (above).
- **GM awards.** The GM decides when a Crawler Skill levels up, usually after you relied on it under pressure or did something memorable with it.

A Crawler Skill cannot go above **Skill Level 15**. Only the *Limit Breaker* feat raises this cap. Full rules and all 45 Crawler Skills are in `crawler-skills`.

### Tome Learning
**Core · 1st level**

You can learn spells from spell tomes found in the dungeon. You cast them with a **mana pool**, and you do not need a spellcasting class. See `tome-magic` and `mana`.

**Heal Self.** Every crawler starts the crawl knowing the **Heal Self** tome spell at Rank I, a gift from the System (see `tome-magic`).

### Achievements and Loot Boxes
**Core · 1st level**

The System rewards notable deeds with achievements, which often come with loot boxes. You can open loot boxes only in a safe room. See `loot-boxes` and `achievements`.

## Survivor Features

Everything you learned to stay alive on the first floors stays with you. You gain these features by **character level**, and you **keep them** after you choose a class on Floor 3 (see `class-selection`).

**Overlaps with your class.** Survivor features sit on top of your class features, with these limits:

- **Extra Attack** does not stack. If your class gives you Extra Attack and you took it from Hardened, you can switch Hardened to Arcane Edge, or keep both and gain nothing extra.
- **Duplicate proficiencies** (for example, Bulwark's armor and a fighter's) give you nothing extra. Instead, choose one other weapon type or tool to be proficient with.
- **Similar bonus actions** (Run Away! and a rogue's Cunning Action) both work, but you still have only one bonus action each turn.

### Scrappy
**Survivor · 1st level**

You fight with whatever is at hand.

- **Unarmed strikes:** 1d4 bludgeoning. You can use Strength or Dexterity.
- **Improvised weapons:** 1d6 damage, or 1d8 in two hands. You can throw them (range 20/60 feet).
- **Breakage:** When you hit with an improvised weapon, roll a d6. On a 1, it breaks after the attack.

If you also have the Iron Fist Crawler Skill, use whichever unarmed damage die is higher.

### Will to Live
**Survivor · 1st level · Bonus action**

You regain hit points equal to **1d8 + your character level**.

- **Uses:** 1. You regain it when you finish a short or long rest.
- At **8th level** you have 2 uses.

### Run Away!
**Survivor · 2nd level · Bonus action**

You take the Dash or Disengage action.

### Inclination
**Survivor · 3rd level, improves at 6th and 9th**

Your fighting style starts to show. Choose one Inclination. It often hints at your Floor 3 class, but it does not bind you.

#### Bruiser

| Level | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| 3rd | Once per turn, an unarmed strike or improvised weapon hit deals **+1d4** damage. Advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to grapple or shove. |
| 6th | Extra damage becomes **1d6**. When you deal it, a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save (DC 8 + PB + Str modifier) or falls prone. |
| 9th | Extra damage becomes **1d8**. Resistance to bludgeoning damage while you wear no medium or heavy armor. |

#### Skulker

| Level | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| 3rd | Once per turn, a finesse, ranged, or thrown improvised weapon hit with advantage deals **+1d6** damage. Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks while moving at half speed. |
| 6th | Extra damage becomes **2d6**. You also deal it without advantage if an ally is within 5 feet of the target and you do not have disadvantage. Run Away! can also take the Hide action. |
| 9th | Extra damage becomes **3d6**. |

#### Bulwark

| Level | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| 3rd | Proficiency with medium armor and shields. Will to Live also gives temporary hit points equal to your character level. |
| 6th | *Reaction:* when you take damage, reduce it by **1d10 + Con modifier**. PB times per long rest. |
| 9th | Proficiency with heavy armor. |

#### Dabbler

| Level | Benefit |
|-------|---------|
| 3rd | Learn one cantrip from any class spell list (Intelligence is the spellcasting ability). Your mana pool increases by your character level. |
| 6th | Learn one more cantrip. Learn one 1st- or 2nd-level spell as a tome spell, without a tome. |
| 9th | Once per long rest, cast a tome spell of 3rd level or lower without spending mana. |

### Hard to Kill
**Survivor · 4th level · 1 per long rest**

When you drop to 0 hit points and do not die outright, you can drop to 1 hit point instead.

### Hardened
**Survivor · 5th level**

Choose one:

- **Extra Attack.** You attack twice, instead of once, when you take the Attack action on your turn.
- **Arcane Edge.** Add your Intelligence modifier to the damage of cantrips you cast.

### Danger Sense
**Survivor · 7th level**

You have advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see, such as traps and spells. This does not work while you are blinded, deafened, or incapacitated.

## Designer Notes

**Power budget**
- Crawlers gain 3–4 levels per floor, with no level cap (see `leveling`).
- **Crawlers are meant to feel like they are on a power trip.** There is no cap on ability scores, survivor features stack with class features, and Stat Points arrive every level. The dungeon answers with tougher monsters: scale encounters up as the party grows, not the other way round.
- By level 20, a crawler has about 14 more ability points than a standard 5e character, plus 10 Skill Picks.
- Survivor features stay after class selection, so a classed crawler is stronger than a normal character of the same level. Build encounters as if the party were **one level higher** from Floor 3 onward. Adjust after playtest.

**Why stats start at 10**
- Every Stat Point feels like progress, and it matches the "ordinary person in a bathrobe" opening.
- By about 4th level, with a race added, a crawler is even with a standard point-buy character.

**Crawler Skills vs. Skill Picks**
- Skill Picks are the player's planned choices. GM awards reward what the player actually does at the table.
- The cap of 15 makes the top level feel earned. Limit Breaker is the only way past it.

**Open questions for playtest**
- Inventory slot count.
- Is Run Away! too strong at 2nd level?
- How often should the GM award Skill Levels? Starting target: about one per player per session.
