# Warlock — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the warlock as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new warlock changes here; the Warlock Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Pact Magic becomes Pact Mana.** A small pool that refills on a short or long rest and always casts at your pact level (see the table above and `mana`). A World Dungeon spell cast with Pact Mana gains one rank for each level your pact level is above the spell's level.
- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Warlock list. Patron spells are written into your tome as their World Dungeon replacements.
- **Pact Beam** replaces *eldritch blast*, and counts as eldritch blast for every invocation. See *Eldritch Invocations* below.
- **Magical Cunning.** Regain half your Pact Mana (rounded up).
- **Contact Patron.** Cast *Ask the System* at Rank II (for *contact other plane*) once per long rest without mana. Your patron answers. The System listens in.
- **Mystic Arcanum.** Choose one World Dungeon Warlock spell of each level from 6th to 9th as you reach it. You can cast each once per long rest without mana. Mystic Arcanum casts don't count against the one-big-spell-per-level rule.
- **Eldritch Master.** Magical Cunning restores all your Pact Mana.
