# Paladin — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the paladin as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new paladin changes here; the Paladin Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Paladin list, up to 5th level. Your oath spells are written into your tome as their World Dungeon replacements.
- **Paladin's Smite.** You always know *Smackdown* (for *divine smite*). You can cast it once per long rest without mana.
- **Smite spells** (searing, wrathful, thunderous, branding) are all *Signature Move*.
- **Faithful Steed.** You always know *Mount Up* (for *find steed*), and can cast it once per long rest without mana. Your steed is not a pet and doesn't take your pet slot.
- **Lay On Hands** stacks with *Heal Self* and the *First Aid* Crawler Skill.
- **Aura of Protection** covers your allies against traps and environmental saves on every floor.
- **Breaking your oath on camera** is worth a lot of Audience. It is also, technically, breaking your oath.
