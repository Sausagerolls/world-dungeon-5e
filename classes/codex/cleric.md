# Cleric — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the cleric as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new cleric changes here; the Cleric Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Cleric list. Your domain spells are written into your tome as their World Dungeon replacements and don't count against your number.
- **Divine Order: Thaumaturge** gives one extra cantrip from the World Dungeon Cleric list.
- **Channel Divinity** works as normal. Turn Undead is very good on Floor 1's Catacomb Tours.
- **Divine Intervention** (10th level) casts any World Dungeon Cleric spell of 5th level or lower without mana. If you don't know the spell, it is cast at Rank I.
- **Greater Divine Intervention** (20th level) casts *Feature Request* (for *wish*).
- **Gods and the System.** Your god is real, and is watching the show like everyone else. Divine Intervention never undoes a floor collapse or the floor timer.
