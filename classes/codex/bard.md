# Bard — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the bard as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new bard changes here; the Bard Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Bard list (see *Spells* at the end of this codex). You don't prepare spells: the Prepared Spells column is how many you know.
- **Bardic Inspiration and the crowd.** Whenever a creature uses one of your Bardic Inspiration dice to turn a failure into a success in front of the cameras, the GM can count it as a Showmanship moment for *Crowd Pleaser* and *Hype Man*.
- **Font of Inspiration.** "Expend a spell slot" to regain a use of Bardic Inspiration means spend mana: 2 mana for a use.
- **Magical Secrets** (10th level) opens the World Dungeon Cleric, Druid and Wizard lists. Spells you learn from them are bard spells.
- **Words of Creation** (20th level) gives you *Unalive* (for *power word kill*) and *Medic!* at Rank V (for *power word heal*), and each can target a second creature within 10 feet of the first.
- **Countercharm** works against the System's charm effects and sponsor mind games too.
