# Monk — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the monk as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new monk changes here; the Monk Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Martial Arts** stacks with the *Iron Fist* Crawler Skill: add the skill's damage bonus to your Martial Arts die.
- **Unarmored Defense and Unarmored Movement** need you to wear no armor and carry no shield, as normal.
- **Focus Points** are not mana. Features that cast a spell by spending Focus Points cast its World Dungeon replacement.
- **Warrior of Shadow.** You know *Lights Out* (for *darkness*) and *Cardboard Cutout* (for *minor illusion*). Shadow Arts: spend 1 Focus Point to cast *Lights Out* without mana.
- **Slow Fall** works against pit traps and the stairwell drops of the World Dungeon. It doesn't save you from a floor collapse.
- **Evasion** stacks with the *Escape Artist* and *Parkour* Crawler Skills.
