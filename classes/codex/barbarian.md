# Barbarian — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the barbarian as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new barbarian changes here; the Barbarian Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Rage and magic.** While your Rage is active you can't cast spells or concentrate on them, and that includes tome spells and *Heal Self*. Heal up before you rage, or after.
- **Unarmored Defense** works as normal. Crawler armor has no penalties (*Learning by Doing*), but Unarmored Defense still needs you to wear no armor.
- **Danger Sense** overlaps the survivor feature of the same name. Use the overlap rules in `crawler`: you gain the better version, and the GM gives you a replacement for the duplicate.
- **Extra Attack** at 5th level overlaps the survivor feature if you have it (see `crawler`).
- **Weapon Mastery** uses your Crawler weapon proficiencies too. The *Weapon Specialist* Crawler Skill stacks with it.
- **Crawler Skills.** *Bruiser*, *Bloodlust* and *Powerful Strike* add to your damage while you rage. *Tough as Nails* stacks with your d12 hit die.
- **Primal Champion** raises Strength and Constitution with no cap of 24 in the World Dungeon (there is no ability score cap).
