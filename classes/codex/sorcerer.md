# Sorcerer — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the sorcerer as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new sorcerer changes here; the Sorcerer Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Sorcerer list. Subclass spells are written into your tome as their World Dungeon replacements.
- **Font of Magic.** Spend sorcery points to gain the mana cost of a spell level (the same sorcery point costs as creating a spell slot); mana gained this way vanishes when you finish a long rest. Or spend the mana cost of a level to gain sorcery points equal to that level.
- **Metamagic** works on World Dungeon spells. **Twinned Spell** works on a spell whose next rank adds a target: the spell counts as one rank higher for this casting (up to Rank V).
- **Sorcerous Restoration** regains sorcery points, not mana.
- **Wild Magic Surge** results that name a spell cast its World Dungeon replacement at Rank I.
- **Mana Well** and *Tome Scholar* stack with your class mana.
