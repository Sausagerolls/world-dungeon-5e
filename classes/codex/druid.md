# Druid — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the druid as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new druid changes here; the Druid Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Druid list. Circle spells are written into your tome as their World Dungeon replacements.
- **Druidic.** You always know *Pet Translator* (for *speak with animals*).
- **Wild Shape** uses beasts from the 2025 Monster Manual. The Crawler Sheet lists them. Your gear melds into your new form, but your Crawler Inventory still works: storing or taking an item is your free interaction, as long as the form has a way to hold it.
- **Pets.** Wild Shape doesn't change your pet. Your active pet (see `pets`) keeps fighting beside you, and *Pet Translator* lets you talk to it.
- **Wild Companion** summons *Hench-Critter* (for *find familiar*) without mana.
- **Wild Resurgence.** Spend 2 mana to regain a use of Wild Shape, or spend a use of Wild Shape to regain 2 mana (once per long rest).
- **Elemental Fury: Potent Spellcasting** adds your Wisdom modifier to the damage of World Dungeon druid cantrips.
- **Archdruid: Nature Magician.** Each Wild Shape use you turn into magic gives the mana cost of a spell level of 2 (3 mana), up to a 6th-level cost in total.
