# Wizard — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the wizard as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new wizard changes here; the Wizard Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellbook = your tome.** A wizard doesn't keep a separate spellbook. Every spell you know, from any source, is in your System Tome.
- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Wizard list. A wizard reading a tome holding a wizard spell can learn it up to your class's maximum spell level, ignoring the tome spell level limit (the Intelligence requirement still applies).
- **Arcane Recovery.** Regain **2 mana per slot level** it would restore. A 6th-level wizard (3 levels) regains 6 mana.
- **Memorize Spell.** After a short rest, replace one wizard spell you learned from your class with another from the World Dungeon Wizard list. The new spell starts at Rank I.
- **Spell Mastery.** Choose a 1st-level and a 2nd-level wizard spell in your tome. You can cast them at their current rank without mana.
- **Signature Spells.** Choose two 3rd-level wizard spells in your tome. You can cast each once without mana, regaining the uses on a short or long rest.
- **Tome Scholar** is made for you. So is *Mana Well*.
