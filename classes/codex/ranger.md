# Ranger — World Dungeon Changes

Everything here changes or adds to the ranger as the Player's Handbook describes it. Where this page and the class text disagree, this page wins. Add new ranger changes here; the Ranger Codex is built from it.

## Changes

- **Spellcasting.** You learn spells from the World Dungeon Ranger list, up to 5th level. Rangers learn no cantrips, except through the Druidic Warrior fighting style (two cantrips from the World Dungeon Druid list).
- **Favored Enemy.** You always know *Quest Marker* (for *hunter's mark*). You can cast it without mana as many times as Favored Enemy says, regaining the uses on a long rest.
- **Relentless Hunter** (13th level): taking damage can't break your concentration on *Quest Marker*.
- **Precise Hunter** (17th level): you have advantage on attack rolls against the target of your *Quest Marker*.
- **Foe Slayer** (20th level): *Quest Marker*'s extra damage die becomes a d10, or a d12 if it is already a d10.
- **Beast Master.** Your Primal Companion is your **active pet** (see `pets`). It uses the Primal Beast stat block, or 4 × your level in hit points if that is higher, and can take pet upgrades and evolutions.
- **Pet Trainer** is the Crawler Skill rangers love most.
