# Crawler Skills

Crawler Skills are trained abilities the System tracks for each crawler: *Demolitionist*, *Iron Fist*, *Cooking*, and so on. They level up from **Skill Level 1 to 15**. They improve when you spend Skill Picks on them, and when the GM awards a level for using them well.

> **Name note.** "Crawler Skill" is not the same as a 5e skill proficiency (Athletics, Stealth, and so on). These rules always say "Crawler Skill" for this system, and "proficiency skill" for 5e skills.

## Rules

### Gaining a Crawler Skill

You can gain a new Crawler Skill at **Skill Level 1** in any of these ways:

- **Skill Pick.** Spend a Skill Pick (see `crawler`).
- **Skill book.** Read a skill book from loot or a shop. It crumbles when read.
- **Loot boxes and achievements.** Some rewards grant a Crawler Skill or a Skill Level.
- **System grant.** If you keep doing something over many sessions without the Crawler Skill for it, the GM can grant it at Skill Level 1. It arrives as a System notification.

There is no limit to the number of Crawler Skills you can know.

### Raising Skill Levels

**The GM decides when a Crawler Skill levels up.** There are no marks to track. Good times to award a Skill Level:

- After a session in which the crawler relied on the Crawler Skill under pressure.
- After a big moment: the skill won a fight, saved a life, or got the party out of trouble.
- As a reward from a skill book, loot box, achievement or sponsor.

Each Crawler Skill entry has a **GM cue** line that describes the kind of use that should earn a level. Players can also spend a **Skill Pick** to raise a Crawler Skill by 1 level.

> **Pacing guide.** About one Skill Level per player per session works well on the early floors. Slow down later, so that Skill Level 15 feels like an achievement.

### Skill Cap

A Crawler Skill cannot go above **Skill Level 15**. Only the **Limit Breaker** feat (below) can raise this cap.

### Reading an Entry

Each Crawler Skill entry has:

- **Metadata line:** category and key ability.
- **GM cue:** the kind of use that should earn a Skill Level.
- **Scaling:** a value that grows with Skill Level. *SL* means Skill Level.
- **Milestones:** extra features at **Skill Level 1, 5, 10 and 15**. Each milestone adds to the ones below it unless it says it replaces them.
- **Level 20 milestone:** only for a Crawler Skill raised past 15 with the **Limit Breaker** feat.

**Shared terms**

- **PB** is your proficiency bonus.
- **Skill DC** = 8 + PB + the key ability modifier.
- Uses "per long rest" all return when you finish a long rest.

## Breaking the Cap

### Limit Breaker
**Feat · Prerequisite: a Crawler Skill at Skill Level 15**

You have pushed a Crawler Skill past what the System thought possible. You can take this feat in place of a class Ability Score Improvement, or as a reward from a loot box or achievement. You can take it more than once, choosing a different Crawler Skill each time.

- **Ability.** Increase the Crawler Skill's key ability score by 1.
- **New cap.** Choose one Crawler Skill at Skill Level 15. Its cap becomes **Skill Level 20**. The GM still decides when it levels up.
- **Scaling continues.** The Crawler Skill's Scaling value keeps growing with its formula. If the Scaling is a damage die that stops at d12, it instead adds +1 damage for each Skill Level above 15.
- **Level 20 milestone.** At Skill Level 20, gain the Crawler Skill's **Level 20** milestone, listed with each Crawler Skill below.

## Combat

### Demolitionist
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Use explosives in a fight, under a time limit, or to change a battlefield.
*Scaling:* Explosives you set or throw deal **+1 damage die per 5 SL**, and their save DC increases by **+1 per 5 SL**.

- **Level 1 — Handler.** You are proficient with explosives and alchemist's supplies. You never set off an explosive by accident. You can set a fuse to go off at the start or end of any turn within the next minute.
- **Level 5 — Bomb Maker.** During a short rest, you can make explosives from dungeon scrap, up to your PB per long rest. Each one fills a 10-foot radius: Dexterity save (Skill DC), **2d6** fire damage, half on success. You have advantage on saves against explosions.
- **Level 10 — Shaped Charge.** When you set or throw an explosive, you can make its area a 30-foot cone or a 60-foot line instead. Choose up to PB creatures in the area. They automatically succeed on the save and take no damage.
- **Level 15 — Big Boom.** Once per long rest, an explosive you set or throw deals maximum damage, and its area is doubled.
- **Level 20 — Extinction Event.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your explosives deal **double damage** to creatures, and you are immune to damage from your own explosives. Big Boom recharges on a short rest.

### Iron Fist
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength or Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Win or turn a dangerous fight with your bare hands.
*Scaling:* Unarmed strike damage die: **d6** (SL 1–4), **d8** (SL 5–9), **d10** (SL 10–14), **d12** (SL 15).

- **Level 1 — Brawler.** When you take the Attack action, you can make one unarmed strike as a bonus action.
- **Level 5 — Hardened Knuckles.** Your unarmed strikes count as magical.
- **Level 10 — Knockout.** When you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike, the target must succeed on a Constitution save (Skill DC) or be stunned until the end of your next turn.
- **Level 15 — Unstoppable.** Your unarmed strikes ignore resistance to bludgeoning damage.
- **Level 20 — One-Inch Punch.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your unarmed strikes ignore all resistance and immunity to bludgeoning damage. Once per turn, a hit can push the target up to 30 feet; if it hits a wall or creature, both take 2d10 extra bludgeoning damage.

### Powerful Strike
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength**

*GM cue:* Land a Powerful Strike that drops or seriously hurts a strong enemy.
*Scaling:* Extra damage is **1d8 per 3 SL**, rounded up (1d8 at SL 1, 5d8 at SL 15).

- **Level 1 — Heavy Hit.** PB times per long rest, when you hit with a melee attack, add the extra damage (same type as the weapon).
- **Level 5 — Knockback.** A Large or smaller target hit by a Powerful Strike is pushed 10 feet away from you.
- **Level 10 — Second Wind.** When you roll initiative with no uses left, you regain one use.
- **Level 15 — Relentless.** Your uses return when you finish a short or long rest.
- **Level 20 — Earthsplitter.** *Limit Breaker only.* Powerful Strike has no use limit. Once per turn when you use it, every creature of your choice within 10 feet of the target takes half the extra damage.

### Pitcher's Arm
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength or Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Hit an important target with a thrown object at long range or under pressure.
*Scaling:* Thrown weapon attacks deal **+1 damage per 2 SL**, rounded down.

- **Level 1 — Good Arm.** You are proficient with thrown improvised weapons. Your thrown weapons' normal and long ranges are doubled. You can draw a thrown weapon from your inventory as part of the attack.
- **Level 5 — Ricochet.** Once per turn, when you hit with a thrown weapon, you can make one more thrown attack, at disadvantage, against a different target within 15 feet of the first.
- **Level 10 — Trick Shot.** Your thrown attacks ignore half and three-quarters cover.
- **Level 15 — Bank Shot.** Ricochet attacks no longer have disadvantage, and you can ricochet twice per turn.
- **Level 20 — Fastball.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your thrown weapons' range is 300 feet with no long range. You can throw one extra weapon each time you take the Attack action, and thrown explosives can ricochet too.

### Weapon Specialist
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: weapon's ability**

You choose one weapon type when you gain this Crawler Skill (for example: longsword, crossbow, frying pan). You can gain it more than once, choosing a different type each time.

*GM cue:* Win or turn a dangerous fight with that weapon type.
*Scaling:* Attacks with that weapon type deal **+1 damage per 3 SL**, rounded down.

- **Level 1 — Familiar Grip.** You are proficient with that weapon type, and gain +1 to attack rolls with it.
- **Level 5 — Second Nature.** You can draw or stow the weapon as part of an attack with it.
- **Level 10 — Precise.** Your attacks with that weapon type score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
- **Level 15 — Master.** Critical hits on 18–20. The attack bonus becomes +2.
- **Level 20 — Living Weapon.** *Limit Breaker only.* Critical hits with your chosen weapon type on 17–20. Your weapon of that type counts as magical, can't be disarmed, and returns to your hand as a free action.

### Dirty Fighting
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength**

*GM cue:* Win an exchange with a cheap trick: a trip, a shove, sand in the eyes.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks to shove.

- **Level 1 — Cheap Shot.** Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can also try to shove it or knock it prone, without using an action.
- **Level 5 — Riposte.** When a creature within 5 feet misses you with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to make one melee attack against it.
- **Level 10 — Stay Down.** A creature you knock prone cannot stand up until the end of its next turn.
- **Level 15 — Pocket Sand.** When you use Cheap Shot, the target must also succeed on a Constitution save (Skill DC) or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
- **Level 20 — No Rules.** *Limit Breaker only.* Cheap Shot and Pocket Sand work on every hit, not once per turn. Creatures you knock prone also lose their reaction until the end of their next turn.

### Bodyguard
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Constitution**

*GM cue:* Protect an ally from an attack that would have hurt them badly.
*Scaling:* Bodyguard range is **5 feet + 5 feet per 5 SL**.

- **Level 1 — Watch Out.** When a creature you can see attacks an ally within range, you can use your reaction to give the attack disadvantage.
- **Level 5 — Take the Hit.** You can instead use your reaction to become the target of that attack, if the attacker can reach you.
- **Level 10 — Braced.** You have resistance to damage from attacks you redirect.
- **Level 15 — Wall.** Once per round, you can use Watch Out or Take the Hit without using your reaction.
- **Level 20 — Immovable Object.** *Limit Breaker only.* You have resistance to all damage while an ally is within your Bodyguard range. Damage you take for an ally is halved before resistance.

### Dead Eye
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Make a ranged shot that decides a fight.
*Scaling:* Ranged weapon attacks deal **+1 damage per 3 SL**, rounded down.

- **Level 1 — Steady Aim.** As a bonus action, if you have not moved this turn, you gain advantage on your next ranged attack this turn. Your speed becomes 0 until the end of the turn.
- **Level 5 — Long Shot.** Attacking at long range does not give you disadvantage on ranged weapon attacks.
- **Level 10 — Weak Spot.** Your ranged weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
- **Level 15 — Called Shot.** When you hit with an attack that used Steady Aim, the target must succeed on a Constitution save (Skill DC) or suffer one effect of your choice until the end of its next turn: blinded, deafened, or speed 0.
- **Level 20 — Thousand-Yard Stare.** *Limit Breaker only.* Steady Aim works even if you moved. Ranged attacks you make with advantage score a critical hit on 18–20, and Called Shot can impose two effects at once.

### Two-Fisted
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength or Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Win or turn a fight with a weapon in each hand.
*Scaling:* Your off-hand attacks deal **+1 damage per 3 SL**, rounded down.

- **Level 1 — Dual Grip.** You add your ability modifier to the damage of your off-hand attack. You can two-weapon fight with any one-handed melee weapons, including improvised weapons.
- **Level 5 — Guard Hands.** You gain +1 AC while you hold a melee weapon in each hand.
- **Level 10 — Cross Cut.** When your Attack action and your off-hand attack both hit the same target in one turn, it takes extra damage equal to your SL.
- **Level 15 — Blender.** When you make an off-hand attack, you can make two off-hand attacks instead of one.
- **Level 20 — Whirlwind.** *Limit Breaker only.* Once per turn, as part of your Attack action, you can make one attack with each weapon against every creature within your reach.

### Wrestler
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Strength**

*GM cue:* Grapple something that should not be grappled.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks to grapple and to keep a grapple.

- **Level 1 — Clinch.** You can grapple creatures up to two sizes larger than you. You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature you are grappling.
- **Level 5 — Suplex.** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a slam against a creature you are grappling. It takes **1d8 + your Strength modifier** bludgeoning damage and falls prone. The grapple continues.
- **Level 10 — Pin.** A creature you are grappling is also restrained.
- **Level 15 — Piledriver.** Your slam deals **3d8 + your Strength modifier** damage. The target must also succeed on a Constitution save (Skill DC) or be stunned until the end of your next turn.
- **Level 20 — Final Fall.** *Limit Breaker only.* You can grapple a creature of any size. Your slam deals **5d8 + your Strength modifier** damage, and you can slam two grappled creatures into each other for the same damage to both.

### Backstab
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Strike an enemy who never saw you coming.
*Scaling:* When you hit a creature that is surprised or cannot see you, deal **+1d6 per 3 SL** (rounded up) extra damage, once per turn. This stacks with Sneak Attack.

- **Level 1 — First Strike.** You have advantage on attack rolls against creatures that have not taken a turn yet in this combat.
- **Level 5 — Fade.** You can take the Hide action as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Twist the Knife.** Any hit you score against a surprised creature is a critical hit.
- **Level 15 — Silent Kill.** Once per long rest, when you hit a creature that cannot see you, and it has **SL × 10** hit points or fewer after the damage, it drops to 0 hit points. Bosses are immune.
- **Level 20 — Death by a Thousand Cuts.** *Limit Breaker only.* Silent Kill works on any creature that is not a boss, recharges on a short rest, and against a boss deals 10d10 extra damage instead.

### Bloodlust
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Constitution**

*GM cue:* Keep fighting, and win, while badly hurt.
*Scaling:* While you are below half your hit points, your melee attacks deal **+1 damage per 3 SL** (rounded down, minimum 1).

- **Level 1 — Blood Up.** While you are below half your hit points, you have advantage on Strength checks and Strength saves.
- **Level 5 — Bloodthirst.** When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack, you gain temporary hit points equal to **SL + your Constitution modifier**.
- **Level 10 — Red Mist.** While you are below half your hit points, you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage.
- **Level 15 — Refuse to Fall.** Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points and do not die outright, you drop to **SL × 2** hit points instead.
- **Level 20 — Berserker God.** *Limit Breaker only.* While below half your hit points, you can make one extra attack whenever you take the Attack action, and you regain hit points equal to half the damage you deal with melee attacks.

### Bomb Surgeon
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Disarm, rewire or rebuild an explosive while it is dangerous.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks to disarm or modify explosives and mechanical traps.

- **Level 1 — Steady Hands.** You have advantage on checks to disarm explosives. When you Inspect an explosive, you learn its damage, area and trigger.
- **Level 5 — Rewire.** As an action, you can change the trigger of an explosive or explosive trap you can touch: timer, pressure plate, tripwire, or a spoken word.
- **Level 10 — Frankenbomb.** During a short rest, you can combine two explosives into one. Add their damage dice together (maximum total dice equal to your SL). Its area is the larger area + 5 feet.
- **Level 15 — Defuse Anything.** When an explosive within 30 feet of you is about to go off, you can use your reaction to make an Intelligence check against its save DC. On a success, it does not go off. You can do this PB times per long rest. Boss and System explosives are immune.
- **Level 20 — Bomb Whisperer.** *Limit Breaker only.* Defuse Anything has no use limit, and works on boss and System explosives. When you defuse one, you can store it in your inventory, ready to use.

### Gunslinger
**Combat · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Win or turn a fight with a firearm.
*Scaling:* Firearm attacks deal **+1 damage per 3 SL**, rounded down.

- **Level 1 — Trigger Discipline.** You are proficient with firearms (see `firearms`). Being within 5 feet of a hostile creature does not give you disadvantage on attacks with pistols.
- **Level 5 — Speed Reload.** You can reload a firearm as a bonus action. You can clear a misfire as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Dead Shot.** Your firearm attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
- **Level 15 — Fan the Hammer.** Once per turn, when you take the Attack action with a pistol or revolver, you can make one extra attack with it.
- **Level 20 — Bullet Time.** *Limit Breaker only.* Once per long rest, for 1 minute, you make two extra firearm attacks whenever you take the Attack action, and your firearms never misfire or need reloading.

## Survival

### Tough as Nails
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Constitution**

*GM cue:* Survive a fight in which you drop below a quarter of your hit points.
*Scaling:* Your hit point maximum increases by **1 per character level** (SL 1–4), **2** (SL 5–9), or **3** (SL 10–15).

- **Level 1 — Thick Skin.** Scaling only.
- **Level 5 — Endurance.** You have advantage on saves against exhaustion.
- **Level 10 — Shrug It Off.** When you take damage, you can use your reaction to reduce it by your Constitution modifier + SL. You can do this PB times per long rest.
- **Level 15 — Unbreakable.** Once per long rest, when you take damage, you can take none instead.
- **Level 20 — Invincible.** *Limit Breaker only.* Unbreakable recharges on a short rest. While you have at least 1 hit point, you cannot be reduced below 1 hit point by a single hit.

### Cockroach
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Constitution**

*GM cue:* Come back from 0 hit points.
*Scaling:* When you regain hit points while at 0, you regain **SL extra** hit points.

- **Level 1 — Still Kicking.** You have advantage on death saving throws.
- **Level 5 — Get Up.** When you are stabilized, you regain 1 hit point and can stand up using 5 feet of movement.
- **Level 10 — Not Today.** Once per long rest, when you fail a death saving throw, you succeed instead and regain hit points equal to your character level.
- **Level 15 — Unkillable.** You can use Not Today PB times per long rest.
- **Level 20 — Can't Stay Dead.** *Limit Breaker only.* Once per floor, when you die, you return to life at the start of your next turn with hit points equal to your level, unless your body was destroyed.

### Escape Artist
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Escape a grab, trap, restraint, or a fight you should not be in.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks and saves to escape grapples and restraints.

- **Level 1 — Slippery.** You have advantage on checks and saves to escape grapples and restraints. Nonmagical restraints cannot hold you for more than 1 minute.
- **Level 5 — Footwork.** Difficult terrain does not slow you. Opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage.
- **Level 10 — Blink.** Once per long rest, when you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to teleport up to 30 feet to a space you can see. The attack misses.
- **Level 15 — Gone.** You can use Blink PB times per long rest.
- **Level 20 — Houdini.** *Limit Breaker only.* You cannot be grappled or restrained unless you allow it. Blink no longer uses your reaction, and the attack misses everyone nearby, not only you.

### Iron Stomach
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Constitution**

*GM cue:* Eat, drink or breathe something that should have hurt you.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to saves against poison and disease.

- **Level 1 — Strong Gut.** You have advantage on saves against poison and disease. You can safely eat anything that is not actively poisonous.
- **Level 5 — Toxin Tolerance.** You have resistance to poison damage.
- **Level 10 — Purge.** You cannot be poisoned.
- **Level 15 — Immune.** You are immune to poison damage. You can drink a potion as a bonus action.
- **Level 20 — Bottomless.** *Limit Breaker only.* You are immune to disease and the poisoned condition, and you can drink any potion as a free action. Potions you drink have twice their normal effect.

### Night Eyes
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Spot something important in darkness or bad visibility.
*Scaling:* You have darkvision to **50 feet + 10 feet per SL** (60 feet at SL 1). If you already have darkvision, use whichever is longer, +30 feet.

- **Level 1 — Low Light.** Scaling only.
- **Level 5 — Fog Sight.** Smoke, fog and dust do not obscure your vision within 30 feet.
- **Level 10 — Void Sight.** You can see normally in magical darkness within 30 feet.
- **Level 15 — True Eyes.** You have truesight to 30 feet.
- **Level 20 — All-Seeing.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your truesight range becomes 120 feet, and you can see through walls up to 5 feet thick.

### Trap Sense
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Find, avoid or disarm a dangerous trap.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks to find and disarm traps.

- **Level 1 — Wary.** You have advantage on checks to find and disarm traps. You remember every trap you have found, and never set off one of them by accident.
- **Level 5 — Flinch.** You have advantage on saves against traps, and resistance to damage from traps.
- **Level 10 — Sixth Sense.** You automatically notice any trap within 10 feet of you.
- **Level 15 — Redirect.** Sixth Sense range becomes 30 feet. When you trigger a trap, you can use your reaction to choose a different target in its area.
- **Level 20 — Trap Lord.** *Limit Breaker only.* Sixth Sense range becomes 60 feet. Traps never affect you, and you can rearm any trap you find to work for you.

### First Aid
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Keep an ally alive in a fight or a crisis.
*Scaling:* Healing you give with a healer's kit or a potion restores **+SL** extra hit points.

- **Level 1 — Patch Up.** You are proficient with healer's kits. As an action, you can spend one use of a healer's kit to restore **1d6 + SL** hit points to a creature. Each creature can benefit once per short rest. You can stabilize a creature as a bonus action.
- **Level 5 — Field Medic.** You can give a potion to another creature within 5 feet as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Surgeon.** During a short rest, you can end one disease or the poisoned condition, or remove one level of exhaustion, for up to PB creatures.
- **Level 15 — Not on My Watch.** Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 feet would die, you can use your reaction to make it stable at 0 hit points instead. This does not work against effects that leave no body.
- **Level 20 — Miracle Medic.** *Limit Breaker only.* Not on My Watch recharges on a short rest. Your Patch Up restores **5d6 + SL** hit points and can be used as a bonus action.

### Parkour
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Cross dangerous ground, or escape, by climbing, jumping or sliding.
*Scaling:* Your long and high jump distances increase by **1 foot per SL**.

- **Level 1 — Free Runner.** You have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. You can make running jumps without a run-up.
- **Level 5 — Wall Run.** You can move along vertical surfaces for up to half your speed in a turn. You fall if you end your turn on one.
- **Level 10 — Roll With It.** When you fall, you can use your reaction to reduce the falling damage by **5 × SL**. You land on your feet.
- **Level 15 — Untouchable Line.** When you Dash, you can move through hostile creatures' spaces, and you do not provoke opportunity attacks.
- **Level 20 — Gravity Optional.** *Limit Breaker only.* You can move along walls and ceilings for your whole turn without falling, and you take no falling damage.

### Sneak
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Get past serious danger without being seen.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

- **Level 1 — Quiet Feet.** You can move at full speed without a penalty to Stealth.
- **Level 5 — Blend In.** You can try to hide when you are only lightly obscured, or in a crowd of creatures your size or larger.
- **Level 10 — Ghost.** If you are hidden and you miss with an attack, you stay hidden.
- **Level 15 — Not Here.** Once per long rest, as an action, you become invisible for 1 minute (concentration). It ends early if you attack or cast a spell.
- **Level 20 — Phantom.** *Limit Breaker only.* Not Here no longer needs concentration, recharges on a short rest, and does not end when you attack.

### Forager
**Survival · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Find food, water, or useful material in a hostile place.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to Survival checks to forage, track, and identify plants and monster parts.

- **Level 1 — Live off the Land.** Each day, you can find food and water for up to PB creatures, if any exists nearby. You know if a monster part is edible.
- **Level 5 — Harvester.** After a fight, you can spend 10 minutes harvesting monster parts. They count as crafting materials for Tinkerer and Alchemy (GM sets the value).
- **Level 10 — Trail Boss.** Difficult terrain does not slow your party's travel pace. Your party cannot be surprised while it travels with you.
- **Level 15 — Alchemical Eye.** You can learn every property of a plant, potion or monster part by handling it for 1 minute.
- **Level 20 — Dungeon Gourmet.** *Limit Breaker only.* When you harvest, every creature gives two extra Monster Parts or Essence. Food you forage counts as a Cooking meal with one Chef's Special.

## Utility

### Cooking
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Cook a meal for the party in a dangerous place, or with strange dungeon ingredients.
*Scaling:* Each creature that eats your meal gains temporary hit points equal to **your character level + SL**.

- **Level 1 — Hot Meal.** During a short or long rest, you can cook a meal for up to 6 creatures (needs food and heat).
- **Level 5 — Comfort Food.** Each creature that eats also has advantage on its next saving throw within 8 hours.
- **Level 10 — Chef's Special.** Choose a bonus for the meal, which lasts 8 hours: +10 feet speed, resistance to one damage type, or +1 to attack rolls.
- **Level 15 — Feast.** Choose two Chef's Special bonuses. Each creature that eats also removes one level of exhaustion.
- **Level 20 — Five-Star Chef.** *Limit Breaker only.* Meals give all three Chef's Special bonuses, the temporary hit points are doubled, and each diner regains one spent Hit Die.

### Lock and Key
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Open a lock that matters, under time pressure or danger.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks with thieves' tools.

- **Level 1 — Picks.** You are proficient with thieves' tools.
- **Level 5 — Quick Hands.** You add double your PB to checks with thieves' tools. You can pick a lock as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Skeleton Key.** Once per long rest, you can open one magical lock or seal without a check. Floor boss and System seals are immune.
- **Level 15 — Master Key.** You can use Skeleton Key PB times per long rest.
- **Level 20 — Open Sesame.** *Limit Breaker only.* Skeleton Key has no use limit, and works on boss seals. You can lock any door or container so only you can open it.

### Cartographer
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Find a route, shortcut, or stairwell under time pressure.
*Scaling:* Travelling through explored areas costs **1 floor turn per 3 areas** (normally 2). At SL 10, 1 per 4 areas.

- **Level 1 — Pathfinder.** You have advantage on checks to find secret doors, hidden passages and shortcuts. Once per floor, you can ask the GM "Is there a shorter way?" and get a true answer.
- **Level 5 — Read the Signs.** When you enter an area, you can ask the GM one question, such as "Is a boss near?", "Has anything big been through here?" or "Is there a safe room within 3 areas?" The GM answers truthfully. Once per area.
- **Level 10 — Stairwell Sense.** You always know the direction to the nearest stairwell, and roughly how far it is (near, far, or very far).
- **Level 15 — Survey.** When you arrive on a new floor, the GM tells you its neighbourhood themes, roughly where each one is, and where one stairwell is. The GM can hand you a rough sketch instead.
- **Level 20 — Living Atlas.** *Limit Breaker only.* When you arrive on a new floor, the GM tells you where every stairwell and safe room is.

### Pack Rat
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Pull the right item out of your inventory at the right moment.
*Scaling:* Your inventory gains **2 slots per SL**.

- **Level 1 — Organized.** Scaling only.
- **Level 5 — Juggler.** You can store or retrieve two items with one free object interaction.
- **Level 10 — Quick Swap.** Once per turn, you can swap the item in your hand with one in your inventory, with no action.
- **Level 15 — Pocket Catch.** When a nonmagical object or projectile that fits in one slot is thrown or shot at you, you can use your reaction to store it in your inventory. The attack misses.
- **Level 20 — Bag of Everything.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your inventory gains 50 slots. Once per long rest, you can pull out any mundane item worth 10 gp or less that you do not have.

### Tinkerer
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Repair, build or rig something that gets the party out of trouble.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks with tinker's tools.

- **Level 1 — Fixer.** You are proficient with tinker's tools. During a short rest, you can use dungeon scrap to repair a broken item, or build a simple trap or tool (GM approval).
- **Level 5 — Salvage.** You can break down a common magic item into crafting material. Use it to add a minor property to another item (light, waterproof, returns to your inventory, and so on).
- **Level 10 — Craftsman.** In a safe room, you can craft uncommon magic items at half the normal time and cost.
- **Level 15 — Artificer.** Craftsman applies to rare magic items too.
- **Level 20 — Mad Inventor.** *Limit Breaker only.* Craftsman applies to very rare magic items, and you can craft one item per floor at no gold cost (materials still needed).

### Appraisal
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Judge loot, or a deal, correctly when it matters.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to checks to identify items and judge their value.

- **Level 1 — Price Tag.** When you Inspect an item, you learn its value, and whether it is magical.
- **Level 5 — Identify.** When you Inspect a magic item, you also learn its properties, except curses.
- **Level 10 — Curse Check.** Inspect also shows curses, charges and attunement needs.
- **Level 15 — Loot Sense.** Once per floor, when you open a loot box, the GM rolls its contents twice and you choose one result.
- **Level 20 — Treasure Hunter.** *Limit Breaker only.* Loot Sense works on every loot box you open, not once per floor.

### Haggler
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Get a deal from a shopkeeper, NPC or sponsor that should not have happened.
*Scaling:* Shop prices are **2% lower per SL** for you. Items you sell earn **2% more per SL**.

- **Level 1 — Bargain.** You have advantage on Charisma checks to haggle with merchants.
- **Level 5 — Full Price.** Safe room shops buy your items at full value, not half.
- **Level 10 — Back Stock.** Each shop visit, the shop shows you one extra item from a better loot table.
- **Level 15 — Black Market.** Once per floor, you can buy one item of a rarity the shops do not normally sell on this floor.
- **Level 20 — Shopkeeper's Nightmare.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your discount is 50%, and once per floor you can buy one legendary item from any shop.

### Pet Trainer
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Wisdom**

*GM cue:* Your pet does something that matters to the fight or the story.
*Scaling:* Your pet's hit point maximum increases by **2 × SL**.

- **Level 1 — Companion.** Your pet (see the *Pets* handbook) adds your PB to its AC, attack rolls, damage rolls and saves. You can tame creatures of CR 1/4 or lower.
- **Level 5 — Partner.** You can tame and keep pets of CR 1 or lower. You can command your pet to attack as part of your Attack action.
- **Level 10 — Shared Pain.** You can tame and keep pets of CR 3 or lower, and its attacks count as magical. When your pet takes damage, you can use your reaction to take that damage instead.
- **Level 15 — Evolution.** You can tame and keep pets of CR 5 or lower. Your pet gains one evolution from the Evolutions table in the *Pets* handbook.
- **Level 20 — Beast King.** *Limit Breaker only.* You can tame and keep pets of CR 10 or lower. Your pet gains one more evolution, and attacks twice when commanded to attack.

### Sticky Fingers
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Dexterity**

*GM cue:* Steal something important while someone is watching.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks.

- **Level 1 — Quick Pocket.** When you steal an item, you can store it in your inventory as part of the same action.
- **Level 5 — Light Touch.** You can make a Sleight of Hand check, or take the Use an Object action, as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Snatch.** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a Sleight of Hand check against the target's Athletics or Acrobatics. On a success, you take one one-handed item it is holding and store it in your inventory.
- **Level 15 — Grand Theft.** Snatch also works on two-handed items, and on worn items other than armor.
- **Level 20 — Phantom Thief.** *Limit Breaker only.* Snatch works on armor and on items a creature is holding with both hands. You can Snatch once on every hit.

### Smooth Talker
**Utility · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Talk your way out of, or into, serious trouble.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to Charisma (Persuasion) and Charisma (Deception) checks.

- **Level 1 — Silver Tongue.** Once per long rest, you can reroll a Persuasion or Deception check. Use the new roll.
- **Level 5 — Read the Room.** You can make a Wisdom (Insight) check as a bonus action. On a success, you also learn the creature's attitude toward you.
- **Level 10 — Calm Down.** As an action, choose a hostile creature within 30 feet that can understand you. It must succeed on a Wisdom save (Skill DC) or not attack you for 1 minute, unless you or your allies harm it. You can do this once per short rest.
- **Level 15 — Deal Maker.** Once per long rest, choose a creature that is not a boss and can understand you. It must succeed on a Wisdom save (Skill DC) or be charmed by you for 1 hour.
- **Level 20 — Silver-Tongued Devil.** *Limit Breaker only.* Deal Maker works on bosses, recharges on a short rest, and lasts 24 hours.

## Showmanship

The audience is always watching. *(These Crawler Skills tie into the forthcoming `viewers-and-sponsors` rules.)*

### Crowd Pleaser
**Showmanship · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Do something spectacular in front of the cameras.
*Scaling:* None.

- **Level 1 — Showboat.** Once per long rest, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a critical hit, a stunt, or anything the GM finds entertaining, you gain Inspiration.
- **Level 5 — Share the Spotlight.** You can use Showboat twice per long rest. You can give your Inspiration to an ally within 30 feet as a bonus action.
- **Level 10 — Crowd Favorite.** When you spend Inspiration, roll two extra d20s and use any one of the three rolls.
- **Level 15 — Superstar.** You can use Showboat PB times per long rest.
- **Level 20 — Fan Favorite.** *Limit Breaker only.* Showboat has no use limit. Whenever you spend Inspiration, every ally within 30 feet also gains advantage on its next d20 roll.

### Trash Talk
**Showmanship · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Make a dangerous enemy lose its temper and make a mistake.
*Scaling:* A creature that fails the save takes psychic damage equal to **half SL** (rounded up).

- **Level 1 — Taunt.** As a bonus action, insult a creature within 30 feet that can hear you. It must succeed on a Wisdom save (Skill DC) or have disadvantage on attacks against creatures other than you until the end of its next turn.
- **Level 5 — Cutting Words.** Add your Charisma modifier to the psychic damage.
- **Level 10 — Heckler.** You can target up to two creatures with one Taunt.
- **Level 15 — Tilted.** A creature that fails the save also cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.
- **Level 20 — Roast Battle.** *Limit Breaker only.* Taunt targets every creature you choose within 30 feet. A creature that fails the save also takes psychic damage equal to your SL.

### Hype Man
**Showmanship · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Rally the party when it is losing.
*Scaling:* Pep Talk gives temporary hit points equal to **SL + your Charisma modifier**.

- **Level 1 — Pep Talk.** As a bonus action, choose one ally within 30 feet that can hear you. It gains the temporary hit points. You can do this PB times per long rest.
- **Level 5 — Momentum.** The target of Pep Talk also adds 1d4 to its next attack roll or saving throw.
- **Level 10 — Team Spirit.** Pep Talk can target up to three allies.
- **Level 15 — Encore.** Once per long rest, as a bonus action, every ally within 60 feet ends the charmed and frightened conditions on itself and gains the benefit of Pep Talk.
- **Level 20 — Hype Train.** *Limit Breaker only.* Pep Talk targets every ally within 60 feet. Encore recharges on a short rest.

### Dramatic Entrance
**Showmanship · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Charisma**

*GM cue:* Start a fight in a way the audience will remember.
*Scaling:* Add **half SL** (rounded down) to initiative rolls.

- **Level 1 — Make an Entrance.** On your first turn in a combat, your speed increases by 10 feet, and your first hit deals +1d6 damage.
- **Level 5 — Stunned Silence.** When you roll initiative, choose one creature that can see you. It must succeed on a Wisdom save (Skill DC) or be unable to take reactions until the end of its first turn.
- **Level 10 — All Eyes on Me.** Stunned Silence can target up to PB creatures.
- **Level 15 — Showstopper.** You cannot be surprised. On your first turn in a combat, you can take one extra action: Attack, Dash, or Use an Object.
- **Level 20 — Main Character.** *Limit Breaker only.* Showstopper's extra action can be any action, and you get it on your first two turns of every combat.

## Magic

These Crawler Skills improve tome magic (see `tome-magic`).

### Mana Well
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Run your mana pool to empty in a dangerous fight.
*Scaling:* Your mana pool increases by **SL**.

- **Level 1 — Reserve.** Scaling only.
- **Level 5 — Surge.** When you roll initiative, you regain 2 mana.
- **Level 10 — Deep Well.** When you finish a short rest, you regain half your mana pool + SL.
- **Level 15 — Overflow.** Once per long rest, when you have 0 mana, you can regain half your mana pool as a bonus action.
- **Level 20 — Infinite Reserve.** *Limit Breaker only.* When you roll initiative, you regain mana equal to your SL. Overflow recharges on a short rest.

### Tome Scholar
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Learn a new tome, or use a tome spell to solve a problem in a new way.
*Scaling:* None.

- **Level 1 — Speed Reader.** You can learn tome spells one spell level higher than your normal limit. You learn a tome in 1 action instead of 1 minute.
- **Level 5 — Efficient.** Tome spells cost 1 less mana to cast (minimum 1).
- **Level 10 — Deep Study.** When you read a duplicate tome, the spell gains 2 ranks instead of 1.
- **Level 15 — Archmage.** You can cast each tome spell of 6th level or higher twice per long rest, not once.
- **Level 20 — Grand Archmage.** *Limit Breaker only.* Tome spells cost half their normal mana (rounded up). You can cast tome spells of 6th level or higher three times per long rest each.

### Signature Spell
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence · Prerequisite: know a tome spell**

Choose one tome spell you know when you gain this Crawler Skill. You can gain it more than once, choosing a different spell each time.

*GM cue:* Cast the chosen spell at a turning point in a fight.
*Scaling:* None.

- **Level 1 — Favorite.** You can cast the spell once per long rest without spending mana.
- **Level 5 — Practiced.** You can cast it twice per long rest without spending mana.
- **Level 10 — Perfected.** When you cast the spell, you can roll its damage twice and use either total.
- **Level 15 — Instinct.** Once per long rest, you can cast the spell as a bonus action, if its casting time is 1 action.
- **Level 20 — Spell of Legend.** *Limit Breaker only.* Your signature spell costs no mana and can be cast as a bonus action once per round.

### Alchemy
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Brew something that saves the day.
*Scaling:* Healing potions you brew restore **+SL** extra hit points. The save DC of poisons you brew increases by **+1 per 5 SL**.

- **Level 1 — Brewer.** You are proficient with alchemist's supplies. During a long rest, you can brew one common healing potion from dungeon ingredients.
- **Level 5 — Batch.** During a long rest, you can brew up to PB items in any mix: healing potion, antitoxin, alchemist's fire, or basic poison (Constitution save, Skill DC, 2d6 poison damage and poisoned for 1 minute on a failure).
- **Level 10 — Greater Brew.** Once per long rest in a safe room, you can brew a greater healing potion, or an uncommon potion you have identified before.
- **Level 15 — Master Alchemist.** Once per long rest in a safe room, you can brew a superior healing potion, or a rare potion you have identified before.
- **Level 20 — Philosopher's Stone.** *Limit Breaker only.* Once per long rest in a safe room, you can brew a very rare potion you have identified before. Potions you brew last twice as long.

### Mana Shield
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Survive a big hit by burning mana.
*Scaling:* Each mana you spend on Barrier prevents **2 damage** (3 at SL 5, 4 at SL 10, 5 at SL 15).

- **Level 1 — Barrier.** When you take damage, you can use your reaction to spend up to PB mana to reduce the damage.
- **Level 5 — Ward Ally.** You can use Barrier when an ally within 30 feet takes damage.
- **Level 10 — Spell Sink.** When you succeed on a saving throw against a spell, you regain 2 mana.
- **Level 15 — Aegis.** Once per round, you can use Barrier without using your reaction.
- **Level 20 — Arcane Fortress.** *Limit Breaker only.* Each mana you spend on Barrier prevents 10 damage, and you can spend any amount of mana at once.

### Spellblade
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence · Prerequisite: know a tome spell or cantrip**

*GM cue:* Mix weapon attacks and tome magic in a fight that matters.
*Scaling:* Charged Strike deals **1d6 force damage, +1d6 per 5 SL**.

- **Level 1 — Charged Strike.** Once per turn, when you hit with a weapon, you can spend 1 mana to add the force damage.
- **Level 5 — War Caster.** You have advantage on Constitution saves to keep concentration on tome spells. You can cast tome spells with your hands full.
- **Level 10 — Blade and Spell.** When you take the Attack action, you can replace one attack with a tome cantrip.
- **Level 15 — Arcane Surge.** When you cast a tome spell with a casting time of 1 action, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action.
- **Level 20 — Blade Arcana.** *Limit Breaker only.* Charged Strike costs no mana and can be used on every hit. Blade and Spell can replace any number of attacks with tome cantrips.

### Quick Cast
**Magic · Crawler Skill · Key ability: Intelligence**

*GM cue:* Get a spell off at the last possible moment.
*Scaling:* None.

- **Level 1 — Snap.** Once per long rest, you can cast a tome cantrip or a 1st-level tome spell with a casting time of 1 action as a bonus action instead.
- **Level 5 — Quick.** You can use Snap PB times per long rest, on tome spells of 3rd level or lower.
- **Level 10 — Counter.** You know *counterspell* as a tome spell, without a tome.
- **Level 15 — Double Cast.** Once per long rest, as an action, you can cast two tome spells that each have a casting time of 1 action. Pay mana for both. Only one can need concentration.
- **Level 20 — Spell Storm.** *Limit Breaker only.* Double Cast recharges on a short rest, and both spells can need concentration.
