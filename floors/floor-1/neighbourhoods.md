# The Neighbourhoods

Seven neighbourhoods and the club around the main stairwell. Each one has an encounter table, a boss room, the boss's mechanics, the hints that lead up to it, and the stat blocks for its custom creatures. **\*** marks a custom creature; its stat block is at the end of its neighbourhood. Every other creature is from the 2025 Monster Manual. Save DCs in the stat blocks are 8 + proficiency bonus + the listed ability modifier.

## 1. The Llama Pens

*Level 1 · where the crawlers start*

Stone corridors strewn with straw and scorched with lava spit. The rooms are stables, feed rooms and shearing rooms. "BEWARE OF LLAMA" is scratched into every door. The **Bad Llama** is the local menace: a llama, but bad, that spits lava. The starting safe room (General Store and Armory) is here.

### Boss Room: The Big Barn

**Boss · Mama Drama, Queen of the Herd · CR 2 · Level 2**

A long barn hall. **Hay lofts** run along both walls (10-foot platforms, reached by ladders). A **lava trough** runs down the middle like a river. Straw covers the floor, there are stable doors at both ends, and a **dinner bell** hangs by the entrance. Two side pens hold her crias.

#### Mechanics

- **Lava bath.** When Mama Drama ends her turn in the trough, she regains 10 hit points. Pouring a barrel of water into a section of trough (an action) cools it into stone. Each barrel stops one 10-foot section.
- **Dramatic faint.** The first time she takes 10 or more damage from one hit, she collapses and appears dead. At the start of her next turn she bursts back up with a lava blast.
- **Herd call.** While she is bloodied, 2 Bad Llama crias arrive from the pens at the start of each of her turns. Ringing the dinner bell (an action) sends every cria to the feed troughs for 2 rounds instead.
- **Hay fire.** Lava that hits a hay loft sets it alight. A creature that starts its turn in a burning loft takes 1d6 fire damage.

#### Hints on the Way In

- In the feed room, a scratched note on the water barrels: *"…NOT in the troughs. Makes her furious. And rocky."*
- Llamas drinking at a trough are tougher (encounter 11).
- The "dead" llama in the carcass room was faking (encounter 10).
- Crias freeze and look around whenever a bell rings anywhere nearby.
- Half-burned hay bales next to every lava puddle.

#### Other Ways to Win

Turn the trough to stone and fight her on dry ground. Set the lofts on fire under her. Ring the bell and pick off the herd first.

#### Rewards

Boss Box (Gold), Boss Trophy: **Mama Drama's Golden Fleece**. Bonus achievement: ***Dinner Is Served*** (Notable): win without her ever healing in the trough.

### Boss Room: The Showtime Stage

**Boss · Chuckles the Cheese Mouse · CR 3 · Level 2–3**

A stage behind a red curtain, with the animatronic band (2 animated armor, inactive until Chuckles is bloodied) at the back. A **ball-pit moat** (difficult terrain, 5 feet deep) separates the stage from six party tables. A **lighting rig** hangs over the stage. A "STAFF" door leads backstage to the **control booth**.

#### Mechanics

- **Happy Birthday.** At the start of each of his turns, Chuckles sings. Creatures that can hear him must save or be charmed and sing along (see his stat block).
- **Showtime power.** While the stage lights are on, Chuckles regains 10 hit points at the start of each of his turns, and he cannot be surprised.
- **Kill the lights.** In the control booth, flipping the breaker takes an action and a DC 12 check with tinker's tools or Strength. With the lights off, Chuckles is "between shows": his speed is halved, he cannot sing, and he stops healing.
- **The band.** When Chuckles is first bloodied, the band wakes up and joins the fight.
- **"It's my birthday!"** Once per crawler, a crawler can shout it. On his next turn Chuckles cannot attack that crawler, and gives them cake instead.

#### Hints on the Way In

- In corridors, the lights flicker now and then. Every time they do, every animatronic freezes for a second.
- The memo in the STAFF ONLY room (encounter 11): *"If the mouse acts up again, KILL THE BREAKER (booth behind the stage). Do NOT let him finish the song."*
- A faded sign on Party Room 1: *"Birthday kids get a FREE present from Chuckles! Just tell him it's your birthday!"*
- Earplugs in the lost-and-found box. A deafened creature cannot hear the song.
- A side room holds a melted animatronic, scorched by lightning. It went down fast.

#### Rewards

Boss Box (Gold), Boss Trophy: **Chuckles' Golden Ticket Dispenser**. Bonus achievement: ***Lights Out*** (Notable): kill the breaker before he finishes his first song.

### Boss Room: The Main Stage

**Boss · DJ Spore-Lord · myconid sovereign (MM p. 223) and court · Level 2–3**

A huge cavern. The **DJ booth** sits on top of a giant mushroom (a 15-foot platform), above a springy fungus **dance floor**. Four **speaker stacks** (shrieker fungi) stand at the corners. **Disco balls** (2 gas spore fungi) drift overhead. Chill-out alcoves line the edges.

**The fight:** a myconid sovereign with a court of 4 myconid sprouts and 2 myconid adults.

#### Mechanics

- **The beat.** On initiative count 20, the bass drops. Each creature on the dance floor makes a DC 12 Constitution save, taking 2d6 thunder damage on a failure, half on a success. A creature that **danced** this round (a bonus action and a DC 12 Performance check) is unaffected and has +1 AC until its next turn.
- **Speaker stacks.** While any stack stands, the sovereign's spore effects reach twice as far. Each stack has AC 5 and 13 hit points. Each destroyed stack lowers the bass-drop damage by one die. With all four gone, the music stops.
- **Disco balls.** Popping a gas spore over the dance floor hits everyone below it, crawlers and myconids alike.
- **Dance-off.** At any point, a crawler can challenge the DJ. They must win three Performance contests in a row, one per round (DC 13, then 14, then 15). If they do, the DJ concedes: same rewards, no fight.

#### Hints on the Way In

- The ravers move to a rhythm, and the bass drop never seems to hurt them (encounter 10).
- Myconid roadies in the speaker room: *"Careful with the stacks! The Lord needs every one for the set."*
- The VIP door's bouncer says the Lord "respects a real dancer".
- Burned-out patches of fungus under every disco ball.

#### Rewards

Boss Box (Gold), Boss Trophy: **the Spore-Lord's Golden Headphones**. Bonus achievement: ***Drop the Beat*** (Notable): win the dance-off. Every crawler who wins it also gets a **VIP wristband** for Club Stairwell.

### Boss Room: The Recycling Center

**Boss · Mega-Sweeper 9000 · CR 3 · Level 3**

A high-ceilinged room around a central **recycler pit** (20 feet deep, grinding). **Conveyor belts** circle the pit, all flowing inward. A **charging dock** stands in one corner. **Sprinkler heads** line the ceiling, and a big red **FIRE ALARM** lever is on the wall.

#### Mechanics

- **Dry use only.** On a wet surface, Mega-Sweeper's speed is halved, it cannot use Vacuum Pull, and it has vulnerability to lightning damage. Pulling the fire alarm (an action) or starting any fire sets off the sprinklers and soaks the whole room for 1 minute.
- **Low battery.** When it is first bloodied, it heads for its charging dock, and regains 10 hit points at the start of each turn it spends docked. Jamming the dock (an action and DC 13 tinker's tools, or wedging debris in it) stops this.
- **The pit.** A creature pulled or pushed into the pit takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage. 1d4 rounds later it is spat out onto a conveyor belt.

#### Hints on the Way In

- Sweeper Bots stop dead at the edge of any wet patch (encounter 2).
- The torn manual in the break room: *"Model 9000: DRY SURFACES ONLY. Never operate on wet floors."*
- The supervisor's log: *"Unit 9000 returned to dock at 50% battery. AGAIN."*
- The boiler room's *"Fire suppression: ACTIVE"* sign (encounter 5).
- Dust-free tracks on the floor, all leading to the same corner.

#### Rewards

Boss Box (Gold), Boss Trophy: **Mega-Sweeper's Golden Dustbag**. Bonus achievement: ***Spring Cleaning*** (Notable): defeat it without anyone falling into the pit.
