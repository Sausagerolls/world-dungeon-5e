# Floor 1: Welcome to the Crawl

> *"Welcome, Crawlers! Floor 1 is a classic. Stone corridors, mysterious rooms, and absolutely nothing that wants to kill you. That last part was a joke. Good luck!"*

Floor 1 is a traditional dungeon crawl: stone corridors, heavy doors and rooms, each section taken over by something ridiculous. Chunks of Earth fell into the dungeon with the crawlers, and the System has "decorated" each neighbourhood around a theme.

This book is for the GM. Monster Manual creatures use their **2025 Monster Manual** names and stat blocks (all checked against the 5e.tools data for that book). Custom creatures have full stat blocks at the end of the neighbourhood they appear in, and bosses built on Monster Manual creatures have a boss template there too.

## Running Floor 1

### Encounters

Each neighbourhood has a **d12 encounter table**. Every entry says where it happens: **Corridor**, **Room**, **Door**, **Junction** or **Vent**.

- When the party **enters a new room**, or travels a stretch of **corridor**, roll a d6. On a **1–2**, roll on the neighbourhood's table.
- When an **Ambush Check** fails during a short rest, roll on the table and reroll any result that is not a fight.
- Fights are built as *moderate* encounters for **4 crawlers** at the neighbourhood's level. For 5–6 crawlers, add about half again as many creatures.
- Encounter results never award Audience points by themselves. The audience reacts to what the crawlers **do**.

### Bosses and Hints

Every neighbourhood boss has a **boss room** with its own mechanics. On the way there, crawlers can find **hints**: things they notice about the boss's tricks and weaknesses. Some are written into the encounter tables. Place the rest in rooms near the boss. Sharp players arrive with a plan; careless ones arrive with a eulogy.

### Rewards

Every neighbourhood boss gives:

- A **Boss Box** and the boss's **Boss Trophy** (see the Loot and Achievements and Dungeon Items handbooks).
- The ***Neighborhood Watch*** achievement. It is a Major achievement, which on Floor 1 means a **Gold** box.
- A bonus achievement for beating the boss's mechanic, listed with each boss.

Killing the floor boss gives ***Floor Boss Down*** (Gold) and the main stairwell.

### The Clock

The floor timer is 48 floor turns. Use the GM Floor Tracker sheet. Safe rooms close to new arrivals with 6 turns left. At 0, the floor collapses (see *Safe Rooms and the Floor Timer* in The Crawler handbook).
