# The Pantheon

> *"Eight major gods hold contracts in the World Dungeon this season. Pick one. Or don't. They'll remember either way."*

Eight major gods take worshippers in the World Dungeon, but only crawlers they have met (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`). Each one has a Calling (what gets its attention), a vow, three Devotions, Taboos, four Boons, its own way of punishing Lapses, and its own way of smiting apostates. The rules are in `gods`. Their avatars and lesser kin are in `god-stat-blocks` (GM only).

**Spell boons.** A boon that gives you a spell writes it into your tome as a granted spell (see `spells-replacements`). Its source is the god. Choose Wisdom or Charisma as its spellcasting ability when you gain it. You can cast it once per long rest without mana, and with mana as normal.

**Rivals.** Moldra and Embrix · Saint Payola and Hearthmother Tuul · Thrumm and the Brass Warden · Nyx-Ra and Wobblegob.

| God | Domains | Smites with | Wants you to… |
|-----|---------|-------------|---------------|
| **Mother Moldra** | Death, Life, Nature | Necrotic rot | Eat rot, feed corpses to the soil |
| **Embrix the Unquenched** | Light, War | Fire | Light fires and never put them out |
| **Saint Payola** | Knowledge, Trickery | Force (repossession) | Pay tithes and close deals |
| **Thrumm** | Tempest, War | Lightning | Keep a Grudge List and cross names off |
| **Nyx-Ra** | Grave, Twilight | Psychic | Keep secrets and let the dead stay dead |
| **The Brass Warden** | Forge, Order, Protection | Radiant judgement | Keep promises and hold the line |
| **Wobblegob** | Trickery, Tempest | A very big mallet | Prank, joke, never explain the joke |
| **Hearthmother Tuul** | Life, Peace | Cold loneliness | Share meals and never let anyone die alone |

## Mother Moldra, the Rot Queen
**Major god · Death, Life, Nature · Rival: Embrix · Holy symbol: a mushroom growing out of a skull**

> *"Everything rots. Everything grows back. Mother is very patient. Mother is also very hungry."*

Moldra is the god of rot, fungus and rebirth. Her temples are damp, warm and smell like a forest floor after rain, or a fridge after a power cut. She loves the dungeon: it is the biggest compost heap she has ever seen.

**Calling.** Let something rot that everyone else would burn, or eat something truly disgusting, on camera. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"Everything rots. Everything grows back. I will feed the Mother."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Eat something spoiled, raw or rotten (a monster part counts). Constitution save DC 10, or you are Poisoned for 1 hour. |
| **Offering** | Leave a corpse of CR at least equal to the floor number to rot in soil or at a shrine, with a mushroom planted on it. |
| **Great Deed** | Kill a floor boss and leave its body to rot, untouched and unlooted, for 1 hour. |

**Taboos.** Burning a corpse. Preserving food or a body by magic (*Freshness Seal* and the like). Killing a fungus creature that didn't attack you first.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Iron Gut.** You are immune to disease, and you have advantage on saving throws against the Poisoned condition. |
| Devotee | **Fruiting Body.** Once per long rest, when you drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 instead, and soft fungus sprouts over your wounds: you gain temporary hit points equal to your level. |
| Zealot | **Mother's Gifts.** You learn *Patch Job* and *Not Today*. In soil or rot, *Patch Job* heals its maximum. |
| Chosen | **Rebirth.** Once per floor, when you die, your body is wrapped in a fungal cocoon. One minute later you burst out with half your hit points and 1 Exhaustion level, unless the cocoon (AC 10, 30 hit points) is destroyed first. |

- **Displeasure.** Food rots in your hands. Rations, potions and food you open have a 50% chance to have gone off: a spoiled potion heals half, a spoiled meal gives no benefit.
- **Wrath.** Mold creeps over you. Your hit point maximum drops by twice your level, and *Heal Self* can't heal you.
- **Atonement.** Eat a full meal of rot in front of a shrine: Constitution save DC 15, or you are Poisoned until your next long rest.

**Apostate's Smite: Composted.** Necrotic damage, as the flesh of your face blooms with mushrooms. *Lasting effect:* you can't regain hit points from spells or potions, and you have disadvantage on Charisma checks. Fungus creatures treat you as food.

## Embrix the Unquenched
**Major god · Light, War · Rival: Moldra · Holy symbol: a torch that is never put out**

> *"Let it burn bright. Let it burn on camera. Ratings are a kind of heat, too."*

Embrix is the god of fire, glory and spectacle. He is loud, handsome and permanently on fire, and he treats every fight as a pyrotechnics show. His worshippers are easy to find: follow the smoke.

**Calling.** Set something big on fire in front of a crowd, or kill a monster with fire and pose about it. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"I will burn bright. I will never let the fire die."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Light a fire (a real one: a torch, a campfire, a burning goblin) and let it burn out on its own. |
| **Offering** | Burn something valuable at a shrine: an item worth at least 50 gp × the floor number. |
| **Great Deed** | Kill a floor boss with fire damage. |

**Taboos.** Putting out a fire on purpose (including by magic). Hiding in darkness when you could carry light. Running from a fight while it is on camera.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Torchbearer.** You learn *Torch*. You can't be Blinded by bright light or by fire. |
| Devotee | **Hotter.** Your fire damage ignores resistance to fire (not immunity). |
| Zealot | **Glory Burn.** You learn *Kaboom*. |
| Chosen | **Halo of Flame.** You are immune to fire. Once per turn, when you score a critical hit or drop a creature to 0 hit points, fire bursts from you: each creature you choose within 10 feet takes 3d6 fire damage. |

- **Displeasure.** Fires won't light for you. Your fire spells fizzle (the mana is spent) and torches you hold go out.
- **Wrath.** You are always smoldering. You have vulnerability to fire damage, and you shed dim light in a 10-foot radius you can't put out.
- **Atonement.** Walk through a fire at a shrine (you take 4d10 fire damage) without stopping, screaming or using magic.

**Apostate's Smite: Immolation.** Fire damage, as a pillar of flame comes down from the ceiling. *Lasting effect:* you can't benefit from resistance or immunity to fire, and you shed bright light in a 10-foot radius: you can't hide in darkness.

## Saint Payola, Patron of Sponsors
**Major god · Knowledge, Trickery · Rival: Hearthmother Tuul · Holy symbol: a gold coin with a logo on both sides**

> *"Everything has a price. Prayer is just a subscription. Have you considered the premium tier?"*

Saint Payola is the god of deals, luck and advertising. She owns a stake in most of the dungeon's sponsors and some of its shops. She is always smiling, and her contracts are always longer than they look.

**Calling.** Plug a sponsor at the worst possible moment, or close a deal nobody thought you could. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"Everything has a price. I will always pay mine."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Say a sponsor's slogan out loud, on camera, with feeling. |
| **Offering** | Tithe at a shrine: 10% of the gold you gained on this floor (at least 10 gp). |
| **Great Deed** | Close a deal that should be impossible: sell something to a boss, buy your way past a fight, or get a monster to pay you. |

**Taboos.** Giving something away for free to anyone outside your party. Breaking a deal. Stealing from a shop.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Member Pricing.** Safe room shops sell to you at 10% off. |
| Devotee | **Lucky Pull.** Once per floor, when you roll on a loot box table, you can roll the d100 again and choose either result. |
| Zealot | **Fortune.** You have 3 Luck Points (as the *Lucky* feat), regained on a long rest. |
| Chosen | **Sponsored.** Once per floor, choose any item a safe room shop on this floor sells. It appears in your inventory. Pay its price back before you take the stairs, or gain 2 Lapses. |

- **Displeasure.** Bad credit. Shops charge you double and won't buy anything from you.
- **Wrath.** Late fees. Each time you finish a long rest, you lose 10% of your gold.
- **Atonement.** Pay a double tithe: 20% of all the gold you are carrying.

**Apostate's Smite: Repossession.** Force damage, as an invisible collector shakes you down. *Lasting effect:* your most valuable magic item (the GM decides) is repossessed and vanishes, shops refuse to serve you, and you subtract 10 from every loot box roll.

## Thrumm, the Thunder That Remembers
**Major god · Tempest, War · Rival: the Brass Warden · Holy symbol: a lightning bolt carved over a list of names**

> *"Thrumm forgets nothing. Thrumm forgives nothing. Thrumm is also very, very loud."*

Thrumm is the god of storms, hunting and grudges. Every one of his worshippers keeps a **Grudge List**: a list of creatures that wronged them or their friends. His temples are built where lightning has struck, which in the World Dungeon is surprisingly often.

**Calling.** Swear revenge out loud on a creature that hurt your party, then go after it. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"I forget nothing. I forgive nothing. I will be the thunder."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Add a name to your Grudge List (a creature that wronged you or your party) or read the whole list aloud. |
| **Offering** | Cross a name off: kill a creature on your Grudge List. |
| **Great Deed** | Avenge an ally's death on the floor it happened, by killing the creature that killed them. |

**Taboos.** Forgiving a grudge out loud. Retreating from a creature on your Grudge List while it can see you. Working with a worshipper of the Brass Warden for a whole floor.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Never Forget.** You have advantage on Wisdom (Perception and Survival) checks to find or track creatures on your Grudge List. |
| Devotee | **Rolling Thunder.** Once per turn, when you hit a creature on your Grudge List, it takes an extra 1d6 thunder damage. |
| Zealot | **Storm Called.** You learn *Storm Chaser*. |
| Chosen | **The Thunder Remembers.** You can't be surprised. When a creature on your Grudge List damages you, you can use your reaction to strike it with lightning: 4d10 lightning damage, no save. |

- **Displeasure.** Thunder follows you everywhere. You have disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and so does your party within 30 feet of you.
- **Wrath.** The storm hunts you. Each time you finish a long rest outside a safe room, lightning strikes you for 4d10 lightning damage.
- **Atonement.** Hunt down the oldest living name on your Grudge List and kill it.

**Apostate's Smite: The Stroke.** Lightning damage, from a bolt that comes through any ceiling. *Lasting effect:* you have vulnerability to lightning damage, and your name is on every Grudge List: Thrumm's worshippers and minor deities hunt you on sight.

## Nyx-Ra, the Quiet Dark
**Major god · Grave, Twilight · Rival: Wobblegob · Holy symbol: a closed eye, embroidered in black on black**

> *"Shh."*

Nyx-Ra is the god of secrets, sleep and endings. She keeps every secret anyone has ever whispered into the dark, and she is the reason the dead in the World Dungeon usually stay dead. Her temples are silent. Talking in them is rude. Talking about them is ruder.

**Calling.** Keep a secret that would have helped you, or stop someone raising the dead. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *(whispered)* *"What I know, I keep. What ends, stays ended."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Before you sleep, whisper a secret no one else knows into the dark. |
| **Offering** | Give a shrine a secret of this floor that you found: a hidden area, a boss's weakness, a hidden stash. Tell no one else. |
| **Great Deed** | Put to rest a creature that should not be awake: destroy an undead boss, or a creature that came back from the dead. |

**Taboos.** Revealing a secret someone told you in confidence. Casting a spell that brings the dead back to life (being brought back yourself doesn't count). Saying the name of a dead crawler out loud.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Night Eyes.** You gain Darkvision out to 60 feet, or your Darkvision increases by 60 feet. |
| Devotee | **The Dark Is Mine.** You learn *Lights Out*, and you can see through any darkness you create. |
| Zealot | **Hush.** Once per long rest, as a bonus action while you are in dim light or darkness, you become Invisible for 1 minute (as *Stealth Mode*). |
| Chosen | **The Last Word.** You don't need to sleep. When a creature you can see dies, you can use your reaction to ask it one question; it answers truthfully before its soul goes quiet. |

- **Displeasure.** Insomnia. Long rests don't give back any of your spent Hit Dice.
- **Wrath.** You talk in your sleep. After each long rest, the GM tells the table one of your character's secrets, and you have disadvantage on Ambush Checks (monsters hear you mumbling).
- **Atonement.** Spend a whole long rest at a shrine in total darkness and silence. Speaking, even once, ruins it.

**Apostate's Smite: The Forgetting.** Psychic damage, as part of your mind goes quiet. *Lasting effect:* you lose access to one random spell in your tome (it reappears when the effect ends), and your highest Crawler Skill counts as 5 Skill Levels lower.

## The Brass Warden
**Major god · Forge, Order, Protection · Rival: Thrumm · Holy symbol: a brass key over a closed door**

> *"The law is a wall. The Warden is the wall. Please stand behind the wall."*

The Brass Warden is the god of walls, oaths and protection: a towering figure in brass armor with a face nobody has ever seen. The Warden doesn't care whether you are good. The Warden cares whether you keep your word.

**Calling.** Keep a promise that costs you dearly, or hold a doorway alone against a crowd. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"I will hold the line. I will keep my word. The law is the wall."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Make a promise out loud to someone (and then keep it). |
| **Offering** | Build or repair something at a shrine: a door, a barricade, a weapon, a wall. *Duct Tape* or crafting counts. |
| **Great Deed** | Guard a person or a place through a boss fight: they survive, and you never leave their side. |

**Taboos.** Breaking a promise. Breaking a door, wall or lock you could have opened another way. Attacking a creature that surrendered.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Shoulder to Shoulder.** You have +1 AC while an ally is within 5 feet of you. |
| Devotee | **The Shell.** You learn *Protective Shell*. |
| Zealot | **Oathbound.** Once per long rest, when an ally within 30 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to take the hit instead. You have resistance to its damage. |
| Chosen | **The Wall.** You can't be moved or knocked Prone against your will. Once per floor, you can cast *Stonewall* without mana or concentration (it lasts 10 minutes, then crumbles). |

- **Displeasure.** Your armor feels like lead. You have −1 AC and −10 feet of speed.
- **Wrath.** Every door is against you. Locks you try to open have DC +5, and magic can't open them for you.
- **Atonement.** Keep watch alone through a whole night at a shrine (no rest: you gain 1 Exhaustion level).

**Apostate's Smite: Judgement.** Radiant damage, as a brass gavel the size of a car comes down. *Lasting effect:* glowing brass shackles bind your wrists and ankles: your speed is halved and you can't take the Dash action, and the Warden's bailiffs are sent after you (see `god-stat-blocks`).

## Wobblegob, the Laughing God
**Major god · Tempest, Trickery · Rival: Nyx-Ra · Holy symbol: a red rubber nose**

> *"Honk honk."*

Wobblegob is the god of jokes, chaos and pranks. Nobody knows what Wobblegob looks like, because it looks different every time, and it is usually laughing too hard to hold still. The audience adores it. The other gods do not.

**Calling.** Do something so stupid it works, or make a boss laugh. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"Honk honk."* (That's the whole vow. Wobblegob made the System accept it.)

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Play a prank on an ally (harmless, mostly), or tell a joke that makes someone laugh. |
| **Offering** | Do something ridiculous in a boss room before the fight starts. The GM judges how ridiculous. |
| **Great Deed** | Win a fight using only something absurd: a rubber chicken, a pie, a musical number. |

**Taboos.** Refusing a dare. Attacking someone because they pranked you. Explaining a joke.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Second Take.** Once per long rest, reroll any d20. You must use the new roll. |
| Devotee | **Canned Laughter.** You learn *Laugh Track*. |
| Zealot | **Wild Card.** When you roll a 1 on a d20, roll on the Gag table below. The gag happens to the creature you were targeting, or near you if there wasn't one. |
| Chosen | **Punchline.** Once per floor, when a creature scores a critical hit against you, it misses instead and takes the damage itself. |

| d6 | Gag |
|----|-----|
| 1 | A sneeze of confetti: creatures within 5 feet are Blinded until the end of their next turn. |
| 2 | A cream pie hits the target in the face: it is Blinded until the end of its next turn. |
| 3 | Everyone within 30 feet hears the funniest thing ever: Wisdom save DC 15 or fall Prone laughing. |
| 4 | The target's weapon turns into a rubber chicken for 1 minute (it deals 1 damage). |
| 5 | The target teleports 30 feet in a random direction. |
| 6 | Balloons! You regain 2d8 hit points. |

- **Displeasure.** Wobblegob pranks you. Once a day, at the worst possible moment, the GM rolls on the Gag table and it happens to you.
- **Wrath.** Comedy timing. Whenever you roll a 20 on a d20, it counts as a 1.
- **Atonement.** Let your whole party prank you, one after another, and laugh at every one.

**Apostate's Smite: The Punchline.** Bludgeoning damage, from a mallet the size of a building. *Lasting effect:* everything you say comes out as clown honks. You can't cast spells with verbal components, and you have disadvantage on Charisma checks that need words.

## Hearthmother Tuul
**Major god · Life, Peace · Rival: Saint Payola · Holy symbol: a ladle over a hearth**

> *"Sit down. Eat something. You look terrible, dear."*

Hearthmother Tuul is the god of home, food, healing and hospitality. Safe rooms are her favorite places in the whole dungeon, and some say she is the reason they exist. She is kind, she is warm, and she has a long memory for people who leave others to die alone.

**Calling.** Share your last food with a stranger, or stay with a dying crawler you don't know. (see *Meeting a God* in `gods`)

**Vow.** *"Nobody eats alone. Nobody dies alone. Come home."*

| Devotion | What you do |
|----------|-------------|
| **Rite** | Share a meal with at least one other creature. |
| **Offering** | Give away something useful, for free, to someone who needs it: worth at least 25 gp × the floor number. |
| **Great Deed** | Save a crawler outside your party from death, at real risk to yourself. |

**Taboos.** Letting an ally die alone (with no one at their side). Turning someone away from a safe room. Selling food or healing for profit.

| Rank | Boon |
|------|------|
| Initiate | **Comfort Food.** Food and potions you give to others heal 2 extra hit points, and a meal you share counts as a Cooking meal (see `crawler-skills`). |
| Devotee | **Hearth Rest.** Once per floor, a short rest you lead outside a safe room needs no Ambush Check. |
| Zealot | **Everyone In.** You learn *Group Hug*. |
| Chosen | **Come Home.** Once per floor, as an action, you and up to eight willing creatures you can see teleport to the last safe room you rested in (as *Hearthstone*). |

- **Displeasure.** Nothing tastes right. Meals give you no benefit, and healing from other creatures heals you only half.
- **Wrath.** So lonely. You can't benefit from the Help action, or from healing spells cast by other creatures.
- **Atonement.** Cook a feast in a safe room for at least five creatures you don't know.

**Apostate's Smite: Empty Table.** Cold damage, as all the warmth goes out of you. *Lasting effect:* you can't regain hit points from potions, food or other creatures' spells. Only *Heal Self*, Hit Dice and long rests heal you.
